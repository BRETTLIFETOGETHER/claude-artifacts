COACHING SESSION TITLES — AS FILMED

Twenty-four sessions. Every title below is what was actually said on camera, with the verbatim quote so it can be checked against the transcript. Nothing proposed, nothing invented.

Three levels of coverage

  • TITLE — Brett names it as a title on camera. "It's called…" / "The title of this one is…" / "The topic of this one is…"
  • TOPIC — Brett names the subject but does not frame it as a title.
  • NOT NAMED — no session-level naming spoken at all.

CLARITY

Series named on camera as family clarity, organized around the five areas of family legacy. No session in this title was given a spoken name.

Session 1 — NOT NAMED

Content is the overview of the five areas.

"We're very excited to kick off a brand-new series on family clarity, what it takes to help clients and help your families define what matters most… the five areas of family legacy and the dream of helping families really focus on what matters most."

Tom then names all five on camera: personal legacy (including spiritual), family legacy, financial legacy, business legacy, charitable legacy.

Session 2 — TOPIC

Clarifying the work in your personal life

"In this particular session, we're going to talk about clarifying the work in your personal life. Okay? It's your individual, the categories of understanding your personal values, your purpose, and your priorities as an individual."

Subtitle language available from the same sentence: your personal values, your purpose, and your priorities as an individual.

Session 3 — TOPIC

Clarifying the family

"This particular session that we're covering on clarifying the family. Defining really what the family stands for, to have that clear in your mind before one begins to move forward."

Subtitle language: defining what the family stands for.

Session 4 — TOPIC

Clarifying the financial reality

"The second one is clarifying the financial reality, just getting a clean look at where you are. And the goal is to help your clients understand what they have and what are the assets there."

Subtitle language: getting a clean look at where you are.

Note: Brett calls this "the second one" on camera, though it is Session 4. The numbering slips live.

Session 5 — NOT NAMED

Content is the business legacy.

"So Tom, give a quick overview of the four areas and then bring home why business, and this particular one, is so important to you."

Tom names it as your business legacy when he lists the five areas. Brett says "four areas" where he means five.

Session 6 — TOPIC

Clarifying giving and impact

"We're talking about clarifying giving and impact in your family and the clients that you're serving, defining vision for generosity and the kingdom impact with those families."

Subtitle language: defining vision for generosity and kingdom impact.

WHAT THIS SURFACES

Next Generation is ready. Six spoken titles, six spoken subtitles, all consistent with each other and with the series name. Nothing needs deciding.

Clarity has no titles at all. Four sessions carry a "clarifying the…" phrase that functions as a title, and two have nothing. Sessions 1 and 5 need naming from scratch. This is also the title with the strongest source and the front-door position, so it is the most consequential gap.

Alignment names the acrostic once, in Session 3. A viewer starting anywhere else never learns that FAMILY is an acrostic. If these ship as standalone modules, five of six sessions are missing the organizing idea.

Meetings confirms the three-way conflict on Session 3. Spoken singular, script header plural, script contents page different again.

Two on-camera numbering errors in Clarity. Session 4 is called "the second one." Session 5 asks Tom for "the four areas" where the framework has five.

The series name is not stable. Across the twenty-four sessions Brett says "Family Legacy Coaching by Design," "Family Legacy by Design," "Family Legacy Coaching," and once "Family Legacy, Coaching by Design." Four variants.
