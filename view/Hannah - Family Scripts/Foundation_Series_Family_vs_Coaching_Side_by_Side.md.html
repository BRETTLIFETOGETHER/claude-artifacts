Structural note. Same six letters both editions, deliberately. The acrostic is the asset and it is already filmed. What differs is the unit of work. Coaching titles name the instrument, family titles ask the question. Coaching side is a collection checklist, family side is a conversation checklist.

The family titles are inverted on purpose: the question leads, the letter sits in the subtitle. That keeps the acrostic intact without opening a family session with the words "Mandatory Documents."

Structural note. The widest divergence in the series. Coaching Session 1 teaches an advisor to stop being the expert, which has no family equivalent, so the family edition opens on the problem instead. That pushes everything down one and the two editions never re-sync. Coaching Sessions 2 and 3 collapse into family Session 1, and family Sessions 2, 3, and 4 have no coaching counterpart at all; they are assembled from Clarity, Meetings, and Next Generation.

This is also the thinnest-sourced title in either edition.

⚠️ DEFECTS FOUND

1. Book 1 coaching subtitle does not match the title sheet. The script says Building a Multi-Generational Legacy That Lasts. The sheet says Guiding Families to Move from Legacy by Default to Legacy by Design.

2. Book 1 coaching contents page reads "Helping Clients Clarifying Values and Priorities." The session header itself is correct with "Clarify." Contents page only.

3. Book 4 coaching title line reads "Alignment in Action Leading Conversations That Matter." Two book titles concatenated. "Alignment in Action" belongs to Book 3 and has to come off.

4. Book 4 coaching Session 5 exists in two versions. Contents page says "Facilitating Family Conversations," session header says "Facilitating Family Dialogue."

5. Book 5 coaching Session 3 exists in three versions. Contents page says "Leading Meaningful Conversations," session header says "Facilitating Meaningful Conversations," and on camera it is "Facilitating Meaningful Conversation."

6. Eighteen coaching sessions have no formal title. All of Books 2, 3, and 6. They were filmed without a titled script, so the only naming that exists is descriptive language spoken on camera. Everything marked ⬜ above is proposed and needs approval before it can go to layout, packaging, or the certification pathway.

★ RECOMMENDED CHANGES TO THE TITLE SHEET

★1 — Alignment subtitles, both editions. Currently promise values, direction, and shared action. The six filmed sessions are financial statements, assets under management, mandatory documents, insurance, liabilities and taxes, and cash flow. Recommend surfacing the acrostic:

  • Family: Aligning Your Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most
  • Coaching: Helping Families Align Their Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most

★2 — Family Communication subtitle uses an em-dash, against the series editorial rule. Recommend Why Families Don't Talk, and How to Start.

★3 — Family Book 6 title. "Raising the Next Generation" contradicts the content. The title teaches that adult children is an oxymoron and pushes parents to stop treating grown children as children. Brett coins "growing adults" on camera in Session 1. Recommend Preparing Your Next Generation, which also mirrors the you/they pivot the Clarity pair already does well.
