Teleprompter scripts. Brett Eastman and Tom Conway, two-chair format. Six sessions, one per letter of FAMILY.

Curriculum title: Getting on the Same Page as a Family Curriculum subtitle: Aligning Your Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most

The lighter cut. For advisors, FAMILY is a collection checklist: gather it, review it, find the gaps, produce the report. For families it is a conversation checklist: do we both know, do the kids know, and what happens if. Same six letters. Different unit of work.

What came out. Form numbers. Irrevocable life insurance trust mechanics. Cost basis and step-up. Discounts for non-controlling interests. Estate tax percentages. Anything that requires a professional to act on. What stayed. Every relational consequence.

Where advisors get named. Each session ends by identifying the moment a family should bring in help. Honest, useful to the family, and it feeds the coaching side without turning the title into a sales pitch.
