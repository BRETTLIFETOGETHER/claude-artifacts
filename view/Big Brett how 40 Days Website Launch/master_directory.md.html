HOW THE TWO BODIES OF WORK RELATE

These are two related but currently separate libraries, and it's worth being precise about that rather than treating them as one system:

  • The Seven Day Experience catalog (project files, Part 1) is a fully-authored curriculum library — every campaign has a stated weekly goal, a six-day arc with NIV Scripture, and Day 1 (sometimes the full six days) written out across adult/youth/family editions, with TNT video scripts and Celebration Sunday plans. It's built for the Seven Day / 40-Day campaign format specifically, sold through the Lifetogether platform to churches.
  • The Campaign Titles Directory (this conversation, Part 2) is titles and subtitles only — no devotional days, no scripture selections, no video scripts yet written. It was built as a broader taxonomy, seemingly for the larger 40daycampaigns.com-style vision of a much bigger campaign library.

Nothing here has been de-duplicated or merged between the two. Several themes appear in both bodies of work under different names — e.g., the Seven Day Experience already has full campaigns for Legacy, Belonging, Identity, and Forgiveness, while the Titles Directory has its own Legacy, Belonging, and Grief-adjacent categories built from scratch. Before any of the 1,925 new titles get turned into full campaigns, a cross-reference pass against the existing 58+ Seven Day Experience campaigns would catch overlap and let you decide which titles should become genuinely new campaigns versus which should be retired in favor of what's already fully written.
