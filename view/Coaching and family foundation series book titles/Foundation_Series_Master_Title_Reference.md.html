The thirty versus forty day question. Every devotional is built at thirty days, five readings a week across six weeks, one week per session. You asked for forty. Forty is the format you built your reputation on and the number a church audience recognizes on sight, so this is worth a real decision rather than a correction. Going to forty means ten additional readings per book, sixty across the series, and it breaks the clean one-week-per-session mapping unless the extra ten become an opening week and a closing week, or the weeks go to seven readings and lose the open weekends. Thirty holds the session mapping and the weekday rhythm. Forty holds the campaign equity and gives the family edition a shape churches already know how to run. Recommend deciding this before the family editions go to layout, because it changes the contents architecture of twelve books rather than the copy on any one page.
