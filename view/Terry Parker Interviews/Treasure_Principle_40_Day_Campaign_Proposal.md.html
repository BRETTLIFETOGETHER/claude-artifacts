The Treasure Principle: 40 Days

A Churchwide Campaign Proposal

Prepared for Randy Alcorn and Eternal Perspective Ministries Lifetogether · Brett Eastman Status: proposal. Nothing here is licensed or approved.

What this is, and what it is not

EPM already has two excellent small group products for this book. The twelve-week Bible study with Brian Smith goes deep for groups that want depth. The four-session video curriculum, at roughly fifteen minutes a session, works for groups that want a short run.

Neither one is a churchwide campaign, and that is a different animal. A campaign is what happens when a pastor commits six weekends of the pulpit, every adult in the congregation reads the same thing on the same morning, groups form around it, and the whole church moves together for forty days. It is the format that took The Purpose Driven Life from a book to a movement, and it is the format Lifetogether built with EPM once already on the Heaven Church Kit.

This proposal does not replace either existing product. The twelve-week study becomes the natural next step for groups that finish the forty days and want more. The four-session video remains the short-run option. This sits above both.

The proposed structure: six sermons, six group sessions, forty daily readings, one launch weekend, one commitment weekend.

Architecture

The six sessions map one to one onto the six Treasure Principle Keys, in Randy's published order. Each session anchors roughly a week of daily readings.

Session Key Days Campaign title
1 God's ownership and our role as managers 1 to 7 Whose Is It
2 The heart follows where the money goes 8 to 14 Follow the Money
3 Heaven, the New Earth, as our true home 15 to 21 Where You're From
4 Living for the line rather than the dot 22 to 28 The Dot and the Line
5 Giving as the antidote to materialism 29 to 34 The Only Cure
6 Increase given for the sake of giving 35 to 40 The Other Standard

A note on wording. The key descriptions above are paraphrased on purpose. Randy's exact phrasing of the six keys is his and is far better than any restatement, and it should appear verbatim throughout the finished materials under license. Nothing in this proposal should be read as a rewrite of his language.

The forty days

Each reading follows one structure: an opening hook, a story or testimony, the biblical foundation, a transferable principle, practical application, one action step, a next-step challenge, and three reflection questions. Eight hundred to a thousand words. Scripture is NIV throughout unless EPM prefers otherwise.

The sermon series

Six weekends, one key each, titles matching the sessions. Full outlines, scripture, illustrations drawn from Randy's material under license, and a one-page teaching brief per week so a pastor can preach it in his own voice rather than reading someone else's manuscript.

Weekend one launches the campaign, distributes the book and the devotional, and forms the groups. Weekend six is the commitment weekend, built around the giving covenant.

The group companion

Six sessions, ninety minutes each, designed for a group that has been reading the daily material all week. Opening question, a video teaching segment, discussion built on that week's readings rather than on new content, one practice, and a closing. Written so a first-time host can lead it.

The existing twelve-week study is positioned at the end as the deeper next step, which keeps the EPM catalog intact and gives groups somewhere to go.

The kit

Following the pattern of the Heaven Church Kit: video sessions, the group discussion guide, sermon outlines for pastors, PowerPoint and message graphics, bulletin inserts and banners, an outreach event guide, budget worksheets, group sign-up forms, host training, and a quick start guide for the campaign director.

Delivered digitally, with the assembly customized per church: denomination-appropriate language, translation, service format, campaign length, and local branding. That customization is the reason to build this on a platform rather than in a box.

Video, and the forty-voice option

Two paths.

The simple path. Randy teaches all six sessions. Cleanest, fastest, and it is what most churches would expect.

The wider path. Randy teaches the six, and forty short daily video devotionals come from forty voices across the generosity world, one per day. This turns a book campaign into a movement moment and gives every contributing organization a reason to promote it.

The honest tradeoff: forty contributors means forty schedules, forty releases, and forty levels of production quality. Ten contributors at four days each delivers nearly the same experience for a tenth of the coordination, and I would start there.

What EPM would need to approve

Use of the six keys verbatim throughout. Quotation from the book inside the devotional and group guide, within agreed limits. Randy on camera for six sessions. Use of the giving covenant as the campaign's commitment instrument. Series naming and cover treatment. And the shape of the relationship on the campaign format itself, which is the one item worth settling before anything is built rather than after.

Three notes for the conversation

Do not lead with money. Randy and Nanci made a deliberate decision years ago to forgo royalties on his books. Anyone who opens this conversation with revenue projections has misread the man in the first sentence. Lead with reach: how many congregations would run this, and what happens to a church that spends forty days on it together.

Lead with the precedent. The Heaven Church Kit already exists with both names on it. This is not a new idea being pitched. It is the same collaboration applied to the book of his that is most suited to a churchwide campaign and currently least equipped for one.

Name the gap plainly. The Treasure Principle has sold in the millions and there is no way for a pastor to run it across an entire congregation. That is the whole proposal in one sentence.
