Stage 8: Build Continuity Report

Internal document. Not part of the devotional.

Stages 6 and 7

Not applicable. The standing instructions carry a Participant Guide stage list, and the devotional master prompt specifies front matter and thirty readings only, with no back matter and no scenario responses. Both exist in the Communication Participant Guide already. Nothing was invented to fill either stage.

Title status, all sixty phrases

Every title and every subtitle is Tom's own words from the Communication scripts. None is Tom quoting a third party. None was replaced by a scripture reference for want of a source. No gap was flagged, because none occurred.

Light compression was applied to eleven of the sixty. In each case words were removed and none added. Examples: "shifting from being the one who provides answers to being the one who guides the process" compressed to "From Providing Answers to Guiding the Process"; "most of them are not technical, they're relational and emotional" compressed to "Not Technical, They're Relational and Emotional"; "without structure, conversations can drift or become dominated by one or two voices" compressed to "Without Structure, Conversations Drift".

Distribution: five titles and five subtitles per session, drawn evenly. No session was thin.

Phrases excluded as Brett's

Seven strong lines were rejected because they are Brett's rather than Tom's, in keeping with the master prompt. The most significant is "the quality of the conversation often determines the quality of the outcome," which is the best single sentence in the Communication corpus and is not available in a devotional written in Tom's voice. Also excluded: "you're creating space instead of filling it," "the breakthrough didn't come from a better plan, it came from a better conversation," "the issue wasn't disagreement, it was lack of clarity," "people are operating with different versions of the same story," "structure actually creates freedom, not restriction," and "this is about moving from a moment to a pattern."

Story reuse against the curriculum

Six stories exist in the Communication scripts, one per session. All six are already the Opening Story of the corresponding Participant Guide session, so every use here is an overlap. Listed for the client to decide.

Day 3 uses the Session 1 family who met with advisors before. Day 9 uses the Session 2 family whose parents never communicated their vision. Day 14 uses the Session 3 family whose previous conversations turned into silence or disagreement. Day 19 uses the Session 4 family and the distribution question. Day 24 uses the Session 5 family asked what they hope for this family moving forward. Day 27 uses the Session 6 two families with similar starting points.

Difference in treatment: the Participant Guide re-angled three of the six to avoid the stories resolving on the same beat. This devotional tells all six from Tom's own angle as he tells them, which is what the master prompt requires. An advisor reading both meets each story twice, several weeks apart, told slightly differently. That is repetition rather than contradiction, and it is the client's call.

No story is used twice within the devotional. No scripture is used twice within the devotional.
