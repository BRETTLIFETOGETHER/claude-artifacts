3. WHY THIS MATTERS

Eyebrow: Why This Matters

Headline: Families Do Not Divide Over Documents.

Body: Most advisors were trained on one side of legacy planning. The structures, the instruments, the tax treatment, the transfer. That work matters and someone has to do it well. But the families who come apart rarely come apart over a poorly drafted document. They come apart because nobody ever said out loud what the money was for, because the next generation learned the numbers from an attorney's office instead of from their parents, and because the most important conversations kept getting postponed until they could not be had at all.

Technical expertise is table stakes now. What sets an advisor apart is the ability to sit in a family's living room and lead the conversation that nobody else in that family's life is willing to start.

Three points, no headers above them:

Clarity comes before strategy. A family that cannot say what matters most cannot be planned for, no matter how good the plan.

Preparation matters more than distribution. The question is not only what will be passed down, but who the next generation is becoming before they receive it.

Communication is the work. Wealth transfers on its own. Values, wisdom, and unity do not.
