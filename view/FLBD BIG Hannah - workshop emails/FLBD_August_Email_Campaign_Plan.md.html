THE FRAMEWORK

The 5 conversations families avoid

When a family's plan unravels, it's rarely because the documents were wrong. It's because five conversations never happened — or happened badly.

The whole framework is organized around them:

  1. Clarity — what actually matters to this family, named out loud.
  2. Alignment — getting everyone on the same page before decisions land.
  3. Communication — the conversations that build trust instead of eroding it.
  4. Meetings — a rhythm that keeps the family moving, not drifting.
  5. Next Generation — preparing heirs for what they'll actually inherit.

Leading any of these doesn't require the perfect answer. It requires a better way to ask the next question and create space for the family to engage. That's a skill — and it's teachable.

The workshop walks through all five, with a structure advisors can run with a real client the week they get home.
