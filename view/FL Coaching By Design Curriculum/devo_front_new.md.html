A WELCOME FROM TOM AND BRETT

This book is short on purpose.

The curriculum it accompanies asks a great deal of you. Six sessions, real exercises, conversations with clients you have never had before. This is the other half of that, and it works differently. A few minutes a day, five days a week, for six weeks.

We built it because of something we have both watched happen. An advisor goes through good training, agrees with all of it, and returns to a full calendar where nothing changes. The material was not the problem. What was missing was anything that touched the work on a Tuesday.

So each reading here ends with one question to take into an actual client conversation that week. Thirty days produces thirty of those. By the end you will have asked things you have not asked in a decade of meetings, and you will have a written record of what came back.

There is also a section at the end of each day called The Bucket. It is private, nobody sees it, and it turns the day on you rather than on the people you serve. We put it there deliberately. This work does not hold for advisors who apply it only to clients, and families can tell the difference between someone recommending a conversation and someone who has had one.

Read it in the morning if you can. Write the answers rather than think them.

Tom Conway Brett Eastman
