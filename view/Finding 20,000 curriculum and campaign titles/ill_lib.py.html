TECHNIQUES = [
 ["The analogy ladder","Take the abstract idea and step down one rung at a time until it touches something physical. Grace becomes forgiveness becomes a debt cancelled becomes a torn-up invoice on a kitchen table."],
 ["The reversal","Illustrate what the thing is not, then turn. Show a room the counterfeit and the real thing becomes visible without being described."],
 ["The zoom","Start impossibly wide or impossibly close, then move. A galaxy, then a single cell. A hospital, then one hand on a rail."],
 ["The unexpected pairing","Two things nobody puts together. Grief and a supermarket. Holiness and a woodworking tolerance. The surprise is what makes it memorable."],
 ["The withheld ending","Tell most of it, make the point, then finish the story. The room stays with you through the teaching to get the ending."],
 ["The ordinary object","Hold something up. A key, a receipt, a photograph, a stone. Physical objects survive in memory long after words."],
 ["The overheard","Something someone actually said, quoted exactly. Real speech has a texture nobody can write."],
 ["The number that lands","One statistic, made human. Not forty per cent — four people in this row."],
 ["The recurring image","One picture introduced in week one and returned to in every week of a series. By week six the image carries the whole argument."],
 ["The local","Something in this town that everybody in the room has driven past. Nothing beats it and no library contains it."],
]
