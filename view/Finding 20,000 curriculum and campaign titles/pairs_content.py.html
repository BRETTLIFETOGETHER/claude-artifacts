"intro":"One forty-minute shoot produces fifteen interviews and roughly forty-five answers. Most churches use ninety seconds of that and delete the rest. The transcript is what turns a single morning of filming into six weeks of video, and it lets a volunteer do the work without ever watching the footage.",
"key":"Sort the transcript by question, not by person. That one move is the entire method.",
"steps":[
("One &mdash; Transcribe it the free way","Every phone will now transcribe video automatically, and so will every editing app. Export the transcript with timecodes. This step costs nothing and takes about four minutes."),
("Two &mdash; Re-sort by question","Cut and paste so that all fifteen answers to question one sit together, then all fifteen to question two. You are no longer looking at fifteen interviews. You are looking at three collections of fifteen answers."),
("Three &mdash; Read, do not watch","Whoever is choosing clips reads the transcript and highlights eight to ten lines per question. They never open the video. This is why a volunteer can do it in half an hour instead of a staff member losing an afternoon."),
("Four &mdash; Hand over a timecode list","The chooser gives the editor a plain list: 04:12&ndash;04:24, 11:03&ndash;11:19, and so on. The editor pulls them in order and does nothing else. Two people, ninety minutes total, no meetings."),
("Five &mdash; One question becomes one week","Question one is week one's clip. Question two is week two. Question three is week three. Same faces every week, different answers, which builds recognition across the run &mdash; the congregation starts looking for the man in the blue jacket."),
("Six &mdash; Or sort by theme instead","For a challenge rather than a series, ignore the questions and sort the highlighted lines by what they are actually about. Doubt in one pile, family in another, money in a third. Now the clips match your teaching weeks rather than your shoot order."),
],
"yield":[
("One 40-minute shoot","15 interviews, 3 questions each, roughly 45 usable answers"),
("Sorted by question","3 weekly clips of 8-10 answers each"),
("Sorted by theme","5 to 6 clips, matched to whatever you are teaching"),
("Light plus heartfelt","2 distinct videos from the same footage &mdash; one opens a campaign, one closes it"),
("Total staff time","About two hours, and most of it is not the staff"),
],
"rule":"Film once per campaign, not once per weekend. The single biggest reason these stop after two weeks is that somebody decided they needed fresh footage every Saturday. They do not.",
