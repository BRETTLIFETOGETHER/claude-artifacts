THE DECISION I'M READING

Visionary Generosity is the book. Exponential, Radical, and Revolutionary stop being candidate titles and become material inside it — or, where they don't fit, they get released.

That resolves the question I've raised five times and never had ruled on. If you meant something narrower by those two words, say so and I'll redirect the architecture in one pass; nothing below is expensive to undo.

What follows: where each abandoned title's material lands, the revised chapter spine, what gets cut and why, the IP hierarchy, an index of everything built, and what's actually next.

WHY THIS TITLE WON

Revolutionary is The Signatry's mission language. That makes it a claim the manuscript has to earn and permanently ties the book to one organization — a house book by construction.

Radical is twice-taken, and post-Platt the word makes a business owner brace before page one. Its content — the root work on ownership, fear, and appetite — was always the best of the four ideas. It survives as chapters 1 through 3.

Exponential was the strongest runner-up and the only open title, but it promises arithmetic and the book is finally about sight and willingness. Its seven multipliers were never a competing thesis; they were the mechanism half of this one. They fold in below without loss.

Visionary carries the tension. Vision and obedience need each other and fail separately, which gives the book somewhere to go for three hundred pages. Every other candidate asserted something. This one argues something.

WHAT GETS CUT, AND WHY

The four engines from Exponential Part III — family, business, church, fund. The family engine survives as chapter 17 and the business engine folds into 15. The church engine leaves the book entirely and becomes the workshop's church edition; a chapter aimed at pastors inside a book aimed at families serves neither. The fund engine folds into 16, and its vehicle mechanics go to the appendix — a chapter about fund design, written by a fund sponsor, is the one place the book could not survive the suspicion.

"Caught, Not Taught" as a standalone chapter. The contagion argument is true and it is one beat, not four thousand words. It lives inside 14.

The Radical four-part interior architecture. Root, appetite, grip, ego — excellent material, wrong container. Distributed through chapters 1–4 where it does structural work rather than sitting as a separate diagnosis section that delays the argument.

The word revolutionary itself. Not from The Signatry's mission, which is theirs. From this manuscript, where it would read as borrowed authority.
