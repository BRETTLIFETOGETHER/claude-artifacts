PART THREE — OBSERVATIONS AGAINST OUR CURRENT WORK

(observations, not directives — your call on each)

  1. Coaching book one-liners now exist. The live site supplies real descriptions for Clarity through Next Generation — these can replace the series-line placeholders on the prototype's Foundation covers, and the Issuu preview links can be wired in.
  2. Book One name variance: live site says "Family Legacy Coaching by Design"; the manuscript sample says "Legacy Coaching by Design." One should win before print.
  3. Two products, one name risk: the 40-Day Journey (single campaign-style product) and the six-book Foundation Series both live under "Foundation" on the site; the 40-day's movement titles (What Will Your Legacy Be? etc.) differ from the family series book labels in the manuscript samples. Worth a naming map so shop, site, and print agree.
  4. Statistics: the site runs 70% / 97% unattributed. Your standing print policy is attribute-or-exclude (and the 90-vs-97 question is still open in the manuscripts). The web copy and the books currently disagree.
  5. Pricing registers: $1,995 and $495 on the same workshop page repeats the known two-register pricing conflict.
  6. Live workshop is in two days with March early-bird copy still up — smallest fix with the largest optics.
  7. Contact is unreachable — placeholder email/phone sitewide including the Contact page itself; the only working funnel is Shopify.
  8. No CAN branding anywhere on the live site — FLBD throughout, consistent with the prototype rule.
  9. Brand consistency confirmations for the prototype: Five Areas ✓ · "A Complete Library for Designing Generational Legacy" (identical headline) ✓ · default→design language ✓ · Families/Advisors two-door pathing ✓ · endorser set overlaps the prototype's About page ✓.
