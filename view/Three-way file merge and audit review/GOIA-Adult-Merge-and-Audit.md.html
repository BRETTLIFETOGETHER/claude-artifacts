2. Decision list

Twenty items need a call from you. Page numbers are the printed PDF. Recommendations are mine; the reasoning line says why.

Take Allen's wording (port into the current file)

p6 — Ron's welcome letter. Both files are wrong, differently. Allen reads carry, : and the current file reads carry :. Someone removed the comma and left the space.

Fix to: It answers the question so many of us carry: "Will I be okay?"

p109 — Small Group Agreement, Encouragement for Growth. The current file reads "to help each other make one faithful next step." You take a step; you don't make one. Allen has "take." The same line is also missing a comma.

Fix to: To give encouragement, not just receive it. We want to grow, and to help each other take one faithful next step.

p125 — Leading for the First Time. Two problems in one bullet. A double period, and a padded clause.

Current: You are not the teacher, and you don't have to be a financial expert.. The video carries the teaching, this guide carries the questions, and a host who admits they haven't figured out everything about money either will earn more trust... Fix to: You are not the teacher, and you don't have to be a financial expert. The video carries the teaching, this guide carries the questions, and a host who admits they haven't figured money out either will earn more trust...

p128 — Leadership Training 101, first bullet. Missing space, plus a sentence fragment that the current file introduced.

Current: God is with you, and so is your church. And hopefully a co-leader as well.You're not carrying this by yourself... Fix to: God is with you, and so is your church, and hopefully a co-leader as well. You're not carrying this by yourself...

p128 — Leadership Training 101, "Curriculum drives the group." The current file uses "curriculum" twice in adjacent sentences. Allen's "each session" avoids the echo.

Fix to: Curriculum drives the group, not you. You don't have to carry the session. Trust the flow of each session, the questions, the exercise, the story.

p60 — Session 4 introduction. The strongest editorial argument in the set, and the base file backs Allen. The current rewrite flattens three lines. "Whisper in the dark" became "whisper just to themselves." "Gives it two" became "gives two answers." Most costly, the closing antithesis collapsed: Allen's confidence anchored in the Provider holds when the provision doesn't turns on itself; the current holds when the provision feels fragile repeats "fragile" from the previous sentence and loses the turn.

Fix to: It's the question people whisper in the dark, and Ron says it's the one he hears more than any other. Will I have enough? Will I outlive my money? Will my family be all right? It's a legitimate question, and it deserves a real answer rather than a spiritual dismissal. This week gives it two. The first is practical: there are habits that genuinely build financial strength over a lifetime, and they work. The second is deeper: confidence that depends on a number will always be fragile, because the number can move. Confidence anchored in the Provider holds when the provision doesn't.

6. Independent audit of the current file

Ship-blocking

PDF p4 — Contents page swaps Sessions 2 and 3. It reads "SESSION 2 / THE STEWARDSHIP QUESTION / How Much Is Enough?" and "SESSION 3 / THE CONTENTMENT QUESTION / How Do We Learn Faithfulness Through Stewardship?" Titles are swapped; subtitles stayed put.

This is an InDesign error, not a manuscript error. The DOCX Contents page is correct, and so are the body dividers (p27 Contentment, p43 Stewardship), the Small Group Calendar, and the Memory Verse Cards. Only the laid-out Contents page is wrong. It goes to the designer, not to Hannah — and it inverts your canonical order on the first content page a reader opens.

Live placeholders in the source. [final bio and credits to be confirmed] at the end of About the Authors, and [Organizational description to be confirmed: founding and history, mission statement, key programs and audiences served, and website and contact information.] at p121. The first was dropped in layout; the second is printed in the PDF.

35 [QR] placeholders with nothing behind them. Every devotional day entry in the source ends with a [QR] marker. The PDF's devotional pages (25, 26, 41, 42, 57, 58, 73, 74, 89, 90, 105, 106) contain zero images. The only small square images in the file are two on p120, which are the author photos. Either the codes were never generated or the placement step never happened. Thirty-five is the full set, so this is a whole feature, not a miss.

Forty vs. forty-two. p122 closes with "The forty days end." The Small Group Calendar on p112 assigns Days 36–42 to Session 6, and the devotional runs 42 entries. The July 23 base read "forty-two"; the rewrite changed it and both current files inherited the change. Same 40/42 collision you have open on the student devotional, now live in the adult print file.

Ron's career length. p6 says forty-five years, p120 says over four decades. Covered in §2.

"Tender" vs. "sensitive." p9 and p109 describe money in the same construction with different adjectives. Covered in §2.

The dignity promise. p14 and p109 now carry different versions of the same sentence. Covered in §2.

Layout divergences from the source

These three exist in the PDF but not the DOCX, which means the InDesign file was built from a slightly earlier export than the version you just sent:

  • p120 reads "honest conversation around the table" (singular); the current DOCX reads "conversations."
  • Appendix order. The DOCX runs Weekly Check-In → Memory Verse Cards → Prayer and Praise Report.   • Session divider numerals. Sessions 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 use Roman numerals (I, III, IV, V, VI). Session 2 uses a plain Arabic 2. Verified on the rendered pages.

NIV reads "to be rich in good deeds, and to be generous and willing to share." The trim makes it standalone, which is defensible, but it is a silent alteration and should be a documented choice.
