What changed and why

The first edition was written from the six full session recordings, before the clips existed, with day boundaries I chose. This edition is built against the thirty video clips, one per day, so that a QR code on the page returns the material the reading came out of.

Every title and every subtitle is now verbatim from its own clip, not from the session at large. That was verified phrase by phrase before a word of copy was written. Fifty-eight of fifty-eight passed.

Session 5 was also one short. You flagged Session 4. Session 5 had five files but S5_3 and S5_4 are the same take at eighty-six percent word for word, leaving four distinct clips.
