How to Use This

Five readings a week, Monday through Friday, for six weeks.

Each week goes with one session of the Alignment module. Week One goes with Session 1, Week Two with Session 2, and so on through the six letters of the F.A.M.I.L.Y. Review. You can read the week before you watch the session, after you watch it, or in the same week. I would do it in the same week, because the material lands harder when you are already working it.

Weekends are open on purpose. If you miss a Tuesday, you have Saturday. I built the gap in so that missing a day does not put you behind, because in my experience a man who falls two days behind in a thirty day book quits on day nine.

There is a video with each day

Every reading has a code on the page. Scan it and you will get the short piece of the conversation that reading came out of, usually two or three minutes. Watch it first, then read.

That order matters more than it sounds like it should. What you are reading is not a summary of the video. It is the same material slowed down and taken further than a conversation can take it, and it works best when you have just heard me say the thing in my own voice.

If you cannot get to the video, the reading still stands on its own.

Each reading takes a few minutes. Six or seven, if you are actually reading and not skimming.

Write the answers down. Do not think them.

I mean that, and it is the only instruction in this book I would call a requirement. Thinking an answer feels like answering. It is not. Something happens between your head and your hand that does not happen otherwise, which is that you find out whether you actually had an answer or just the feeling of one. Every reading has lines in it. Use them.

There are five parts after the reading itself.

Reflect is four questions on what you just read. These are for you, not for a client.

Practice is a written exercise. Most of them come out of tools I actually use with families, so what you are doing in the margin of this book is what I would be doing on a legal pad in a client's conference room.

One Question to Carry is a single question to take to a client this week. One. Not four, not a list. If you ask one real question a week for six weeks you will have asked six more than most advisors ask in a year.

The Bucket is private. It turns the day back on you rather than on the people you serve. I put it in because I have watched a great many advisors get very good at asking families questions they have never once asked themselves, and it shows. It shows to the family, eventually.

Prayer is a direction, not a script. I did not write the prayers out. You know how to pray and you do not need me to do it for you.

One last thing. This is a companion, not a summary. It does not repeat the module and it will not substitute for it. What it does is slow down about thirty of the things I say quickly on video, so that you have to sit with one of them at a time.
