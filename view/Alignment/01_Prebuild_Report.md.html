Pre-Build Report

Alignment: Thirty Days of Reading

Four decisions taken, not waiting on

Title. The prompt arrived with [TITLE] unfilled. Default: Alignment, since the six Alignment transcripts are the uploaded sources and the Alignment Participant Guide is the module this companion attaches to.

Delivery order. The standing instructions list Session 1, Sessions 2 through 6, appendix sections, and eighteen Scenario Responses. Those belong to the Participant Guide prompt. A devotional has none of them. Mapped as follows: Day 1 stands in for Session 1, Weeks 1 through 6 for Sessions 2 through 6, and the devotional has no appendix and no scenario responses, so those two stages are reported as not applicable rather than invented.

QA script. Scenario counts, self-check counts, and solo fork frequency do not exist in this format. The QA has been rebuilt against the devotional's actual structure: banned characters, nine sections per entry, title sourcing, scripture repetition, story repetition, word counts, and prayer format.

Voice samples. The master prompt offers three sample Tom lines. One is real and traceable: the donor-advised fund line comes from the Workshop Transcripts, where Tom says money moved into a fund has not done God's work any good yet, it is sitting there. The other two, that is a disaster about to happen and that is a poor use of your life, appear nowhere in the Alignment transcripts, the Workshop Transcripts, the Training Manual, the Coaching Book, the Family Book, or the Workbook Legend. They are presumably from another title's videos, which are not in this project. They have not been used.

The attribution problem, stated up front

The six Alignment VTT files carry no speaker labels. Every attribution below is inferred from context, sentence rhythm, and the interview structure, in which Brett asks and frames while Tom answers with specifics, numbers, and client cases.

This matters more here than it did for the curriculum. The curriculum is jointly authored and both voices belong in it. The devotional is Tom alone, and the prompt is explicit that Brett's phrases are not Tom's. Where a phrase sits in a contested stretch, it has been either excluded or flagged.

One phrase was caught by this check and corrected. The transcript reads: He built it by design, not by default. He did do it by design. That's great. We helped him to build it by design. The first sentence is Brett. Tom's version is the third. Day 20 therefore uses We helped him to build it by design, which is longer and less quotable and is the one Tom actually said.

Every title and every subtitle below was grepped against the flattened transcripts before a word of copy was written. All sixty returned a verbatim hit. No title needed a scripture reference substituted for a missing phrase, and no gap was filled with invented language.

Zero scripture references were substituted for missing phrases.

Material excluded on purpose

The three percent tithe statistic. Tom builds a full exchange with forty advisors on it. Unattributed, so cut rather than softened.

The John Wesley quotation. Tom hedges it on tape, saying he thinks it is John Wesley, and the wording differs from the form usually attributed to Wesley. Cut pending verification.

Dr. David Jeremiah. Named public figure, secondhand relay, specific words attributed to him. Cut pending permission.

The how much is enough story, the man who moved from four or five million a year down to two. Brett tells it, not Tom, and Brett hedges the recipient on tape. Excluded under the Brett rule.

Tom's son. Referenced obliquely at Day 11 as a lawyer in the family without a will, because the story is Tom's own and it is the strongest thing in Session 3. His name is not used and the personal injury settlement figure from Session 4 has been kept qualitative. Both still need permission.

Ron Blue appears twice, at Day 22 and Day 29, quoted by name as Tom quotes him. Needs the same permission check as the curriculum.

Thin session warning

Session 5 gave five usable titles and no more. The five Liabilities and Taxes readings are built on genuinely thin material, and two of them lean on the Family Book and the Training Manual for supporting substance rather than on the video. Flagged in place in each entry. If Week Five reads lighter than the rest, that is the transcript, not the build.
