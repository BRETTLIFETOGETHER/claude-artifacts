Alignment: Thirty Days of Reading

Scriptures Tom quotes directly

Five of the thirty are scriptures Tom quotes or references on tape in the Alignment videos. These need no approval.

Hebrews 11:6 is also quoted directly by Tom and appears inside the Day 30 body rather than as a headed verse, since Matthew 6:21 carries that day.

Scriptures that are recommendations needing Tom's approval

Twenty-five. Fourteen of them are already inside Family Legacy by Design material, which means they are house scripture rather than imported. Eleven are outside it and carry more risk.

Drawn from the Family Book, Coaching Book, or Training Manual, so already in the family of material:

Proverbs 27:23 (Day 1), Ecclesiastes 11:2 (Day 6), Proverbs 21:5 (Day 9), Matthew 7:12 (Day 10), 1 Chronicles 29:11-12 (Day 13), Proverbs 6:6-8 (Day 14), Proverbs 21:20 (Day 15), 1 Timothy 5:8 (Day 16), Matthew 6:32 (Day 18), Philippians 4:19 (Day 20), Proverbs 22:7 (Day 22), 2 Corinthians 9:7 (Day 24), Romans 13:7 (Day 25), Proverbs 13:11 (Day 27).

Not drawn from existing material and needing a closer look:

Proverbs 15:22 (Day 3), Proverbs 20:21 (Day 4), Psalm 133:1 (Day 5), Proverbs 24:3 (Day 7), Proverbs 13:22 (Day 8), Habakkuk 2:2 (Day 11), Proverbs 27:1 (Day 12), Proverbs 14:15 (Day 17), Genesis 41:34-36 (Day 19), Luke 12:2 (Day 21), Luke 14:28 (Day 23).

Two of those need a second look for a specific reason. Luke 12:2 is about the eventual disclosure of hidden things, spoken to disciples about hypocrisy, and is used here for the tax return as the most revealing document. The reading says out loud that Jesus is not talking about tax returns, but it is the longest reach in the set. Proverbs 20:21 is rendered here as an inheritance claimed too soon; translations vary considerably on that verse and the wording should be checked against whichever version becomes the standard for the series.

Translation standard. Still unresolved across the whole project. Proverbs 21:5 is cited TLB, following the Family Book. Everything else reads NIV. This needs to be settled once for the curriculum and the devotional together.

Places a gap was flagged rather than filled

Day 11, key verse. No scripture exists in the Session 3 video and none in the companion books attaches to Mandatory Documents. Habakkuk 2:2 is offered with a bracketed note in place. This is the same hole that is open in the curriculum.

Week Five, source thinness. A bracketed note sits at the head of the week. Session 5 is roughly 12,000 characters against 28,000 for Session 6, and two of the five readings draw supporting substance from the Training Manual and the Family Book rather than from the video.

Day 23, the forty percent figure. Tom uses it on tape. Flagged in place as illustrative, since rates are indexed and vary by asset.

Day 15, estate tax liability. The Practice table carries a bracketed note to use current rates and exemption amounts with an as-of date.

Day 16, estate tax on a company-owned policy. Same flag.

Day 25, the $250,000 value of the missed gift. Tom's figure on tape. Flagged, since the benefit depends on rates and circumstances.

Material excluded rather than used

The three percent tithe statistic. Tom builds a full exchange with forty advisors on it. No attribution, so cut rather than softened. This removes roughly ninety seconds of Session 6.

The John Wesley quotation. Tom hedges it on tape. Wording differs from the usual attribution. Cut pending verification.

Dr. David Jeremiah. Named public figure, secondhand relay, specific words attributed. Cut pending permission. Removes roughly three minutes of Session 6.

The how much is enough story. Brett tells it, not Tom, and hedges the recipient on tape. Excluded under the rule that Brett's phrases are not Tom's.

Tom's son's name and the $5 million settlement figure. The son appears at Day 11 unnamed. The settlement figure is not used at all in the devotional, though it underpins a curriculum scenario.

Ron Blue, quoted twice. Day 22 carries the line about a man who looks like he could give you a million dollars, and Day 29 carries the equal and unique formulation. Both are attributed to him by name because Tom attributes them by name.

Attribution confidence

The six VTT files have no speaker labels. Every phrase used in this book was placed with Tom on the basis of context, and one was corrected during the build when the context showed it belonged to Brett. Before print, the thirty titles and thirty subtitles should be checked against the video by someone who can hear which voice is speaking. That is a two-hour job and it protects the central claim of the product, which is that these are Tom's words.
