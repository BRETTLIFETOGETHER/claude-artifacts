Dropped: S1_5. Its content is the net worth definition, what you own minus what you owe. That is a definition rather than a story, it sits comfortably inside the Day 1 reading, and the clip is the shortest in the session at two minutes flat.

The S1_3 trim, and why it is worth asking for. S1_3 is a supercut. Its first ninety seconds are unique and strong: the gaps framing, the question about preparing your child for the ten million your plan already promises him, and the exchange where Tom tells a man his son will receive a check for two and a half million and the man says that would be horrible. After that the file runs straight into S1_4, S1_5, and S1_6 verbatim.

Cut it at "Well, a lot of times you can deal with the relational issues", which is where S1_4 begins. That yields a clean ninety-second clip and preserves the best moment in Session 1.

If you would rather not involve the editor: drop S1_3 entirely and use S1_1, S1_2, S1_4, S1_5, S1_6. You lose the two and a half million dollar story from the video, though it can still appear in the reading since it is in the main transcript.
