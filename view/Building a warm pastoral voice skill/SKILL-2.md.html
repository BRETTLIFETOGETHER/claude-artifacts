name: lifetogether-campaign-builder description: Build LifeTogether-style churchwide campaign content — 6-week/6-session campaign titles, weekly theme titles, session outlines, devotional themes, and brochure copy. Use this whenever the user is drafting a new campaign, naming weekly themes, writing brochure or catalog copy, or outlining any churchwide curriculum resource for LifeTogether — even if they just say something like "give me six week titles for a series on prayer" or "draft the brochure copy for X" without mentioning "skill" or "campaign" by name.

LifeTogether Campaign Architecture

Fixed Structure (always true)

  • Every campaign = exactly 6 weeks / 6 sessions. Never propose more or fewer weeks unless the user explicitly asks for a different length.
  • Every campaign needs, in this order: one Title, one Subtitle/tagline, then 6 Weekly Themes (one per week).

Title & Weekly Theme Pattern

  • Churchwide flagship campaigns (the main churchwide series — the "big six" style campaigns like "A Life That Matters," "Rooted," "Living Fully Alive"): use a repeating word or phrase device across all 6 weekly themes.
  • Example pattern: "Rooted in Christ / Rooted in Scripture / Rooted in Community / Rooted in Character / Rooted in Mission / Rooted for the Future."
  • This repeating device is a signature move for flagship campaigns — don't drop it.
  • Specialized / niche editions (life-stage editions, audience-specific spinoffs, one-off client campaigns): themes can vary in structure and wording. Don't force the repeating device — prioritize clarity and audience fit instead.

Workflow (always follow this order — stop and confirm at step 3)

  1. Title — 2–4 words, evocative, not a full sentence.
  2. Subtitle — one line naming the promise or the shift.
  3. Six weekly themes — one line each. Present these and STOP — ask the user to confirm or adjust before going further.
  4. Only after confirmation — expand into the full output stack: sermon outlines, devotional days, small-group sessions, ABF notes, family edition, leader guide, brochure copy. Don't jump straight to full output before the naming layer is locked in.

Voice

This skill handles structure. For tone and word choice, pair it with the warm-pastoral-voice skill — its repeating-word device rules, title+subtitle formula, tension→resolve pattern, and word bank. Apply both together whenever drafting anything user-facing.

Output Stack (every full campaign includes)

  • Weekend Experience: 6-sermon series, pastor prep guide, presentation slides, graphics, promotion materials
  • Daily Journey: 30- or 40-day devotional, journal edition, family edition, student edition
  • Groups: 6-session small-group curriculum, leader guide, discussion questions, video option
  • Adult Bible Fellowship: expanded teacher guide, historical notes, maps/charts, master teaching manuscript
  • Churchwide Resources: prayer guide, worship recommendations, campaign calendar, volunteer resources, celebration weekend, follow-up pathway

Positioning Rules (non-negotiable)

  • Founder Brett Eastman served on the original Purpose Driven ministry team and co-developed many of the original small-group resources. Frame everything as building on / extending that legacy.
  • NEVER position a new campaign as "another Purpose Driven campaign" or imply an ongoing/official affiliation with that brand.
  • DO position campaigns as building on the five biblical purposes (worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, mission) as a shared, decades-proven foundation — never as a proprietary claim on that legacy.
  • Safe phrasing: "builds on those timeless biblical foundations," "a new generation of churchwide campaigns," "applying those same biblical foundations."
