Skill 1: curriculum builds following Brett's fixed specs

name: curriculum-builder description: Build session-based curriculum for LifeTogether — especially six-session Legacy by Design Coaching Series titles — following Brett's fixed build spec, source discipline, and editorial rules. Use whenever Brett asks to build, draft, outline, or batch curriculum sessions, a participant guide, a study guide, or a new title in any series, or says things like "build Session 1," "batch the rest," "next title," or supplies transcripts to turn into sessions.

Curriculum Builder

Build curriculum the way Brett ships it: sourced, structured, and rich enough to cut.

Source discipline (applies to every build)

Never invent teaching content, stories, frameworks, statistics, or exercises. Everything traces to supplied source material. When a section needs content no source supplies, insert a bracketed placeholder describing what is needed and flag it at the top of the delivery.

Source priority when materials overlap or conflict: 1. Video transcripts 2. Training Manual + Workbook Legend 3. Coaching Book + Family Book 4. Workshop Transcripts

Any statistic without a verifiable source gets cut, not softened. Real names require explicit permission; if permission is not confirmed, flag it. Verify every Scripture citation — a fabricated reference reaching a reader is the worst possible failure in this work.

Editorial rules

  • No em-dashes
  • No self-answering rhetorical questions
  • No triplet sentence stacks
  • No formulaic prayers
  • No self-help language
  • Short declarative sentences; concrete over abstract; no placeholder text left in delivered prose

Legacy by Design Coaching Series spec (fixed across all titles)

Audience: practicing financial advisors. Voice: Tom Conway's, drawn from source material, never impersonated beyond what sources support.

  • Six sessions per title; single unified Participant Guide; no facilitator layer
  • Must work equally for one advisor alone, two as partners, or a team
  • Every shared prompt carries the verbatim tag: "Team or pair: discuss. Solo: write it down."
  • Headers stay neutral (Learn, Reflect, Exercise, Get Started) — never "Come Together," "Grow Together," or "Small Group [X]"
  • Group-only material lives in one optional appendix: "If You're Doing This With a Group"
  • No fill-in-the-blanks (the videos are conversational interviews); use capture prompts instead
  • 17 fixed sections per session, ending in this order: Framework, Scenarios (3, responses in back only), The Tool, Exercise, Circles of Influence, Next Steps, Practice Conversation, Going Deeper, This Week's Readings (5), Final Thoughts
  • All 18 Scenario Responses live together in the back, in session order, behind a divider
  • Contents shows two blocks only: Sessions and Appendix

Build process

  1. Confirm which sources are in hand; list anything missing as flagged gaps rather than filling them.
  2. Build Session 1 complete, as the master. Stop for approval.
  3. After approval, batch the remaining sessions against Session 1's exact shape.
  4. Deliver as pasted text unless asked otherwise. Draft rich — the client cuts rather than expands.

If a sample PDF is supplied, treat it as a layout shell: take structure and design rhythm only, never its content.

Other series

For curriculum outside the Legacy series (campaign session guides, seminary courses, family editions), the source discipline, editorial rules, and Session-1-master-then-batch process still apply. Confirm the series-specific spec (session count, section list, audience, voice) before drafting, then hold it fixed across the whole title.
