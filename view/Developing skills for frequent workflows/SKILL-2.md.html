Skill 2: category × title catalog builds and expansions

name: catalog-architect description: Build and expand LifeTogether's large category × title catalogs — church campaigns (40 Days of...), corporate and HR training (Marketplace Intelligence), seminary course maps, flourishing libraries. Use whenever Brett asks to create a catalog or library, add categories, double a list, take a catalog to a title-count target, generate titles per category, or translate church categories into business language.

Catalog Architect

Brett grows catalogs the same way every time: a fixed category architecture, a fixed depth per category, and expansion in numbered parts. Match that system exactly.

The unit of work

A catalog entry is never a bare title. Standard form: - Title - Subtitle - Series breakdown (commonly a 4–6 part series, or whatever depth the catalog specifies)

Campaign catalogs carry the format in the name ("40 Days of ___") and may span the five-format system: 40-Day, 30-Day, 21-Day, 7-Day, Catalytic Sundays.

Category architecture

  • Fix the depth first (commonly 10 campaigns or courses per category; some catalogs run 20, 25, or 50) and hold it uniform. Uneven depth reads as unfinished.
  • Expansions arrive as either "double the depth" or "add N more categories." Add new categories as a numbered Part (Part Four, Part Five...) that continues the existing category numbering. Never renumber what exists.
  • The master document is the single source of truth. Merge new material into it and recover prior versions rather than forking. Deduplicate against every existing part before delivering new titles.

Honest counting

State the real math every time: categories × depth = titles, with running totals per part. If Brett names a target (say 2,000 titles), never pad or double-count to reach it — show the true current total and a documented path to the target. Accuracy is the trust currency of these catalogs; an inflated count is worse than a shortfall.

Known variants to offer when they fit

  • Biblical sets: Scripture- or figure-anchored titles per category
  • Single-word lists ("40 Days of ___" one-word completions)
  • Audience-specific sets (Adult Ministry, youth, women's, and similar)
  • Dual-language pairs: church edition ↔ marketplace/business edition, same architecture with translated vocabulary
  • Highlight layer: a "Top 10 categories × top 5 titles" featured section in master-directory style, linking into the full library

Title craft

  • Benefit-forward and concrete; the subtitle carries the promise
  • No near-duplicates across categories; vary the sentence shapes so long lists do not read machine-generated
  • Match the catalog's frame: formation language for church, outcomes language for corporate

Delivery

Structure output with category headers, numbered titles, and per-part totals so the math can be verified at a glance on a phone.
