Worked Example — The "40 Days of ___" Family

This is a reference build showing the full method: philosophy, family system, and three per-title essence briefs. New series get their own philosophy and system; reuse the shape of this document, not its contents.

Design Philosophy — "Luminous Passage"

Light is the protagonist. Every composition is a passage of light moving through a field of deep, saturated color — light that has somewhere to go and something to touch. The darkness is never empty; it is the depth that makes the light legible. The work should feel like a moment held still: the instant before dawn breaks, the second a seed leaves the hand, the breath a flame takes before it stands.

Space is generous and vertical. One focal event commands the canvas; everything else is atmosphere serving it. Vast fields of color breathe around the event the way silence surrounds a single spoken word. Nothing crowds. Nothing decorates. Every mark that exists, exists because the composition needs it.

Color works in deep grounds and one luminous counterpoint. The ground is rich and enveloping — indigo, garnet, storm teal — and the counterpoint is warm and alive: gold, ember, dawn. Surfaces carry the tooth of printed lithography: fine grain, soft vignette, ink that feels pressed into paper rather than lit on a screen.

Rhythm comes from patient accumulation. Small repeated marks — rings, seeds, streaks — gather in the hundreds until repetition itself becomes meaning, the way daily practice becomes formation. The accumulation must look meticulously placed, the product of countless hours, rewarding a viewer who leans in.

Composition converges. Light rays, particle paths, halos, and lit zones all conduct the eye to a single destination, and that destination is the word. Typography is not laid over the art; it is where the art arrives. Text stays minimal — an eyebrow, a title, a subtitle, a mark — set with painstaking, master-level care, every measurement the result of refinement after refinement.

Family System (fixed across the series)

  • Format: 1200×1800 (2:3 portrait), supersampled 2× during render
  • Grid: eyebrow "FORTY DAYS OF" at 57% height; title word centered, auto-fit to 78% width; 90px gold rule; subtitle; brand line "LIFETOGETHER" with diamond ornaments at 94%
  • Type: Playfair Display (ExtraBold, tracked) for the title word; Cormorant Garamond (letterspaced caps for eyebrow and brand, italic for subtitle)
  • Ink: warm ivory type with a soft warm glow beneath — legibility earned by a lit zone, never a box
  • Texture: film grain + corner vignette on every cover
  • Structural motif: one luminous focal event above the title whose energy descends into the type zone
  • Unique per title: central metaphor, dominant palette, composition energy

Per-Title Essence Briefs

40 Days of Prayer — "Learning to Live in Conversation with God" Heart: contact between heaven and an ordinary life. Register: hushed, expectant. Metaphor: a struck point of golden light rippling outward in rings, with fine streams of particles ascending from below into it — conversation moving both directions.

40 Days of Generosity — "Open Hands, Overflowing Life" Heart: release that multiplies. Register: warm, abundant, joyful. Metaphor: two cupped arcs — open hands — from which a fountain of gold seeds scatters upward and outward, the outer arcs falling back to frame the word.

40 Days of Courage — "Faith That Stands When Everything Shakes" Heart: a small brave light that will not go out. Register: storm-dark, resolute. Metaphor: a single standing flame holding back a field of diagonal wind, its halo pushing the dark off the title.

Motif Ledger (running)

  • Prayer → rings + ascending streams
  • Generosity → cupped arcs + seed fountain
  • Courage → standing flame + diagonal wind
