Positioning: Not a Campaign — A Whole-Church Operating System

This is the pitch to the Senior Pastor: not one more campaign login, but a Whole-Life Discipleship Operating System — every ministry in the church, from the women's Bible study to the grief support group, running on the same theological foundation, the same teaching architecture, and the same design system, while each ministry keeps its own voice, its own leader, and its own pace.

Whole church, not whole-church-at-once. Everyone can be on the same page when unity matters (churchwide campaigns) and on their own page when their season of life requires it (ministry-specific experiences) — the best of both worlds, curated and vetted by the Senior Pastor so every ministry teaches from the same accurate theology, philosophy, and strategy.

Part 3: The Curriculum Builder

Every one of the 125 titles above runs through the same build:

  1. Choose your length — 21-Day, 30-Day, 40-Day, or a custom week count (4/6/8/10/12+). The system generates Master Teaching Notes, the Small Group Workbook, and the Personal Journey/Devotional at matching length automatically.
  2. Choose your customization tier — Ready to Use / Lightly Customized (branding, leader letter, dates, local application) / Fully Customized (title, teaching, stories, videos, questions, daily readings, design, pathway).
  3. Choose your format — Digital (app/PDF) or Print-Ready — and every layout is Canva-ready so a local team can drop in their own logo, colors, and leader photo without touching the underlying content.
  4. Local voice, master accuracy — the Senior Pastor's own material, church library, and the LifeTogether master library are blended so every ministry teaches from the same verified theology and strategy, even when 25 different leaders are running 25 different studies.

Part 4: The Finder Architecture — Three Doors, One Library

Three distinct entry points into the same library, each solving a different search intent:

Finder What it's for Who uses it
Curriculum Finder Browsing the full 6,200+ title master directory by ministry, felt need, theme, age, group size, teaching style, format Ministry leaders, small group leaders picking their next series
Campaign Finder Churchwide, all-in initiatives — Catalyst Sunday, 7-Day, 21-Day, 30-Day, 40-Day formats Senior Pastor, staff planning whole-church unity moments
Journey Finder (Personal Devotionals) Individual and family daily devotional companions, self-paced Individuals, families, anyone going through material solo

Naming options for the third finder — you flagged it's clever but not yet clear. A few directions to react to: - Journey Finder (Devotionals) — clean, matches the "Curriculum Finder / Campaign Finder" rhythm - The Journey Finder — with "Personal Devotionals" as a permanent subhead rather than parenthetical - My Journey Finder — leans personal/possessive, distinguishes it clearly from the group-facing tools - Daily Journey Finder — foregrounds the daily-devotional use case up front

Part 5: The Business Model — All-Access vs. Module Upsells

  • All-Access Pass — one church subscription unlocks all 25 ministry libraries plus the full Campaign system. Every ministry leader and small group leader logs in under the church account and pulls what's unique to their ministry through the Finder.
  • Module Upsells — Family Module and Youth/Children Module are sold and licensed separately from the 25 adult/life-stage ministries above, since they carry their own companion editions and production cost.
  • Access lives at the church level, not per-leader — consistent with how the Small Group Master Brochure and campaign subscriptions already work.

Part 6: How Access Actually Cascades

A. Inside the Church — Role by Role

Role Sees Customization Rights Can Use the Builder?
Staff / Ministry Directors Full 6,200+ title library, all 25 ministries, all campaigns Fully Customized — title, teaching, stories, video, design Yes — Full Builder
Volunteer Ministry Leaders Their ministry's library only (e.g., Women's leader sees Women's titles) Lightly Customized — branding, leader letter, dates, local application Local Customization Builder only
Small Group Leaders Only the specific series assigned or selected for their group Ready to Use, or Lightly Customized if their ministry leader enables it No — they receive, they don't build
Congregation / Members Personal Journey Finder — devotionals, family journals, personal studies Personal customization: name, family names, cover photo, dedication Personal Journal Builder only

The permission logic is simple: the further down the chain, the narrower the library and the lighter the customization — a small group leader isn't rewriting theology, a member isn't touching teaching notes, they're personalizing a cover and a name field.

B. Outside the Church — Business & Nonprofit Leaders

This is a real fork, worth deciding deliberately rather than defaulting into it:

  • Option 1 — Separate B2B license. Purpose Driven Business (and a parallel nonprofit track) sells directly to companies and nonprofits, independent of any church relationship. Its own pricing, its own sales motion, its own case studies (Movement Mortgage as the flagship).
  • Option 2 — Church-sponsored bridge. A church with an active Marketplace & Workplace Ministry can gift or extend Purpose Driven Business access to business owners in their congregation as a relationship-building value-add — without merging the two licensing systems.

Recommendation: keep Purpose Driven Business and the nonprofit track as their own license, sold on their own terms — but build the bridge as an option, since it turns Marketplace Ministry into a warm lead source for the B2B product rather than asking two very different buyers (a Small Groups Pastor and a company CEO) to go through the same checkout.

C. The "Print One" Model — Fully Customized, Single Unit

Yes — this works as its own standalone micro-product, separate from any subscription:

  • A member (or anyone, church-subscriber or not) builds one fully personalized journal or workbook through a simplified Canva-based Personal Journal Builder — their name, a dedication, a cover photo, maybe a translation preference.
  • Output is print-ready — either a downloadable PDF or a ship-to-print single copy.
  • ~$25 per unit, no subscription required. This is priced and positioned like a personalized gift, not a curriculum purchase: a grief journal personalized with a name, a marriage journal for an anniversary, a family devotional with the kids' names on the cover.
  • It also works inside an All-Access church: a member whose church already has the digital library pays the $25 just for the physical, personalized print copy.

This gives you three simultaneous revenue lines off the same content: church subscription (recurring), B2B licensing (Purpose Driven Business/nonprofit), and individual print-on-demand (one-off, gift-driven).

Part 7: The Seven Ministry Builder Resource Categories

Every one of the 25 ministries gets the same seven resource types — ordered here by the felt need that hits a Senior Pastor first, not alphabetically.

1. Launch & Lead Guide Felt need: "Will this ministry actually get off the ground — and survive past month three?" The step-by-step for standing up this specific ministry: how to structure it, recruit a leader, set the first 90 days, avoid the most common reasons volunteer ministries stall or quietly die.

2. Leader Training & Development Course Felt need: "Is the person I'm about to hand this to actually equipped to lead it well?" A short course (video + guide) that trains the volunteer or staff ministry leader — not just to teach content, but to shepherd people, facilitate hard conversations, and lead a group well.

3. Sermon & Message Support Kit Felt need: "Can I connect this to what I'm already preaching, so the whole church feels the momentum, not just the group?" Message outlines, illustrations, and launch-Sunday material the Senior Pastor can use to introduce or reinforce the ministry from the platform — tying the small group experience to the sermon calendar.

4. Core Campaigns & Curriculum Library Felt need: "What are they actually going to teach, week to week?" The customizable campaign/series content itself — the 125 titles already built, in 21/30/40-day or custom-week formats, at Ready to Use / Lightly / Fully Customized tiers.

5. Best-Practices Playbook & Small Group Handbook Felt need: "Will this still be healthy — and growing — a year from now?" The sustaining wisdom: how to multiply groups, keep volunteer leaders from burning out, measure ministry health, and scale without losing quality.

6. Devotional & Personal Journeys Felt need: "Does this reach people beyond the one hour a week they're in the room?" The daily, personal or family-length companion to the group series, so engagement extends into people's homes between sessions.

7. Tools, Templates & Operational Toolkit Felt need: "Do we have the operational plumbing to actually run this without reinventing it every time?" Transferable, non-content assets: sign-up forms, communication and social templates, event checklists, budget templates — the reusable back-office layer behind every ministry.

Part 8: Full Resource Build-Out — Small Group Ministry (Prototype)

Using Small Group / Community Life Ministry as the template — this is the pattern the other 24 ministries get built from.

  2. Choosing Your Model — open vs. closed groups, sermon-based vs. topical, semester vs. year-round, life-stage vs. affinity
  3. Structuring the Ministry — group size targets, leader-to-coach ratios, staff oversight
  4. Recruiting Your First Leaders — the ideal-leader profile and a recruiting conversation script
  5. The Launch Calendar — a 90-day plan: stage vision-casting, sign-up systems, first-meeting logistics
  6. Curating the First Series — how to pick the right opening study
  7. The First Meeting Playbook — icebreakers, covenant conversation, calendar-setting
  8. Common Pitfalls — attrition, cliquishness, groups that never multiply
  9. 30/60/90-Day Health Check — early warning signs and what to measure

2. Leader Training & Development Course — "Circle Up: Leader Training"

  1. The Heart of a Group Leader — shepherd, not lecturer
  2. Facilitating Discussion — open questions, handling silence, balancing talkers
  3. Creating Emotional Safety — confidentiality, modeling vulnerability
  4. Handling Conflict & Hard Conversations — disagreement, doctrinal rabbit trails, dominant personalities
  5. Pastoral Care Basics — when to refer out (hand-off point to Care & Counseling Ministry), praying with people, recognizing crisis
  6. Group Dynamics Over a Semester — forming/storming/norming/performing, applied
  7. Multiplying Your Group — spotting an apprentice, knowing when and how to split
  8. Practicum & Certification — a mock-led session and a signed leader covenant

3. Sermon & Message Support Kit — "Circle Up Sunday"

  • Launch-weekend message outline: "Better Together — Why You Weren't Meant to Do Life Alone"
  • Testimony interview script/prompt questions for a real group member video
  • Stage announcement script + slide template
  • Bulletin insert / QR sign-up card copy
  • Mid-semester "momentum check" message outline (week 3 of a new push)
  • Annual "State of Our Groups" message outline for the Senior Pastor — health update and re-cast of vision

4. Core Campaigns & Curriculum Library — Mapped to a Maturity Pathway

  • Better Together — entry point: why community matters (best first series)
  • Real Community — deepening: acquaintance to family
  • The Group That Changes You — vulnerability and growth
  • Multiply — advanced: building a multiplication culture
  • Circle Up — functions as dual-use: participant-facing for members curious about leading, and threads directly into the Launch & Lead Guide and Leader Training Course above — worth flagging since it's the one title in this ministry that isn't purely member-facing curriculum.

5. Best-Practices Playbook & Small Group Handbook — "The Healthy Group Handbook"

  1. What Makes a Group Healthy — attendance consistency, depth of spiritual conversation, care activation, multiplication-readiness
  2. The Coaching Structure — how staff/coaches oversee leaders, span-of-care ratios
  3. The Group Life Cycle — forming, thriving, plateauing, multiplying, or ending well
  4. Avoiding Leader Burnout — sabbaticals, co-leading models, an apprentice pipeline
  5. Handling Group Conflict & Splits — shepherding a group through relational fracture
  6. Scaling Beyond 20, 50, 100+ Groups — org structure by ministry size
  7. Annual Ministry Health Report Template — the metrics a Senior Pastor should see quarterly

6. Devotional & Personal Journeys — "Better Together: A Journey Toward Real Community"

A daily devotional (21/30/40-day) built to feed into the group rather than stand alone — each week's readings culminate in one prompt the person brings back to share at their next group meeting, so the personal journey and the group meeting reinforce each other rather than running in parallel.

Part 11: Singles Ministry — Mission, Vision & Values

Mission To help single adults live a whole, purposeful, Christ-centered life — not a waiting room, but a calling.

Vision A church where singleness is a full season of kingdom purpose, not a problem to be solved — where single adults lead, serve, and grow with the same dignity and expectation as every other adult in the church.

Values - Wholeness Before Relationship — identity in Christ comes before relationship status. - Community Over Isolation — every single adult has a place to belong, not just a program to attend. - Purpose Over Waiting — singleness is a season to steward, not simply endure. - Honesty Over Performance — real conversations about dating, purity, and loneliness, without shame. - Every Season Welcome — programming serves the 20-something and the 50-something single adult alike.

Five Premier Experiences

1. Whole — Discovering Your Identity in Christ - Approach: challenges the "half a person" narrative; grounds identity in Christ before relationship status. - Curriculum: 6 sessions — Made Whole, The Comparison Trap, Loneliness Isn't Weakness, Contentment as Warfare, Serving from Wholeness, Living Sent as a Single Adult. - Journey Companion: 21-day devotional ending in a written "wholeness declaration."

2. Worth the Wait — A Biblical Vision for Dating and Purity - Approach: honest, non-shaming framework for dating and purity that addresses app-driven dating culture directly. - Curriculum: 6 sessions — a biblical vision for sex and dating, boundaries, red flags, healthy dating rhythms, purity-culture wounds and grace, discerning a future spouse. - Journey Companion: 30-day devotional/journal with a "values before dating" worksheet.

3. Not Alone — Community for the Single Season - Approach: directly addresses isolation, positioning the group itself as the antidote. - Curriculum: 6 sessions — group covenant, vulnerability, holiday loneliness, chosen family, hospitality practices. - Journey Companion: a 14-day devotional timed for high-loneliness seasons, usable as a stand-alone care resource.

4. Called and Complete — Purpose Beyond Relationship Status - Approach: treats singleness as a season of undivided devotion (1 Corinthians 7) rather than a deficiency. - Curriculum: 6 sessions — spiritual gifts discovery, calling, undivided devotion, serving from singleness, kingdom ambition. - Journey Companion: 40-day "calling journey" devotional pairing Scripture with vocational discernment exercises.

5. Ready for Someday — Preparing Now for the Marriage to Come - Approach: forward-looking, practical formation now rather than passive waiting. - Curriculum: 6 sessions — character now shapes marriage later, financial readiness, emotional health, family-of-origin work, discerning godly character in a partner. - Journey Companion: 21-day devotional built around journaling prompts a person could someday share with a future spouse.
