2. LinkedIn About

Every season of my work has come down to one question: what actually helps a person take their next step of faith?

I started out in Silicon Valley, doing marketing in the early Macintosh era, and I loved it. Then Bob Buford's challenge to move from success to significance rerouted my life. I went to seminary, earned my M.Div., and served as the Small Group Champion at Willow Creek and then at Saddleback during the Purpose Driven era — helping develop 40 Days of Purpose and co-writing the Purpose Driven small group series with Zondervan. Those campaigns traveled to tens of thousands of churches and tens of millions of people, and they became the source code for everything I've built since.

For 25 years I've led LifeTogether Ministries, producing more than 500 video and print campaigns with churches across the country and putting over 50 million studies and resources into people's hands. Along the way I've produced series with John Maxwell (Today Matters) and Ken Blanchard, called Bob Buford a mentor for 25 years, and served alongside Leadership Network for two decades with the pastors and marketplace leaders who shaped a generation.

During COVID, I began listening to a different set of leaders: families of significant wealth and the advisors who walk with them. Different table, same question — how does a family pass on faith, wisdom, and unity, not just assets, to the next generation?

That question is now the center of my work. As co-founder of Family Legacy by Design, I work with Tom Conway to help families move from legacy by default to legacy by design across five areas: personal, family, financial, business, and charitable. With the Ron Blue Institute, I've helped create stewardship and generosity resources, including bringing Leverage: Using Temporal Wealth for Eternal Gain by Kenneth Boa and Russ Crosson into small groups. And as Chief Marketing Officer at Equity Partners, I serve advisors building firms worthy of their clients' trust.

Kellogg trained me to think like a marketer. Seminary taught me why it matters. Twenty-five years with churches taught me how movements actually start: one group, one family, one next step at a time.

If you're a pastor, an advisor, or a family thinking about what you'll pass on, I'd love to talk.

5. FLBD Site — Brett's Bio

Brett Eastman — Co-Founder, Family Legacy by Design

Brett Eastman has spent twenty-five years helping people take their next step of faith — and building the resources that make it possible at scale. A business major and graduate of Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management, Brett began his career in Silicon Valley marketing in the early Macintosh era. Bob Buford's Halftime challenge to trade success for significance rerouted his path: he earned his M.Div. and served as the Small Group Champion at Willow Creek Community Church and at Saddleback Church, where he helped develop 40 Days of Purpose and co-wrote the Purpose Driven small group series — resources that reached tens of thousands of churches and tens of millions of people.

As founder and president of LifeTogether Ministries, Brett has produced more than 500 video and print campaigns and over 50 million studies and resources, creating series with leaders including John Maxwell and Ken Blanchard. For twenty years he served alongside Bob Buford's Leadership Network, and Buford remained his mentor for twenty-five.

In recent years Brett has brought that same discipleship DNA to families of wealth and the advisors who serve them. He has helped create stewardship and generosity resources with the Ron Blue Institute — including bringing Leverage: Using Temporal Wealth for Eternal Gain by Kenneth Boa and Russ Crosson into small groups — and serves as Chief Marketing Officer of Equity Partners. As co-founder of Family Legacy by Design, Brett works with Tom Conway to help families gain clarity, alignment, and communication across the five areas of legacy, so the next generation receives more than wealth: faith, wisdom, and a story worth continuing.

Brett makes his home in Rancho Santa Margarita, California.
