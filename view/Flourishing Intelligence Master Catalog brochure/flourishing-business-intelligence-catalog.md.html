Flourishing Business Intelligence™

A Whole-Organization Flourishing Catalog — 100 Campaigns Across 10 Domains

What it is: Where Flourishing LifeTogether applies a whole-life framework to a person, Flourishing Business Intelligence applies the same research-grounded architecture to a business itself — treating the organization as the thing that needs to flourish, not just the people inside it. Ten domains, each with 10 campaigns, each built for a six-part movement: Assess → Diagnose → Realign → Build Practices → Strengthen Relationships → Institutionalize.

Grounded in: The same body of research behind Harvard's Human Flourishing Program and organizational-health literature — the recognition that a business's long-term performance depends on interconnected domains (culture, financial stewardship, people, customers, innovation, operations, governance, resilience, growth, and legacy), not any single metric like quarterly revenue.
