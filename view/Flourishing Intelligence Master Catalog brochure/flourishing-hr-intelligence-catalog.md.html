Flourishing HR Intelligence™

A People-Function Flourishing Catalog — 250 Campaigns Across 10 Domains

What it is: Where Flourishing Business Intelligence looks at the whole organization, Flourishing HR Intelligence is built specifically for the people who run the people function — HR officers, People & Culture leaders, and the managers who carry HR responsibility without the title. It treats HR's own flourishing — not just the flourishing of the employees HR serves — as the subject worth building around.

Grounded in: Organizational psychology, occupational health research, and the same flourishing framework used across this catalog family — applied to the specific pressures of a role that sits between leadership and employees, and often absorbs the tension of both.
