Flourishing Leaders Intelligence™

A Mid-Level Leadership Catalog — 100 Campaigns Across 10 Domains

What it is: Distinct from CEO & President Intelligence (the top seat) and Business Owner Intelligence (financial and identity entanglement) — this catalog is for the people who lead without either: first-time managers, team leads, department heads, and project leaders. The everyday craft of leading a team you don't own and don't have final authority over.

Grounded in: The same flourishing framework as the rest of this catalog family, applied to the specific realities of middle leadership — managing former peers, leading up and sideways as much as down, and carrying responsibility without always carrying full authority.
