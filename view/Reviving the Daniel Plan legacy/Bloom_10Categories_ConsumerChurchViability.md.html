The 10 Core Categories & Series for Bloom/Vitality Platform: The cascade positioning with consumer + church viability analysis

BLOOM/VITALITY: 10 CORE CATEGORIES + 10 FLAGSHIP SERIES

The Cascade Model: Body → Community → Meaning → Resilience → Spirit

Core Positioning: "Christians have been told: 'Fix your mental health. Fix your marriage. Fix your finances. Fix your meaning.' We say: Start where the body is. Rest. Eat well. Move. Be with people. Watch what happens. Energy returns. Community forms. Belonging restores meaning. Meaning makes mental resilience possible. Resilience opens the capacity for spiritual depth and virtue. It's not linear. It's cascading. That's flourishing."

Research Foundation: Byron Johnson (Baylor/Harvard): Religious community participation is the strongest predictor of flourishing across all domains—stronger than wealth, education, or health alone. Integration of physical health + spiritual formation + relational thriving = flourishing.

THE 10 CORE CATEGORIES

CATEGORY 1: THE BODY AWAKENS

Gateway Category — Physical health as permission

6-Week Series: "The Body Is Prayer: Reclaiming Your Physical Self"

Sessions: 1. Permission — The theology: your body matters to God (incarnation, resurrection, image-bearing) 2. Sleep — The foundation: why rest is obedience, not laziness 3. Movement — The invitation: embodied prayer and confidence 4. Nourishment — The practice: eating as gratitude and boundary-setting 5. Belonging — The shift: from isolated self-care to communal flourishing 6. Integration — The cascade: how physical awakening opens everything else

Small Group Format: - Teaching video (8-10 min) - Personal reflection workbook ("Where is your body asking for attention?") - Group conversation starter ("What did you notice this week in your body?") - Daily devotional (40-day option: physical practices paired with Scripture) - Action: One small change this week (add 10 min movement, skip one screen hour, try one new meal)

Why It Works: - For consumers: "I thought I had to fix my mind first. Turns out my body was asking the whole time." - For churches: Gateway series for anyone—no theological baggage, totally practical, builds curiosity for deeper work - Byron Johnson angle: "Religious participation + physical practice = belonging + meaning"
