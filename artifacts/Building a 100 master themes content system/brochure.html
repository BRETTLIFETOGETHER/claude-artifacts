The 167-hour problem

40 DAY CAMPAIGNS · 02

A weekend message, however good, evaporates by Tuesday unless it has somewhere to live during the week. Your people hear one hour of truth — and then walk into 167 hours of everything else.

A campaign closes that gap. It is one theme, one anchor passage, and one journey shared by the entire congregation in the same season — the weekend teaching, a daily devotional in every hand, small groups around every table, students and kids on the same track, and one small obedience every single day. The message stops being a sermon and becomes a shared life.

Twenty-five years of churchwide campaigns — from the rooms where the Purpose Driven Life campaign was designed to more than 500 church partnerships — proved the same pattern every time: when everyone moves together, everything moves.
