FAQ

How do 40-day campaigns
work for churches?

Campaigns are licensed to your church and ready to launch — choose one, customize it, and roll it out across your whole congregation.

What is a churchwide campaign? −
A campaign is a coordinated discipleship experience built around one theme for a defined season — weekend teaching, a daily devotional, small-group curriculum, family editions, and a clear next step, all moving together so the message becomes part of daily life.
Can we use our pastor's sermon series? +
Yes. Any campaign can be built around an upcoming series, past sermons, a book, or teaching notes — so the campaign sounds like your pastor and serves your church.
Do we need small groups already in place? +
No. Many campaigns are designed to launch groups, not just serve existing ones — they create appetite for community before asking people to commit.
What formats are available? +
Every campaign comes in 7-day, 21-day, 30-day, and 40-day formats, plus a 6-week small-group series and one-year initiatives — with editions for families, students, and children.
