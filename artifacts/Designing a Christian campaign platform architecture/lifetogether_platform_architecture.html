The six layers

One record, six layers of meaning

Every campaign is a single database record that surfaces through six stacked layers. A campaign lives in exactly one category (its permanent home) but can appear in many collections and ship as many affinity editions — without ever being duplicated. This is the spine that prevents the spreadsheet problem.

01
Channels 10 domains · the stores within the store
Top-level, evergreen lanes a pastor browses by topic. Each holds 8–12 categories.
02
Categories 85+ · permanent taxonomic homes
A campaign belongs to exactly one category inside one channel. Min 50 / target 100 campaigns each.
03
Collections curated cross-channel shelves
Seasonal, Outcome, Affinity, and Editorial shelves that pull across many channels. Temporary or merchandised, not taxonomic.
04
Affinities 7 audiences · the lens, not the location
Re-curate the entire front door for a persona, and define which editions a campaign ships.
Cuts across all layers above and below — this is the layer your old "audience = channel" model wrongly flattened.
05
Campaigns 10,000+ · ranked, flagship-first
Top 10 flagship row per category, then the next 90 behind "view all." Rank sets order and flagship selection.
06
Assets the deliverables inside each campaign
Devotional, small-group guide, sermon series, leader guide, graphics, editions, launch plan — across six formats.
Layers 01 + 02

The 10 channels & their categories

The optimal top-level structure — domains, not audiences and not seasons. Search filters categories live; counts are target depth at maturity (min 50 each). Flagship categories are marked.

Layer 04 · the lens

The affinity architecture

An affinity answers "who is this person?" — and re-skins the whole platform for them. Advisor, Business Owner, Christian School, and Church-Planting Network were never categories; they're audiences. Each affinity surfaces certain channels first and demands certain editions.

A category is a location

Prayer lives in Spiritual Formation. Always. It's where the campaign is permanently filed — one home, fixed in the taxonomy.

An affinity is a lens

The Business Owner affinity re-orders the home page (Purpose & Work, Money & Generosity, Leadership first) and triggers a Business Owner edition of campaigns. Same library, re-curated.

Layer 03 · curated shelves

Collections, not more categories

The same campaign appears in many collections but only one category. Seasons belong here — never as channels — so nothing gets double-filed.

Layer 05 · finding a campaign

How a pastor reaches a campaign

Multiple valid entry points, ranked. Format (7 / 21 / 30 / 40-day) is a filter and a badge — never a top-level path, because pastors start with need, not duration.

Recommended default
Affinity→ Channel→ Collection→ Campaign

Persona first, then domain, then a curated shelf. Three confident taps from "I'm a family pastor" to the right campaign.

Also strong — topic-led
Channel→ Category→ Campaign

For the pastor who already knows the subject. Lands on the ranked flagship row inside the category.

Power path — the decision engine
Search + Filter→ Scoped results→ Compare→ Campaign

Your existing Campaign Finder spec, kept almost entirely. Scope search within affinity + channel so it stays fast at 10k.
