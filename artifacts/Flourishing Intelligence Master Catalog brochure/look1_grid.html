03 · The Research Behind It

Grounded, Then Answered Biblically

The ten domains echo the territory mapped by Harvard's Human Flourishing Program and the positive-psychology research it helped popularize: meaning, character, relationships, health, happiness, and financial stability as interconnected, not separate. Flourishing LifeTogether takes that research seriously, then answers it with Scripture as the deeper authority.

04 · The Why / The Breakthrough

A marriage study here, a financial course there — nobody hands you the whole picture. People aren't looking for one more topic. They're looking for a whole life that works.
