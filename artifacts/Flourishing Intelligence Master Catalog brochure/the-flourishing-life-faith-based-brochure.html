Strategic Positioning

Credible, not overstated.

The architecture rests on real correspondence between flourishing research and biblical formation — the positioning has to reflect that honestly.

Avoid

"Harvard discovered the five biblical purposes."

Use instead

"Modern flourishing research is confirming that human beings need more than success, comfort, or financial security — dimensions that biblical discipleship has addressed for centuries."

The Architecture

Five primary domains, ten felt-need categories each, ten campaigns per category.

100 campaigns per domain — 500 in total — built around the felt needs people actually name, then answered biblically.
