Why Now

Performance was never the whole story.

Organizations invest heavily in performance while employees quietly carry stress, disconnection, family strain, low trust, and uncertainty. Flourishing Intelligence connects human well-being with sustainable organizational performance — treating the two as one problem, not two departments.

What it is: a browsable library of 600 campaigns, curricula, coaching journeys, assessments, and conversation guides — plus 450 role-specific journeys built for companies, executives, managers, teams, and families. Every entry is grounded in a named research foundation, from Harvard's Human Flourishing Program to attachment theory to occupational health science, and every entry can be personalized by role, need, life stage, goal, duration, and learning preference.

Production Architecture

One catalog, eight deliverable formats.

Every campaign and journey can be produced as any of the following, each customized with the company's own stories, brand, and data.

Online brochure

Markets and explains each series — why, what, who, outcomes, module map, and next steps.

10-page conversation guide

Structured dialogue with context, assessment, guided questions, exercises, and commitments.

21-day journey

One daily practice — insight, reflection, action, conversation, and tracker.

30-day journey

Deeper habit formation with weekly reviews, coaching prompts, and measurement.

4-week curriculum

Compact team learning — four sessions with video, discussion, and application.

6-week curriculum

The full flourishing pathway — six sessions integrating all dimensions, with an action plan.

Coaching edition

Moves insight into action through goals, questions, commitments, and review — AI coach, human coach, or peer cohort.
