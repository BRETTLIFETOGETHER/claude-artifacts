The Research Behind It

Grounded, then answered biblically.

The ten domains echo the territory mapped by Harvard's Human Flourishing Program and the positive-psychology research it helped popularize — meaning, character, relationships, health, happiness, and financial stability as interconnected. Flourishing LifeTogether takes that seriously, then answers it with Scripture as the deeper authority.

The Why

Most resources treat your life one slice at a time.

A marriage study here, a financial course there — nobody hands you the whole picture. People aren't looking for one more topic. They're looking for a whole life that works.
