# Flourishing Human Side of Wealth™
### A Family Wealth Flourishing Catalog — 250 Campaigns Across 10 Domains

**What it is:** The flourishing-catalog counterpart to the Human Side of Wealth Operating System — built for families of wealth and the advisors who serve them. Where technical wealth management handles the numbers, this catalog handles what the numbers alone can't: identity, relationships, preparation, and meaning across generations. The ten domains here condense the platform's full 20-collection taxonomy into a usable flourishing structure, 25 campaigns each.

**Grounded in:** The Family Legacy By Design framework's Five Areas of Legacy (Personal, Family, Financial, Business, Charitable) and its three core words — Clarity, Alignment, Communication — paired with the same flourishing research foundation used across this catalog family.
