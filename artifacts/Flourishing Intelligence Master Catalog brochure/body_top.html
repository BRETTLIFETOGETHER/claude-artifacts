Folio III · The Research Behind It

Grounded in real research, answered with real Scripture.

The ten domains in this catalog echo the same territory mapped by Harvard's Human Flourishing Program and the broader body of positive-psychology research it helped popularize — the now well-established recognition that human wellbeing isn't one measure of success, but several interconnected domains: meaning and purpose, character and virtue, close relationships, physical and mental health, happiness and life satisfaction, and financial stability. Flourishing LifeTogether takes that research seriously, but answers it biblically rather than secularly — treating Scripture, not social science alone, as the deeper authority on what it actually means for a person to flourish.
