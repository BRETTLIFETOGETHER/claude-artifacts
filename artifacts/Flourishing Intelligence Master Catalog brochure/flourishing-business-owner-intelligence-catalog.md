# Flourishing Business Owner Intelligence™
### An Owner-Operator Flourishing Catalog — 100 Campaigns Across 10 Domains

**What it is:** Distinct from CEO & President Intelligence — this catalog is for the owner whose personal wealth, identity, and daily life are directly tied to the business itself, whether that's a founder-operator, a family business owner, or a solo entrepreneur. The pressures are different from a hired CEO's: there's no separation between "my company" and "my net worth," no board to report to, and often no clean line between owner and employee.

**Grounded in:** The same flourishing framework as the rest of this catalog family, applied to the specific realities of ownership — personal financial entanglement with the business, the absence of external accountability structures, and the singular weight of being both capital and labor.
