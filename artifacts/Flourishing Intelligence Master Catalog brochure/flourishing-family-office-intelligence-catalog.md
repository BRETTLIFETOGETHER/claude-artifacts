# Flourishing Family Office Intelligence™
### A Felt-Need Catalog for the Family Office Itself — 100 Campaigns Across 10 Categories

**What it is:** Distinct from Human Side of Wealth (built for the family) — this catalog is for the people who run the family office: the CIO, the president, the ops director, the small team caught between serving a founder, managing rising-generation expectations, and staying professionally credible on a compensation structure that can't compete with Wall Street. Built around the actual pain points family offices report, not generic wealth-management content with a family label on it.

**Grounded in:** The same flourishing framework as the rest of this catalog family, applied to the specific structural pressures unique to family offices — serving people who can fire you personally, navigating loyalty that outlasts any one family member, and proving value to a family that could always just leave.
