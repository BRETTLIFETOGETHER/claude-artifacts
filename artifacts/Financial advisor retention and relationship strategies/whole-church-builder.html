How the Builder Works

One All-Access Pass unlocks all 25 ministry libraries and the full Campaign system for the whole church — then the Builder lets every leader make it theirs, at exactly the level their role calls for.

One church subscription unlocks all 25 ministry libraries plus the full Campaign system. Every ministry leader and small group leader logs in under the church account and pulls what's unique to their ministry through the Finder.

Three Doors, One Library

Three distinct entry points into the same library, each solving a different search intent.

Browsing the full 6,200+ title master directory by ministry, felt need, theme, age, group size, teaching style, format.

Ministry & small group leaders picking their next series

Churchwide, all-in initiatives — Catalyst Sunday, 7-Day, 21-Day, 30-Day, 40-Day formats.

Senior Pastor & staff planning whole-church unity moments
Journey Finder (Personal Devotionals)

Individual and family daily devotional companions, self-paced.

Individuals, families, anyone going through material solo
Still Open — Naming the Third Finder
  • Journey Finder (Devotionals) — clean, matches the "Curriculum Finder / Campaign Finder" rhythm.
  • The Journey Finder — with "Personal Devotionals" as a permanent subhead rather than parenthetical.
  • My Journey Finder — leans personal/possessive, distinguishes it clearly from the group-facing tools.
  • Daily Journey Finder — foregrounds the daily-devotional use case up front.

Part Six — Beyond the Church

Business & Nonprofit Leaders

A real fork, worth deciding deliberately rather than defaulting into it.

Option 1 — Separate B2B License

Purpose Driven Business (and a parallel nonprofit track) sells directly to companies and nonprofits, independent of any church relationship — its own pricing, its own sales motion, its own case studies, with Movement Mortgage as the flagship.

Option 2 — Church-Sponsored Bridge

A church with an active Marketplace & Workplace Ministry can gift or extend Purpose Driven Business access to business owners in their congregation as a relationship-building value-add — without merging the two licensing systems.

Recommendation

Keep Purpose Driven Business and the nonprofit track as their own license, sold on their own terms — but build the bridge as an option. It turns Marketplace Ministry into a warm lead source for the B2B product, rather than asking two very different buyers — a Small Groups Pastor and a company CEO — to go through the same checkout.

Part Seven — The Micro-Product

The "Print One" Model

A fully customized, single-unit product — separate from any subscription, and open to anyone, church-subscriber or not.

Standalone Gift

A member — or anyone at all — builds one fully personalized journal or workbook through a simplified Canva-based Personal Journal Builder: their name, a dedication, a cover photo, maybe a translation preference. Output is print-ready, as a downloadable PDF or a ship-to-print single copy.

Inside an All-Access Church

A member whose church already has the digital library pays the same $25 just for the physical, personalized print copy — a grief journal with a name on it, a marriage journal for an anniversary, a family devotional with the kids' names on the cover.

~$25 per unit, no subscription required — priced and positioned like a personalized gift, not a curriculum purchase.
