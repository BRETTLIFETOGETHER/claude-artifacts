The Product Story

Do not say ours are better sermons.

That claim is subjective and impossible to defend. Say the thing that is structurally true instead, and let the free library carry the traffic while the next step carries the business.

One honest correction before this ships

The draft headline says ten thousand free sermons. The real catalogue is 5,155 sermon messages and 17,319 campaigns — 22,474 titles, which is a better number and a truer one. Lead with the campaign figure, since nobody else has a comparable one, and never publish a count the catalogue cannot substantiate.

The naming instinct in the draft is right: do not use anything confusable with the incumbent’s brand. That is a trademark problem, not just a positioning one.
