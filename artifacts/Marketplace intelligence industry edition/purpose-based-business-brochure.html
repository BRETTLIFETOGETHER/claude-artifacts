Purpose-Based Business

The Five Purposes as a Weekly Framework for the Marketplace

Every LifeTogether churchwide campaign is built on five biblical purposes — Worship, Fellowship, Discipleship, Ministry, and Mission. Purpose-Based Business carries that same architecture into the workplace: five weeks, five purposes, one company learning to see its ordinary work as an extension of God's Kingdom.

The Framework
Five Weeks. Five Purposes. One Company on Mission.
01Week One
Worship

Work as Worship

Discovering God's presence in the ordinary work of business — where excellence, not just Sunday, becomes an offering.

02Week Two
Fellowship

Team as Family

Building a culture of authentic belonging, where colleagues become companions rather than just coworkers.

03Week Three
Discipleship

Growing Leaders, Not Just Employees

Developing character alongside competence, so people leave better than they arrived.

04Week Four
Ministry

Serving Through Your Business

Using the gifts of the company — products, profits, and people — to bless customers and community.

05Week Five
Mission

Business on Mission

Carrying Kingdom purpose beyond the walls of the company — into the marketplace, the city, and the world.

This is the purpose-built theme layer for Purpose-Based Business — the weekly architecture, not yet expanded into full session content, discussion guides, or campaign variants.
