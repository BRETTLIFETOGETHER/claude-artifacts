Why Tools

A tool is what turns
a good afternoon into
a repeatable business.

Right now the method lives in your hands and John's memory. That works beautifully for twelve guests at a time and not at all for anything beyond it. The moment a question, a sequence, or a framework is written down and designed, four things happen: you can charge for it, you can hand it to somebody else to run, you can put it in front of a woman in Tennessee who will never make it to Oregon, and you can use it to start a conversation that ends in a commission.

These twelve are not twelve products to sell separately. Some are free and exist only to bring people to the studio door. Some are sales pieces that do the persuading so you don't have to. Some are the deliverable itself. Together they cover the whole path a person travels — from I saw something on Instagram to we commissioned a piece for the family.

Build them in the order on page seven. Do not build all twelve before running the pilots; build the four that make the first bookings happen, and let real guests tell you what the other eight should say.

Each tool does one job

Attract — brings a stranger close enough to ask
Qualify — finds out who she is and what she needs
Sell — makes the case without a phone call
Deliver — carries the experience itself
Extend — turns one afternoon into a next step

If a tool doesn't do one of these five, don't build it yet.

Tools One Through Four

The Discovery Tools

01Faith Through Art Intake Form

Before she arrives, you already know her.

A short, warm questionnaire sent the moment a booking is confirmed: what season she's in, what she's carrying, a word or Scripture that matters to her right now, colors she loves, and what she hopes to take home. Kim gets the palette. John gets the text. The guest gets the unmistakable feeling that this was made for her — which is the entire justification for premium pricing. It is also, quietly, your best source of testimonial language and commission leads.

02Felt Need Finder

Start where she actually is.

Twelve questions that sort a person into one of six needs — rest, freedom, grief, direction, gratitude, legacy — and route her to the right theme, the right journey, and the right experience. Free, fast, and shareable. This is the front door of the whole business, the best email-list builder you will ever have, and the piece that later becomes the engine described on page nine.

03My Word for This Season Tool

One word, and everything else follows.

A guided exercise for naming what God appears to be forming right now — not a resolution, not a goal, a word. That word becomes the title of the painting, the spine of the journal, the thing she says out loud when someone asks about the canvas in her hallway, and the reason she books again next January. Works as a standalone downloadable, a January campaign, and the opening move of every retreat you host.

04Prayer-to-Art Discovery Guide

How a prayer becomes a color.

The translation layer — the actual method for moving from feeling to symbol to color to composition. Seven steps, worked examples, and the questions Kim already asks without realizing they're a method. This is the most distinctive intellectual property the two of you own, and it is what makes Paint Your Prayer a repeatable practice rather than a lovely idea that only works when Kim is in the room.

Tools Five Through Eight

The Journeys and the
Pieces That Sell Them

05Paint Your Prayer 10-Day Journal

The flagship.

Ten days from Created to Create to Living the Prayer — Scripture, devotional, reflection questions, a creative exercise, a journal prompt, and a prayer each day. She finishes with a completed painting, a title for it, a word for the season, and a written testimony. Sold as a book, gifted at retreats, licensed to churches, and taught live. Everything else in the catalog can be built from this one.

06Created Free 10-Day Journal

For the woman who swears she isn't creative.

The companion volume — ten days out of perfectionism, comparison, and self-editing into play, grace, and courage. This one sells to people who would never buy an art product, because it isn't really about art. Expect it to be the strongest gift purchase and the easiest women's ministry group buy of the twelve.

07Girls Road Trip Experience Catalog

Designed to be handed over by someone else.

Twelve beautiful pages a concierge, an Airbnb host, or a tasting room manager can put directly into a guest's hands: photography, the three experiences, group sizes, what an afternoon holds, what she takes home, price bands, and one obvious way to book. Build it so a partner looks generous handing it over, and it will circulate without you.

08Private Studio Retreat Guide

Sells a $2,500 day without a phone call.

The premium piece. Who this is for, hour by hour what the day holds, the pre-retreat questionnaire, the blessing at the close, what she leaves with, and the investment stated plainly. People spending real money on something this personal want to read carefully and decide privately. Give them the document that lets them.

Tools Nine Through Twelve

Legacy, the Church,
and the Master Catalog

09Legacy in Color Family Guide

The commission conversation, in printed form.

A guided process for a family — often grandparents — to name the values, Scriptures, stories, and symbols they want to hand down, and then see them gathered into one piece. It works as a retreat, as a family evening, and as the natural front end of a $2,500–$10,000 commission. It is also the tool most likely to interest financial advisors and family offices, who are always hunting for a client experience they didn't have to invent themselves.

10Live Sermon Art Planning Guide

Removes every reason a church says "let us think about it."

Written for the executive pastor, not the senior pastor: sightlines and easel placement, lighting, timing against the message, what the congregation sees on screen, what happens to the original, what to announce, and what to do the following Sunday. Logistics answered in advance turn a maybe into a date on the calendar.

11Christian Artist Calling Workbook

The mentoring pathway, on paper first.

For the emerging artist or designer: creative voice, audience, pricing and commission structure, turning originals into prints, handling comparison and visibility, and using AI honestly as a working artist. Sold on its own, it funds itself. Its real job is to identify the twenty people who will pay for a cohort.

12Faith Through Art Experience Catalog

One document that holds everything.

The master piece — experiences, private retreats, live worship art, commissions, courses, and resources, with honest price bands and a single clear path to inquire. This is what goes to churches, partners, past buyers, and anyone who asks the question every business owner waits for: so what exactly do you two do?

The Long Horizon · Concept

Faith Through
Art Intelligence

Once the twelve tools exist, something becomes possible that isn't possible now. Every one of them shares the same underlying logic: a person names where she is, and the right theme, prompt, journey, and next step follow from it. That logic is currently in John's head and in Kim's instinct. Written into a system, it can serve a thousand people at once without either of you in the room.

A guest tells the system her felt need and her season. The system returns her devotional, her art prompt, her reflection guide, her journey length, the video that walks her through it, the group experience that fits, and — when she's ready — the commission brief, the retreat kit, the church curriculum, or the family legacy piece.

This is not a replacement for the studio. It is the thing that makes the studio the front door of something much larger, and it is the only version of this business that scales beyond the number of Saturdays in a year.
