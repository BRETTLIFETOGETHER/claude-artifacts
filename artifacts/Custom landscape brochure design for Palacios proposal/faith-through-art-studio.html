The Ladder

Where the membership sits

The membership is not the business. It is what keeps people warm between the things that actually pay — the private retreats, the church weekends, and the commissions. A member who has painted with you for eleven months is the easiest booking either of you will ever take.

The discipline that makes this survivable: one theme a month, one guided project, one live session, one printable, one community share. Not a tutorial library, not a daily posting community, not a custom app. The month it becomes a second full-time job is the month it starts costing more than it earns.
