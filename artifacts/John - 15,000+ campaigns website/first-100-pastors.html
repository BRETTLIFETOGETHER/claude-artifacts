The First 100 Pastors

Working roster for the founding-church webinar. One hundred slots, nine rings, warmest first. Day-1 email subject line, from your personal inbox: "Would you join me for 60 minutes?"

"The first 100 founding churches are already in your network. You already know their names." — the webinar plan

The fill rule. No name goes on this list without a real prior touchpoint or a named referrer beside it. Famous pulpits are not the profile — the founding tiers ($199 / $599 / $1,499) are built for the typical congregation and the Saturday-night pastor, and the invitation travels person to person, not press release to public. A handful of legacy names appear below as amplifiers — people who forward, endorse, or open rooms — not as founding buyers, and every one is marked verify because roles change.
Waves. W1 = the Day-1 personal email to the 50 you know best · W2 = the wider 500-list send · R = arrives by referral, invited by name.
