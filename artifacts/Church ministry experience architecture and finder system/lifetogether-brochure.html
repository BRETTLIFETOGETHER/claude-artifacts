The Curriculum Builder

Every one of the 125 titles runs through the same build.

1

Choose your length

21-Day, 30-Day, 40-Day, or a custom week count (4/6/8/10/12+). The system generates Master Teaching Notes, the Small Group Workbook, and the Personal Journey/Devotional at matching length automatically.

2

Choose your customization tier

Ready to Use / Lightly Customized (branding, leader letter, dates, local application) / Fully Customized (title, teaching, stories, videos, questions, daily readings, design, pathway).

Choose your format

Digital (app/PDF) or Print-Ready — every layout is Canva-ready so a local team can drop in their own logo, colors, and leader photo without touching the underlying content.

4

Local voice, master accuracy

The Senior Pastor's own material, church library, and the LifeTogether master library are blended so every ministry teaches from the same verified theology and strategy, even when 25 different leaders are running 25 different studies.

Ministry Experience LibraryPart IV

The Finder Architecture

Three doors, one library.

Three distinct entry points into the same library, each solving a different search intent.

FinderWhat it's forWho uses it
Curriculum Finder Browsing the full 6,200+ title master directory by ministry, felt need, theme, age, group size, teaching style, format. Ministry leaders, small group leaders picking their next series.
Campaign Finder Churchwide, all-in initiatives — Catalyst Sunday, 7-Day, 21-Day, 30-Day, 40-Day formats. Senior Pastor, staff planning whole-church unity moments.
Journey Finder (Personal Devotionals) Individual and family daily devotional companions, self-paced. Individuals, families, anyone going through material solo.

Naming options for the third finder

Clever, but not yet clear — four directions to react to.

  • Journey Finder (Devotionals)Clean, matches the "Curriculum Finder / Campaign Finder" rhythm.
  • The Journey FinderWith "Personal Devotionals" as a permanent subhead rather than parenthetical.
  • My Journey FinderLeans personal/possessive, distinguishes it clearly from the group-facing tools.
  • Daily Journey FinderForegrounds the daily-devotional use case up front.
Ministry Experience LibraryPart V

The Business Model

All-Access vs. module upsells.

  • All-Access PassOne church subscription unlocks all 25 ministry libraries plus the full Campaign system. Every ministry leader and small group leader logs in under the church account and pulls what's unique to their ministry through the Finder.
  • Module UpsellsFamily Module and Youth/Children Module are sold and licensed separately from the 25 adult/life-stage ministries, since they carry their own companion editions and production cost.
  • Church-Level AccessAccess lives at the church level, not per-leader — consistent with how the Small Group Master Brochure and campaign subscriptions already work.
Ministry Experience LibraryPart VI

How Access Actually Cascades

A. Inside the church — role by role

RoleSeesCustomization RightsBuilder Access
Staff / Ministry Directors Full 6,200+ title library, all 25 ministries, all campaigns Fully Customized — title, teaching, stories, video, design Yes — Full Builder
Volunteer Ministry Leaders Their ministry's library only Lightly Customized — branding, leader letter, dates, local application Local Customization Builder only
Small Group Leaders Only the series assigned or selected for their group Ready to Use, or Lightly Customized if enabled No — they receive, they don't build
Congregation / Members Journey Finder — devotionals, family journals, personal studies Personal: name, family names, cover photo, dedication Personal Journal Builder only

The permission logic is simple: the further down the chain, the narrower the library and the lighter the customization — a small group leader isn't rewriting theology, a member isn't touching teaching notes, they're personalizing a cover and a name field.

B. Outside the church — business & nonprofit leaders

A real fork, worth deciding deliberately rather than defaulting into it.

  • Separate B2B LicensePurpose Driven Business (and a parallel nonprofit track) sells directly to companies and nonprofits, independent of any church relationship — its own pricing, its own sales motion, its own case studies (Movement Mortgage as the flagship).
  • Church-Sponsored BridgeA church with an active Marketplace & Workplace Ministry can gift or extend Purpose Driven Business access to business owners in the congregation as a relationship-building value-add — without merging the two licensing systems.

Recommendation: keep Purpose Driven Business and the nonprofit track as their own license, sold on their own terms — but build the bridge as an option, since it turns Marketplace Ministry into a warm lead source rather than asking a Small Groups Pastor and a company CEO through the same checkout.

C. The "Print One" model — fully customized, single unit

A member (or anyone, church-subscriber or not) builds one fully personalized journal or workbook through a simplified Canva-based Personal Journal Builder — name, dedication, cover photo, translation preference. Output is print-ready, ~$25 per unit, no subscription required — priced like a personalized gift, not a curriculum purchase. This gives three simultaneous revenue lines off the same content: church subscription, B2B licensing, and individual print-on-demand.

Launch & Lead Guide

The step-by-step for standing up this specific ministry: how to structure it, recruit a leader, set the first 90 days, avoid the most common reasons volunteer ministries stall or quietly die.

Leader Training & Development Course

"Is the person I'm about to hand this to actually equipped to lead it well?"

A short course (video + guide) that trains the volunteer or staff ministry leader — not just to teach content, but to shepherd people, facilitate hard conversations, and lead a group well.

"Will this still be healthy — and growing — a year from now?"

The sustaining wisdom: how to multiply groups, keep volunteer leaders from burning out, measure ministry health, and scale without losing quality.

"Does this reach people beyond the one hour a week they're in the room?"

"Do we have the operational plumbing to actually run this without reinventing it every time?"

Transferable, non-content assets: sign-up forms, communication and social templates, event checklists, budget templates — the reusable back-office layer behind every ministry.

Ministry Experience LibraryPart XI

Singles Ministry — Mission, Vision & Values

A case study in developing the framework beneath a ministry.

Mission

To help single adults live a whole, purposeful, Christ-centered life — not a waiting room, but a calling.

Vision

A church where singleness is a full season of kingdom purpose, not a problem to be solved — where single adults lead, serve, and grow with the same dignity and expectation as every other adult in the church.

Values
  • Wholeness Before Relationship — identity in Christ comes before relationship status.
  • Community Over Isolation — every single adult has a place to belong, not just a program to attend.
  • Purpose Over Waiting — singleness is a season to steward, not simply endure.
  • Honesty Over Performance — real conversations about dating, purity, and loneliness, without shame.
  • Every Season Welcome — programming serves the 20-something and the 50-something single adult alike.

Five Premier Experiences

Experience 01

Whole — Discovering Your Identity in Christ

Approach

Challenges the "half a person" narrative; grounds identity in Christ before relationship status.

Curriculum

6 sessions — Made Whole, The Comparison Trap, Loneliness Isn't Weakness, Contentment as Warfare, Serving from Wholeness, Living Sent as a Single Adult.

Journey Companion

21-day devotional ending in a written "wholeness declaration."

Experience 02

Worth the Wait — A Biblical Vision for Dating and Purity

Approach

An honest, non-shaming framework for dating and purity that addresses app-driven dating culture directly.

Curriculum

6 sessions — a biblical vision for sex and dating, boundaries, red flags, healthy dating rhythms, purity-culture wounds and grace, discerning a future spouse.

Journey Companion

30-day devotional/journal with a "values before dating" worksheet.

Experience 03

Not Alone — Community for the Single Season

Approach

Directly addresses isolation, positioning the group itself as the antidote.

Curriculum

6 sessions — group covenant, vulnerability, holiday loneliness, chosen family, hospitality practices.

Journey Companion

A 14-day devotional timed for high-loneliness seasons, usable as a stand-alone care resource.

Experience 04

Called and Complete — Purpose Beyond Relationship Status

Approach

Treats singleness as a season of undivided devotion (1 Corinthians 7) rather than a deficiency.

Curriculum

6 sessions — spiritual gifts discovery, calling, undivided devotion, serving from singleness, kingdom ambition.

Journey Companion

40-day "calling journey" devotional pairing Scripture with vocational discernment exercises.

Experience 05

Ready for Someday — Preparing Now for the Marriage to Come

Approach

Forward-looking, practical formation now rather than passive waiting.

Curriculum

6 sessions — character now shapes marriage later, financial readiness, emotional health, family-of-origin work, discerning godly character in a partner.

Journey Companion

21-day devotional built around journaling prompts a person could someday share with a future spouse.
