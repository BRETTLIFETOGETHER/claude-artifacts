The Big Idea

More people will walk through your doors this Easter than on any other Sunday of the year — many of them carrying quiet questions, old wounds, and a flicker of hope they can't quite name. Hope Rising meets them there.

It's a single, unforgettable Easter morning message, built to land with first-time guests and lifelong believers alike. And it's a five-week series designed to make sure the hope that rises on Sunday keeps rising long after the lilies are gone — following the empty tomb all the way home, into the places people actually live.

Don't let Easter end on Sunday.

The Easter Sunday Message

The Morning Everything Changed

Because the tomb is empty, hope is real — and it's still rising.

On the first Easter morning, a sealed tomb opened and the whole story of the world turned. Hope Rising preaches that moment as far more than history. It's the proof that no situation is finally hopeless, no story is finally over, and no person is finally too far gone.

It's written to do what the greatest Easter messages have always done — to stop people in their tracks, meet them in whatever they walked in carrying, and invite every person in the room to let hope rise again. And it ends not with a closing thought, but with an open door: a place to take the next step.

“He has given us new birth into a living hope.”

1 Peter 1:3 · NIV
