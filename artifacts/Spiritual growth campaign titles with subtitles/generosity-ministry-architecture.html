The Generosity Ministry
Architecture

One integrated ecosystem — the Generous Living flagship, the Financial Wisdom offerings, the multi-length experience system, five new campaign tracks, Family Legacy, LifeTogether Experiences, and the capital-campaign bridge — all under one roof.

The model. A church buys into a platform once — the Generous Living campaign kit and ministry system (the "razor") — then keeps deploying editions and experiences season after season: 40-day, 30-day, 21-day, and 7-day challenges; youth, children, and preschool editions; annual refreshes (the "blades"). The result is recurring value for the church and recurring revenue for the ministry.
