100 capital-campaign series across 8 arcs. A great campaign usually moves through several of these in sequence — open with Vision, name the Build, make the Sacrificial ask, frame it as Legacy and For the City, call the church All In with Faith to Believe, and close on the Moment. Any of these pairs cleanly with a Nehemiah "rebuilding" teaching track and a commitment-weekend finish.
