Why Generosity Ministry

Generosity is discipled, not assumed.

Most churches talk about giving once a year and hope for the best. A generous church is built differently — through consistent teaching that shapes how people see money, possessions, and the God who owns it all. Generosity Ministry gives you the full arc: a churchwide campaign to launch the vision, small-group curriculum to go deep, and classes to disciple people's finances toward freedom.

Two hundred fifty proven series, organized into seven tracks, so you never have to build a generosity series from scratch again.
