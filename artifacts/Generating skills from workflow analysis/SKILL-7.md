# IP Multiplier

## The premise

Strong content is the seed of a product family. One asset can become: 40-day devotional · six-session small group series · teaching videos · assessment · leader guide · sermon series · family edition · advisor edition · donor edition · children's edition · 10-day challenge · 21-day journey · online course · coaching program · church campaign · licensing package · keynote · podcast · social · email campaign.

## The discipline (this is what makes it a skill, not a list)

1. **Do the asked task first, completely.** Multiplication comes after, never instead of.
2. **Name the two or three extensions with the greatest strategic leverage — not every possible one.** A twenty-item list is brainstorming; three ranked moves is strategy.
3. Rank by Brett's filters, roughly in order: Kingdom impact, scalability, trusted relationships, **existing distribution**, repeatable economics, brand credibility, content leverage, church adoption potential, advisor/family integration, AI leverage, movement potential. A profitable but strategically isolated extension loses to one that compounds relationships and distribution.
4. **Ask who already owns the audience and trust** for each extension before proposing to build a channel from scratch. Distribution is part of product design.
5. Run the triage categories: **core / required / extensions / experiments / distractions / duplicates / platform opportunities.** Say plainly when a "new product" is really a feature, module, or audience edition of an existing platform (check against the wheel: Weekend / Week / Circle / Classroom / Team / Household, with the Campaign as the rim) rather than a separate brand.

## Movement vs. product check

For any extension pitched as more than a product, answer the movement questions: message · catalytic experience · champions · distribution system · repeatable model · economic engine · what produces testimonies · what creates multiplication · next-step pathway · platform · what makes it self-propagating. If most can't be answered, call it a product and don't dress it up.

## Sequencing output format

**Vision / Phase 1 / Next Action** — keep the whole vision visible, name the wedge inside it, and make the next action doable this week. Never shrink the vision into a small MVP; sequence it instead.

## Status honesty

Every mapped derivative is labeled concept until built. The map is a map, not a catalog of things that exist.
