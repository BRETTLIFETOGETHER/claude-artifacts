Why This Library

Every church needs a next season — a stretch of weeks where the weekend message, the group conversation, and the family table all point the same direction. That is what a churchwide campaign does. This library gives you nine, ready when you are.

For over 30 years, the five biblical purposes have helped shape thousands of churches and inspired more than 50 million readers around the world. LifeTogether builds on those timeless biblical foundations with a new generation of churchwide campaigns, devotionals, curriculum, classes, and discipleship experiences designed to help churches go deeper, wider, and further in every purpose.

The Legacy

LifeTogether founder Brett Eastman served on the original Purpose Driven ministry team and authored and co-developed many of the original Purpose Driven small-group resources published by Zondervan. Today, LifeTogether is helping churches apply those same biblical foundations through a new generation of customizable campaigns, curriculum, and discipleship tools for every age and stage of life.
