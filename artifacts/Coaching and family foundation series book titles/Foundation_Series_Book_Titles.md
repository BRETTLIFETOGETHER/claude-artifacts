## OPEN ITEMS

**1. Book 1 short name.** "Family Overview" does not work as a cover line, so the default above is **Family Legacy**. It is accurate, it matches the root of both curriculum titles, and it keeps the set of six reading cleanly. Two things to weigh. It sits close to the series name, and it takes the strongest two words in the whole system and spends them on the book people buy first rather than the one they finish with. The alternative worth considering is **Family Foundation**, which names the series and the book together, but that phrase already means a private philanthropic entity to every advisor and every estate attorney who sees it, so a legacy book called Family Foundation will be shelved and read as a book about foundations. Recommend Family Legacy.

**2. The two editions now share all six short names.** By design, since the short name is the topic and both editions teach the same topic. It works as long as the series lockup sits on every cover, so the reader sees Coaching Foundation Series or Family Foundation Series above Family Clarity. If the two ever sit on the same table or the same web grid without that lockup, they are indistinguishable. Simplest safeguard is an edition line under the short name on the coaching covers only: **Family Clarity, Coaching Edition**.

**3. Book 1 coaching subtitle.** The Overview script reads *Building a Multi-Generational Legacy That Lasts.* Your sheet reads *Guiding Families to Move from Legacy by Default to Legacy by Design.* The sheet version is shown throughout this document. Pick one and correct the other, because the script version will otherwise reach the prompter when Book 1 films.

**4. Book 4 coaching title line.** The Communication script's title line currently reads *Alignment in Action Leading Conversations That Matter.* That is Book 3's title welded onto the front of Book 4's, with no punctuation between them. The subtitle underneath is correct. This needs fixing in the script file itself, not just here.

**5. Book 4 family subtitle carries an em-dash.** *Why Families Don't Talk—and How to Start.* This is your sheet's version and it is reproduced verbatim above, but it conflicts with the no-em-dash rule enforced across all eighteen family products. Cleanest fix preserving the rhythm: *Why Families Don't Talk, and How to Start.*

**6. Book 3 subtitles do not describe Book 3.** Not a source conflict, an editorial one, and worth raising before covers are set. Both Alignment subtitles promise values, decisions, and direction. The six filmed sessions are financial statements, assets under management, mandatory documents, insurance coverage, liabilities and taxes, and yearly cash flow. That is the FAMILY acrostic, and it is the most technical material in either series. A family who buys *Aligning Your Values, Decisions, and Direction* opens the book and finds a balance sheet. The acrostic itself is the fix, since it is memorable and already on camera:

- Coaching: *Helping Families Align Their Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most*
- Family: *Aligning Your Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most*

**7. Book 3 script header.** The Alignment script header reads BOOK 3 - ALIGNMENT - FAMILY/BALTIC. Still unexplained.
