Core transformation framework

One pathway. Six stages. One instrument.

The narrative arc runs from ownership to stewardship, obedience, generosity, and legacy. The measurable instrument underneath it is the six-stage spine — the same framework that powers the assessment, the profiles, the forty-day map, and the curriculum.

How the other frameworks fit. The six stages are the spine. The twelve generosity modules live inside the Generous stage; the financial-wisdom H framework belongs to the Ron Blue lane; the family legacy dimensions govern the advisor door. They nest — they do not compete.
The assessment

Where has God already done something — and where is He still asking?

Answer honestly. This is aspirational, not condemning: it identifies what is already true of you and what is still being asked of you, and recommends a next step for each. Six to eight minutes.

Scale: 1 = not yet true of me · 5 = consistently true of me

Legacy families · The Living Pledge

  
Don't merely leave a legacy. Live it.

  
The Living Pledge is not a giving commitment. It is a commitment to intentionally live your faith, generosity, values, and legacy now — while the generations are still together. Giving stays the catalytic behavior; family flourishing is the larger promise.

  
How this travels: invitation-only, one family at a time. It is never announced from a church platform, and no answer given here is ever routed to a development officer.

Platform

  
More than content. A transformation engine.

  
The long-term platform is licensable by churches, advisor firms, family offices, foundations, and ministry partners — co-branded with the partners who own the trust.

  

    
1. Assess
Capture posture, practice, and aspiration across the six stages.

    
2. Personalize
Recommend the next best journey, conversation, or tool — strengths first.

    
3. Deliver
Deploy devotionals, sessions, exercises, and family tools.

    
4. Facilitate
Guide groups, families, pastors, and advisors through real conversations.

    
5. Track
Measure progress without reducing formation to metrics.

    
6. Connect
Link people to churches, advisors, coaches, and next steps.

  

  
The moat is not the library — anyone can build a library. The moat is the profile: what the system knows about a person and a family before it produces anything. Answer one question on one day and the next day is chosen a little better.







  
Pricing · church licensing

  
Priced by attendance. Never by seat.

  
Volunteers rotate every term — per-seat pricing turns every new host into a purchase order and makes churches ration access. So: unlimited seats at every tier, attendance is the only variable, one invoice, one renewal date. Diagnostics stay free forever.
