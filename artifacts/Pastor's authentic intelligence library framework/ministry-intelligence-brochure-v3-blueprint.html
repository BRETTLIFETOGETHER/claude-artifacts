Before We Design Anything

The As-Built Survey

No architect drafts new plans without first surveying what's already standing. Before we design your first campaign, we survey your entire preaching library.

Somewhere in your files sits thirty years of load-bearing material — sermons, series, stories, and outlines nobody has measured in years.

The Pastor's Library Project is your as-built survey. We gather every message you've ever delivered — pulpit series, Vision Sundays, membership talks, staff devotionals, even outlines that never left a notebook — and document it into one permanent, searchable record.

Every entry is measured against theme, audience, anchor passage, and season, so what took decades to preach becomes something your team can locate in seconds. No future campaign starts from a blank sheet again — it starts from a site that's already been surveyed.

  • Full transcription and cataloging of your preaching archive
  • Thematic and Scriptural indexing across every series
  • A searchable, permanent digital record
  • A master title directory, cross-referenced and deduplicated
  • Verified material, ready for the next set of drawings
