The One-Year Retainer gives you a quarterly rhythm — one campaign built each season. But some churches don't need seasonal projects. They need ongoing hands, every week, on everything that comes up. That's the gap the Team Retainer fills.

Project Cadence

One-Year Retainer

One campaign or curriculum built each quarter. Best when your need is predictable and seasonal.

Staffing Cadence

Team Retainer

A dedicated team embedded in your weekly rhythm. Best when your need is constant and varied.
