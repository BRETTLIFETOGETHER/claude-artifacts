CAN Engines™

The technology and product architecture behind the Christian Advisor Network

For the CEO, the board, and the product and technology leadership deciding what actually gets built — and in what order.

CAN should not be built as 125 unrelated tools or 225 disconnected journeys.

It should be built as a small number of powerful intelligence engines — engines that generate many different outputs depending on the advisor, the family, the firm, the situation, the relationship stage, and the next stewardship need.

Seven engines form the core operating system. Each answers one fundamental question. Together they form a single continuous loop, and every turn of that loop makes the next one better.

Three

One Family Through Seven Engines

A worked example. Parents 67 and 65, three adult children, a family business, a donor-advised fund, strong faith, no family mission, no family meeting in three years.

  • 1Family IntelligenceCAN discovers the structure — parents, three children, business, DAF, faith orientation, and a stated concern about entitlement. It does not merely store this. It interprets it: high urgency on next-generation preparation; moderate risk in the relationship with the daughter; generosity as a low-conflict entry point; no shared family mission; one child has never met the advisor.
  • 2PrescriptionCAN recommends Wisdom Before Wealth™ — because heir preparation is the family’s own highest-rated concern, and because it is actionable now.
  • 3PersonalizationCAN produces the Anderson family’s own edition of that journey, in their faith language, naming their children, presented by their advisor and their firm.
  • 4Advisor IntelligenceThe advisor receives five prioritized questions, two sensitivities to avoid, one relationship gap to close, and three possible next steps.
  • 5Family ExperienceThe family completes the journey and holds a facilitated family meeting.
  • 6Relationship IntelligenceCAN records that all three children are now engaged. The Relationship Depth Score improves.
  • 7Enterprise IntelligenceThe CEO sees one additional high-value household that now has G2 coverage.

That is the complete loop — and it began with a conversation, not a campaign.

Six

What to Build First

The seven engines do not need to launch simultaneously.

Phase One — a family-facing product

Know, prescribe, personalize, engage

  • Family Intelligence — profiles, assessments, relationship map
  • Stewardship Prescription — basic journey recommendation
  • Personalization — personalized journey and meeting guide
  • Family Experience — 30-day journey plus family meeting

That is enough for the first family-facing product.

Phase Two — an advisor product

Prepare, remember, measure

  • Advisor Intelligence — meeting prep, questions, follow-up, relationship reminders
  • Relationship Intelligence — Relationship Depth Score, G2 and G3 tracking, risk

Now CAN becomes materially more valuable to advisors.

Phase Three — an enterprise platform

Scale culture, capability, and relationship equity

  • Enterprise Intelligence — culture, advisor capability, client experience
  • Relationship-retention dashboards and M&A integration

Now CAN is an enterprise RIA platform.

The explanation

“First, CAN helps your advisors understand the whole family, not merely the portfolio. Second, it identifies the family’s most important next stewardship conversation. Third, it creates personalized resources for that specific family. Fourth, it prepares the advisor to lead the conversation well. Fifth, it turns those resources into family experiences and action. Sixth, it measures whether trust is spreading across generations. And seventh, it gives leadership visibility into whether that model is actually scaling across the firm.”

That is what makes CAN more than content.
