Part Two

The M&A Relationship Integration System™

A firm acquires a $600 million practice. The transaction closes. Then the real question arrives.

Did you buy the relationships — or just the revenue stream?

Integration begins immediately, on a defined six-phase sequence, while trust in the selling founder is still the strongest asset in the book.

Phase One — First 30 days

Preserve trust

Every acquired advisor completes the 30-Day Advisor Stewardship Journey™: our story, our culture, our client promise, family stewardship, family relationships, generosity, next-generation engagement, AI and human relationship, family meeting facilitation, transferable trust.

Every senior advisor also completes a Client Relationship Inventory™ — top fifty families, founding-client relationships, single-advisor dependencies, vulnerable spouses, unknown heirs, important outside professionals, and the emotional relationships tied to the selling founder.

Phase Two

Map the families — know what you bought

Every strategic household is tagged: Generation 1, 2, and 3, spouses, business successors, trustees, primary and secondary advisor, estate attorney, CPA, family office, and current relationship stage.

The relationship stages

  • 01Unknowna name on the account
  • 02Identifiedwe know who they are
  • 03Introducedwe have met
  • 04Connecteda working relationship exists
  • 05Engagedthey participate
  • 06Trustedthey call us first
Phase Three

Assess relationship risk

CAN generates a Relationship Retention Risk Score™ from AUM, client age, advisor age, relationship concentration, spouse relationship, next-generation relationship, family meeting history, succession preparedness, acquisition anxiety, client tenure, and number of firm relationships.

Highest-risk profile: high AUM + aging client + single advisor + unknown heirs. Those families get immediate attention.

Phase Four

Engage top families

Every priority family receives a Family Stewardship Assessment, a personalized welcome, one recommended journey, a family meeting invitation, and a next-generation introduction opportunity.

Phase Five

Build multiple relationships

The acquired relationship moves from founder ↔ client to firm ↔ family — introducing a secondary advisor, the service team, a next-generation advisor, specialists, and enterprise leadership where appropriate. The objective is relationship diversification.

Phase Six

Management dashboard

Leadership sees the Relationship Retention Dashboard™: top families, AUM exposed to transition, households dependent on one advisor, spouses not engaged, adult children not engaged, G2 and G3 relationship percentages, family meetings and assessments completed, journeys active, high-risk families, advisor succession exposure.

Instead of asking only how much acquired AUM remains, ask how much acquired relationship equity you have actually retained and expanded.

Part Three

AI Without Commoditizing the Advisor™

The client should think “my advisor understands us incredibly well” — not “my advisor sends me impressive AI output.”

AI backstage. Human relationship frontstage.

Automate

  • Meeting transcription and summaries
  • Research organization and resource retrieval
  • Administrative follow-up drafts and task creation
  • Document preparation and data organization
  • Internal meeting preparation

Augment

  • Family pattern recognition
  • Conversation preparation and question generation
  • Personalized resources and scenario analysis
  • Family meeting agendas
  • Next-best-journey recommendation and relationship reminders

Never fully delegate

  • Emotional conversations and family conflict
  • Spiritual discernment and pastoral care
  • Death and incapacity conversations
  • Sensitive succession and major family decisions
  • Reconciliation, trust-building, and final professional judgment

If the value comes from empathy, trust, discernment, or presence, keep the human in front.

The core rule

The Three-Language Personalization Model™

The underlying architecture never changes. The expression does.

  • OneFaith-forward — What has God entrusted to your family?
  • TwoFaith-friendly — How do your faith and values influence your family’s decisions?
  • ThreeValues-based — What values do you want your family’s resources to reinforce?

The Human Review Gate™

Nothing reaches a family until the advisor has answered eight questions.

  • Is it accurate?
  • Does it sound like this family?
  • Is the faith language appropriate?
  • Does it reveal anything one spouse told us privately?
  • Could any statement create unnecessary conflict?
  • Do I need to deliver this personally?
  • Is technical advice appropriately reviewed?
  • Have I added a human note?

AI creates capacity. Relationship creates value.

Remember more · Notice more · Prepare more · Personalize more · Administrate less

Part Seven

The Advisor Value Story™

Advisors need language for explaining what is different. Five versions, one message.

Thirty seconds

“We certainly help manage investments and financial planning, but our work goes further. We help families think through the relationships, values, generosity, next-generation preparation, succession, and legacy connected to their wealth. Our goal is not simply to help you manage money well, but to help your family steward it wisely across generations.”

Sixty seconds

“Traditional wealth management often focuses on the assets. We believe the family around those assets matters just as much. That means understanding your spouse, your children, your values, what you want wealth to accomplish, how you think about generosity, and what needs to happen before leadership and assets eventually transfer. We coordinate with your other professionals and help create the conversations and experiences that prepare your family. We call that Family Stewardship.”

Faith-forward

“We believe financial success comes with stewardship responsibility. Our goal is to help families prayerfully consider everything God has entrusted to them — their wealth, relationships, influence, generosity, and legacy — and then make wise decisions that serve both this generation and those that follow.”

Next generation

“We’re not here simply because we work with your parents. We’d like to understand you — your goals, questions, values, and what responsibility may eventually look like for you. You don’t have to become your parents. Our role is to help you become a wise steward of whatever is eventually entrusted to you.”

Acquisition

“You may have worked with your advisor for many years, and we respect the trust that has already been built. Our objective isn’t to replace that relationship. It’s to strengthen it by bringing additional resources, people, and family capabilities around you while preserving the things you value most.”
