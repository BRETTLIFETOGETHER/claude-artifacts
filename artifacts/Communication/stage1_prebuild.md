Terminology note carried forward from the guide build: source material is described as in the scripts, imported from source, or recommended.

### The finding that matters most

Sixty phrases were required, thirty titles and thirty subtitles, every one of them Tom's own words rather than a description of his ideas. All sixty were found in the scripts. Not one gap, and not one title written by me.

That is a better result than the material looked like it would give. The reason is that Tom repeats himself in a specific way. He states a principle, Brett reflects it back, and Tom restates it more sharply the second time. The sharper restatement is almost always the usable phrase, and there are five or six of them per session.

### Phrases excluded because they are Brett's

The master prompt is explicit that Brett's language is not Tom's, and several of the best lines in the scripts are Brett's rather than Tom's. These were all rejected as title candidates despite being strong: so you're creating space instead of filling it, the breakthrough didn't come from a better plan it came from a better conversation, so the issue wasn't disagreement it was lack of clarity, so people are operating with different versions of the same story, so structure actually creates freedom not restriction, so this is about moving from a moment to a pattern, and the quality of the conversation often determines the quality of the outcome.

That last one is the single best sentence in the entire Communication corpus and it is not usable in a devotional written in Tom's voice. Flagging it because it is the kind of line that gets absorbed by accident.

### Story inventory and curriculum overlap

Six stories exist, one per session, and all six are already used as the Opening Story of the corresponding Participant Guide session. Every use in this devotional is therefore an overlap, flagged in place and listed in the Stage 8 report. Default call: use them anyway, once each, on Days 3, 9, 14, 19, 24, and 27, told from the same angle Tom tells them rather than re-angled as they were in the guide. An advisor reading both will meet each story twice, six weeks apart, which is repetition rather than contradiction. The alternative is a devotional with no stories in it.

No story is used twice within the devotional.
