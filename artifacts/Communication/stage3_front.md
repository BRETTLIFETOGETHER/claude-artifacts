# How to Use This

Five readings a week, Monday through Friday, for six weeks.

Each week matches one session of the Communication module. Read the week that matches the session you are working through, and the two will reinforce each other. If you are reading this on its own, without the module, it still works. Nothing here depends on having watched anything.

The weekends are open on purpose. If you miss a Tuesday, you have Saturday. I built the gap in because I know what your week looks like, and because a reading plan you abandon in week two is worse than no reading plan.

Each one takes a few minutes. There is a body to read, four questions to answer, an exercise to write out, one question to carry into a client conversation that week, and a short private section at the end.

Write the answers. Do not think them.

I want to be direct about why. Thinking an answer feels like answering and is not the same thing. When you write it down you find out whether you actually have an answer or only the impression of one, and in this material the difference matters. The section called The Bucket at the end of each reading is the one most likely to get skipped, and it is the one that does the most work. Nobody is going to read it. That is the point of it.

Some of these will land on a specific client the moment you read them. When that happens, write the name down in the margin. By the end of six weeks you will have a list, and the list is the real output of this book.
