The Adult Bible Fellowship Library

Where Sunday Morning Becomes a Daily Journey

Churches organize adult community around life stage almost as much as they organize it around Bible books. This library serves both — a Felt Need Track that draws people in, and a Biblical Formation Track that builds mature disciples — stitched into one class experience.

124+ formation titles across 7 book studies 44+ felt-need topics, general & age-specific 6 life-stage affinity groups
I.

Two Tracks, One Class

Every course in the library runs 8–12 weeks and is built the same way: a Master Teacher, teacher lecture notes, a participant workbook, a discussion guide, a daily reading plan, and a 30-day companion. What changes is which door the class walks through first.

Draws Them In

Felt Need Track

Practical, life-stage topics tied to something a person is already carrying — a decision, a relationship, a season of anxiety or transition. This is what gets people to sign up.

Takes Them Deeper

Biblical Formation Track

Rooted in Scripture — a book of the Bible, a biblical character, or a core doctrine, studied verse by verse. This is what actually produces transformation.

Every course, every group: 8–12 weeks · Master Teacher · Lecture Notes · Participant Workbook · Discussion Guide · Daily Reading Plan · 30-Day Companion

V.

From Sunday to Every Day

Every 8–12 week course can ship at one of three levels. The recommendation: make the middle tier the default across the whole library.

Level One

Course Only

Teacher lecture notes, participant workbook, and discussion guide for the 8–12 week class.

Level Two — Default

Course + 30-Day Companion

Everything in Level One, plus a daily reading plan that carries the class into the other six days of the week.

Level Three

Complete Class Experience

Full course, daily companion, and connected care or mentoring resources for pastorally sensitive topics.

"What begins in class on Sunday continues in every member's life all week long."
