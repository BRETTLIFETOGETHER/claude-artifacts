## 2. Decision list

Twenty items need a call from you. Page numbers are the printed PDF. Recommendations are mine; the reasoning line says why.

### Take Allen's wording (port into the current file)

**p6 — Ron's welcome letter.** Both files are wrong, differently. Allen reads `carry, :` and the current file reads `carry :`. Someone removed the comma and left the space.
> **Fix to:** `It answers the question so many of us carry: "Will I be okay?"`

**p109 — Small Group Agreement, Encouragement for Growth.** The current file reads "to help each other **make** one faithful next step." You take a step; you don't make one. Allen has "take." The same line is also missing a comma.
> **Fix to:** `To give encouragement, not just receive it. We want to grow, and to help each other take one faithful next step.`

**p125 — Leading for the First Time.** Two problems in one bullet. A double period, and a padded clause.
> **Current:** `You are not the teacher, and you don't have to be a financial expert.. The video carries the teaching, this guide carries the questions, and a host who admits they haven't figured out everything about money either will earn more trust...`
> **Fix to:** `You are not the teacher, and you don't have to be a financial expert. The video carries the teaching, this guide carries the questions, and a host who admits they haven't figured money out either will earn more trust...`

**p128 — Leadership Training 101, first bullet.** Missing space, plus a sentence fragment that the current file introduced.
> **Current:** `God is with you, and so is your church. And hopefully a co-leader as well.You're not carrying this by yourself...`
> **Fix to:** `God is with you, and so is your church, and hopefully a co-leader as well. You're not carrying this by yourself...`

**p128 — Leadership Training 101, "Curriculum drives the group."** The current file uses "curriculum" twice in adjacent sentences. Allen's "each session" avoids the echo.
> **Fix to:** `Curriculum drives the group, not you. You don't have to carry the session. Trust the flow of each session, the questions, the exercise, the story.`

**p60 — Session 4 introduction.** The strongest editorial argument in the set, and the base file backs Allen. The current rewrite flattens three lines. "Whisper in the dark" became "whisper just to themselves." "Gives it two" became "gives two answers." Most costly, the closing antithesis collapsed: Allen's *confidence anchored in the Provider holds when the provision doesn't* turns on itself; the current *holds when the provision feels fragile* repeats "fragile" from the previous sentence and loses the turn.
> **Fix to:** `It's the question people whisper in the dark, and Ron says it's the one he hears more than any other. Will I have enough? Will I outlive my money? Will my family be all right? It's a legitimate question, and it deserves a real answer rather than a spiritual dismissal. This week gives it two. The first is practical: there are habits that genuinely build financial strength over a lifetime, and they work. The second is deeper: confidence that depends on a number will always be fragile, because the number can move. Confidence anchored in the Provider holds when the provision doesn't.`

### Neither version is right

**p19 — Grow Together discussion question.** Allen has "most **absent from** how you handle money"; the current file has "most **challenging for**." Allen's creates more tension, which is the standard, but "absent from how you handle money" is awkward. "Challenging for" is smooth and toothless.
> **Fix to:** `Which of these four practices is most missing from how you handle money right now?`

**p36 — Lifestyle Finish Line exercise.** Two issues. The current file changed "Not what you fear" to "Not what you want," which is the better contrast for a ceiling exercise. But the line is a triplet stack: *Not what you have. Not what you want. Just enough.*
> **Fix to:** `For each area, write one sentence describing what enough could look like for you. Not what you have, and not what you want. Just enough.`

**p109 — Safe Environment.** The current file put scare quotes around a word that isn't quoting anything, and swapped "tender" for "sensitive" while p9 still says "a subject as tender as money."
> **Fix to:** `To help create a place where people can be heard and feel loved and supported, especially on a subject as tender as money. No quick fixes, no snap judgments, no unsolicited advice.`

**p109 — No Numbers.** This is the one worth your attention. Allen carries the established campaign line, "Dignity matters more than disclosure." The current file replaced it in the Agreement with "You and God know your numbers and that's what counts" — while Session 1 on p14 still carries **both**, and phrases the new line differently: "that's **enough**." So the same sentence appears in two places in two forms, and the campaign's signature phrase now survives only in Session 1.
> **Fix to (p109):** `To never ask each other what we make, owe, give, or have saved. Dignity matters more than disclosure. You and God know your numbers, and that's enough.`
> Then make p14 match word for word.

**p120 — About the Authors.** "Ron Blue has spent **over four decades**." Two problems: it violates the forty-five-year standard, and it contradicts p6, where Ron says "more than forty-five years" in his own voice, fourteen pages earlier in the same book.
> **Fix to:** `Ron Blue has spent more than forty-five years equipping Christians with biblical financial wisdom...`

## 6. Independent audit of the current file

**This is an InDesign error, not a manuscript error.** The DOCX Contents page is correct, and so are the body dividers (p27 Contentment, p43 Stewardship), the Small Group Calendar, and the Memory Verse Cards. Only the laid-out Contents page is wrong. It goes to the designer, not to Hannah — and it inverts your canonical order on the first content page a reader opens.

**Live placeholders in the source.** `[final bio and credits to be confirmed]` at the end of About the Authors, and `[Organizational description to be confirmed: founding and history, mission statement, key programs and audiences served, and website and contact information.]` at p121.
