Where this goes next

The future of Small Group ministry

Pulled from the same forecasting that shapes LifeTogether's preaching calendar — the shifts already visible in where pastors, and now small groups, are headed through 2029.

Rising Topic

Faith in the Age of AI

The category pastors are least ready for. Small groups become where a church actually processes AI together, not just hears a sermon about it — The Prompt Series is LifeTogether's first entry here.

Rising Topic

Community over content

As content multiplies and loneliness deepens anyway, the group itself — not the material — becomes the draw. Curriculum quality matters less than whether people keep showing up for each other.

Format Shift

Curious-to-committed on-ramps

Short formats like the 7-Day Challenge become the front door before someone joins a full 40-day group — meeting rising spiritual curiosity where it actually starts.

Platform Shift

Custom, not just canned

Churches increasingly expect to shape curriculum around their own congregation, not just consume what's handed to them — the whole reason the Builder exists alongside the Finder.

Structural Shift

One theme, every age, next door

Kids, teens, and adults running the same season in parallel rooms — wonder, honesty, and stewardship as one connected arc instead of three disconnected ministries.

Market Shift

Beyond the church walls

The same small-group architecture, licensed into the workplace — Purpose Driven Business proving the discipleship model translates to teams that never set foot in a sanctuary.
