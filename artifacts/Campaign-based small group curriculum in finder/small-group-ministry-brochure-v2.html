How a series is born

From Sunday to the living room

Nothing in the Small Group library starts from a blank page. Every series has already been preached, written, or piloted at churchwide scale — the small group edition is just its next form.

01

A source series

A 40-day campaign, a named curriculum, or a corporate training arc — the churchwide or workplace version already built.

02

Six-session distillation

Condensed for a living room instead of a stage: one big idea, key and supporting scripture, and discussion questions per session.

03

Teaching notes, always

Every session ships with a full leader script, so any capable leader can teach it, with or without video.

No video required
04

Two doors

The finished series lists in the Finder as a ready-to-run study, and enters the Builder as a template a church can reshape into their own.

Two doors, one library

Finder or Builder — same shelf, different reach

Most churches want curriculum handled. A smaller, more capable group wants to shape it. Both are served from the same source material, so nothing built in either door ever drifts from what LifeTogether has already vetted.

The Finder

Browse. Pick. Meet tonight.

Curriculum, not a project.

Every campaign, discipleship theme, and preaching arc in the library — browsable by topic, length, and audience — arrives complete: six sessions, teaching notes, a discussion guide, and a verified scripture arc.

Best for: the small group pastor who wants the next six weeks handled, not built.
The Builder

Start from a series. Make it yours.

Their fingerprint, our foundation.

Pick any series in the library as a starting template, then reorder sessions, re-weight the scripture emphasis, or blend two themes into one arc — without ever leaving the library.

Best for: curriculum-savvy staff shaping a custom season for their own congregation.
