Names Never Leave This Device
Here is exactly how this works. The engine writes the study using placeholders — SPOUSE, KID1, M1 — and never sees a single real name. Your browser swaps the real names in afterward, locally. Nothing with your wife's name or your group roster is ever sent to any server, ours or anyone else's. That is not a promise about policy. It is how it is built.
