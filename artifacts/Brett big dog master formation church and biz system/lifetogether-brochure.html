What Every Title Does

1

Names the Felt Need

The title names the exact thing the specific audience is already carrying — before the first page is turned, the person feels seen.

2

Builds 40-Day Conviction

The campaign arc answers the felt need theologically and practically across 40 days — building the conviction that the ministry being launched is essential.

3

Generates Stories

Every daily step produces a story. Celebration Sunday is the moment the stories prove that what the campaign promised is actually available in this congregation.

4

Launches the Ministry

The ministry invitation on Celebration Sunday is the conclusion the entire campaign has been building toward — natural, inevitable, and fully prepared.

I Ministry Category One

Family Legacy Ministry —
Ultra-High-Net-Worth Families

These titles must do what no estate attorney, financial advisor, or wealth manager can do — speak to the soul of a successful family and offer what no balance sheet can measure. Titles ranked by their power to open doors that have never been opened.

Ministry Launch Goal

A family legacy cohort — high-capacity families meeting quarterly around values, calling, and generational faithfulness.

40 Days of Family Legacy Flagship Title · Strongest for Family Legacy by Design

The foundational title. Clear, aspirational, and serious enough to command the attention of a high-capacity family that is already thinking about what they will leave behind. Works as the flagship campaign built on Tom Conway's Family Legacy By Design curriculum. Speaks to the family that has built something significant and now wants to ensure the right things survive them.

2 40 Days of What Matters Most Midlife Inflection · Legacy Awakening

Cuts through every accomplishment a successful family has accumulated and asks the one question they have been postponing. Speaks directly to the midlife inflection point that drives high-capacity families toward legacy work for the first time.

3 40 Days of Lasting Wealth High Net Worth · Values Transfer

Reframes wealth as something that includes — but extends far beyond — financial assets. Values. Faith. Character. The things that compound across generations. No wealth manager talks about this kind.

4 40 Days of The Second Half Bob Buford · Halftime Framework

Built on the Halftime language that has resonated with high-capacity leaders for three decades. Speaks directly to the person who has won the first half of life by every external measure and is now asking what the second half is actually for.

5 40 Days of Generational Faithfulness Psalm 78 · Deuteronomy 6

The most theologically grounded title — rooted in the biblical theology of passing faith from one generation to the next. Appeals to families who want to transfer conviction, character, and covenant — not just wealth.

6 40 Days of The Wealthy Soul Net Worth vs Soul Worth

The most provocative title — built on the tension between net worth and soul worth. Speaks to the high-capacity family that has everything and privately wonders why it does not feel like enough.

7 40 Days of What We Leave Behind Parents · Grandparents · Founders

Emotionally resonant for every parent of adult children and every grandparent of young grandchildren. Honest, universal, and impossible to ignore.

8 40 Days of Family on Purpose Intentional · Strategic · Founders

Appeals to business owners and founders who apply strategic thinking to every domain of their professional life and want to apply it to their family for the first time.

9 40 Days of The Blessed Life Dual Meaning · Spiritually Convicting

Draws on the dual meaning of blessed — financially prosperous and spiritually grounded — and invites high-capacity families to examine which version they are actually building.

10 40 Days of Enough Most Countercultural · Deepest Work

The most countercultural title for an ultra-high-net-worth audience. Changes the conversation from accumulation to distribution, from achievement to faithfulness.

II Ministry Category Two

Small Groups Ministry —
Launching and Multiplying Groups

Every title creates the felt need for community, makes the small group invitation the natural next step, and gives the pastor a sermon arc that builds toward a groups launch on Celebration Sunday.

Ministry Launch Goal

50–100% increase in small group participation by Celebration Sunday — and every new group launched from existing spiritual partnerships.

40 Days of Life Together Strongest Title · Lifetogether Identity · Bonhoeffer + Acts 2

The strongest title in the catalog for a groups launch. Simultaneously the name of the Lifetogether organization, a biblical concept from Acts 2, and the exact phrase Dietrich Bonhoeffer used for covenant Christian community. The small group invitation on Celebration Sunday writes itself: "What we just experienced for 40 days — that is what a small group gives you every week."

2 40 Days of Community Direct · Felt-Need · Universal

Direct, clear, and felt-need driven. The arc moves from isolation to belonging to covenant community. The group sign-up table at Celebration Sunday is the answer the campaign spent 40 days building toward.

3 40 Days of Belonging Most Emotionally Powerful · Visceral

Where "community" is intellectual, "belonging" is visceral. Every person who has ever sat in a church service surrounded by people and still felt alone will feel this title before they open the book.

4 40 Days of Together One Word · Maximum Versatility

Deceptively simple and extraordinarily versatile. One word. Every person knows what it means and every person knows they want more of it. The most adaptable groups-launch title in the library.

5 40 Days of One Another Theologically Precise · 59 NT Commands

Every day teaches one one-another command and shows exactly why a small group is the only context where that command can be consistently practiced.

6 40 Days of Connection Most Culturally Accessible

Uses the culture's own language for what community is supposed to provide — then shows immediately why digital connection fails and covenant community succeeds.

7 40 Days of Never Alone Most Evangelistic · Widest Reach

Loneliness is the most universally felt experience in the culture right now, and "never alone" is the promise that the church uniquely makes and uniquely keeps. Reaches inside and outside the congregation simultaneously.

III Ministry Category Three

Stewardship Ministry —
Beyond the Offering Plate

These titles reframe stewardship from a financial transaction into a whole-life posture — making the conversation bigger, more personal, and more spiritually compelling than any traditional giving campaign.

Ministry Launch Goal

A year-round stewardship culture — giving, serving, time, and whole-life generosity — not just an annual financial campaign.

40 Days of Open Hands Most Pastoral · Clearest Image

The most visually and emotionally clear title — the open hand as the physical posture of stewardship. Everything comes from God, everything passes through open hands, everything belongs to the One who gave it. The most pastoral stewardship campaign in the library.

2 40 Days of Enough Most Transformational · Ron Blue Framework

Reframes the entire conversation from giving more to living from genuine sufficiency and grateful generosity. Addresses the anxiety underneath every financial decision more honestly than any giving campaign in the market.

3 40 Days of The Generous Life Ron Blue · Whole-Life Stewardship

Stewardship as a whole-life posture rather than a financial transaction. Positions the church as a guide for whole-life financial wisdom rather than merely a stewardship drive.

4 40 Days of Faithful with Much Parable of Talents · High Capacity

Drawn from the parable of the talents — speaks to the high-capacity donor and the everyday family simultaneously. Reframes stewardship as faithful deployment of everything entrusted.

5 40 Days of Kingdom Finances Most Theologically Comprehensive

Places every financial decision inside the larger frame of kingdom citizenship. Speaks to the person ready to manage their finances as a kingdom steward rather than a personal consumer.

6 40 Days of More Than Money Time · Talent · Influence · Gifts

Opens the stewardship conversation to time, talent, influence, relationships, and spiritual gifts. Most effective for congregations that resist traditional stewardship campaigns.

7 40 Days of First Matthew 6:33 · Priority Question

Makes stewardship a priority question rather than a percentage question. What comes first in your budget, your calendar, your decision-making? The answer is where the stewardship conversation actually begins.

IV Ministry Category Four

Mental Health Ministry —
The Conversation the Church Must Have

These titles reduce stigma, invite honest self-identification, and position the church as the most trustworthy place in the community to address what mental health culture is reaching for but cannot fully provide.

Ministry Launch Goal

A Stephen Ministry or mental health cohort — trained lay care teams providing pastoral support for every person the campaign surfaced.

40 Days of Whole Most Aspirational · Zero Stigma

Names the destination rather than the diagnosis. Every person knows they are not fully whole and wants to move toward wholeness. Accessible to everyone from mild anxiety to serious depression without requiring anyone to self-identify publicly.

2 40 Days of The Weary Soul Matthew 11:28 · Most Pastorally Warm

"Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened." No stigma. No diagnosis. The universal acknowledgment that everyone is carrying something — and Jesus has a specific offer for those tired of carrying it alone.

3 40 Days of Honest Most Courageous · Permission to Name It

Gives people permission to name what is actually true — which is the first step toward healing. The greatest barrier to mental health in the church is the performance of wellness when genuine suffering is happening underneath.

4 40 Days of Not Alone Isolation · Community as Cure

Directly addresses the most dangerous dimension of mental illness — isolation — and makes the church's covenant community the answer to it.

5 40 Days of Strength in Weakness 2 Corinthians 12 · Faith + Struggle

Gives permission to be struggling and faithful simultaneously. Reframes weakness as the space where God's grace is most visible.

6 40 Days of Peace That Passes Understanding Philippians 4 · Anxiety Addressed

Draws directly from the most cited Scripture in the mental health conversation. Points to a peace that transcends what the culture offers and found insufficient.

7 40 Days of Healing Broadest Reach · Every Person

Healing is simultaneously physical, emotional, relational, and spiritual. Every person in every congregation is waiting for healing in at least one dimension.

V Ministry Category Five

Fitness, Health & Wellness Ministry —
The Honored Body

These titles frame physical stewardship as spiritual formation — making every fitness goal an act of worship and every wellness habit an expression of faithful discipleship rather than vanity or performance.

Ministry Launch Goal

Ongoing wellness small groups, accountability partnerships, and a whole-life health culture that sustains formation beyond the campaign season.

40 Days of Whole Life Health Flagship · Brett Eastman's Framework

Frames health as integrated — body, soul, mind, and spirit — rather than merely physical. Built on Brett Eastman's Whole Life Health framework. The most comprehensive and theologically grounded wellness title in the library. This is the title that makes the campaign more than a fitness challenge.

2 40 Days of Temples 1 Corinthians 6 · Body as Worship

Makes physical stewardship a spiritual discipline rather than a vanity pursuit. Built on 1 Corinthians 6:19–20 — your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit.

3 40 Days of Flourishing John 10:10 · Full-Life Thriving

The most aspirational wellness title — flourishing is the biblical word for full human thriving across every dimension. Broad enough to encompass nutrition, fitness, sleep, mental wellness, and spiritual formation simultaneously.

4 40 Days of Strong Most Action-Oriented · Every Age

Every person in every age and stage of life has a version of strong they are working toward. Creates immediate personal relevance without prescribing a specific fitness goal.

5 40 Days of Running Well Hebrews 12:1 · Endurance + Formation

Built on "run with perseverance the race marked out for us." Creates the natural bridge between physical training and spiritual formation.

6 40 Days of The Honored Body Most Theologically Specific

Particularly effective for congregations where body image, eating disorders, or physical neglect are pastoral concerns that have never been addressed from the platform.

7 40 Days of Starting Over Most Grace-Filled · Removes Shame

This title removes the shame of starting over and extends the invitation to begin again — which is also the deepest theological invitation the Gospel makes.

VI Ministry Category Six

Discipleship Ministry —
Making Following Jesus Daily

These titles make discipleship concrete, daily, and relational — not a class to take or a program to join, but the daily practice of becoming the person Jesus is forming you to be.

Ministry Launch Goal

A sustained discipleship pathway — one-on-one mentoring partnerships, formation cohorts, and a daily practice culture that outlasts the campaign.

40 Days of Following Jesus Most Foundational · Every Other Title is a Subset

The most foundational discipleship title — because discipleship is not a program, a class, or a curriculum. It is the daily practice of following a specific person in a specific direction. Every other discipleship title in this list is a subset of this one.

2 40 Days of Becoming Formation-Focused · Who You Are Becoming

Built on the theological reality that discipleship is not primarily about what you know or do but about who you are becoming. Speaks to the person who has been in church for years and still does not feel like they are growing.

3 40 Days of Daily Most Practical · Habit Formation

The crisis of discipleship in the modern church is not insufficient Sunday teaching but insufficient daily practice. This campaign makes the daily devotional habit the entry point into a formation culture.

4 40 Days of Closer Most Relational · Proximity to Jesus

Built on the simple conviction that the goal of discipleship is proximity to Jesus. Makes discipleship feel like an invitation rather than an obligation.

5 40 Days of Formation Most Theologically Sophisticated

Speaks to the pastor, the spiritual director, and the serious student of faith who understands that Christian maturity is formed over time through intentional practice rather than accumulated information.

6 40 Days of The Disciple's Life Most Comprehensive · Full Portrait

Shows what a fully formed disciple actually looks like across every dimension of daily life — prayer, Scripture, community, service, generosity, mission, and worship.

7 40 Days of One Step Further Most Accessible · Meets Every Person

Meets every person exactly where they are and asks only one thing: take one step further than where you currently stand. No comparison. No standard to meet. Just the next step.

VII Ministry Category Seven

Missions Ministry —
The Church That Goes, Not Just Gathers

These titles activate the missionary identity of every member before anyone boards a plane — establishing that the mission field is across the street before it is across the ocean.

Ministry Launch Goal

An active missions culture — local outreach teams, international partnerships, and a congregation that sees every Monday as mission field, not just every mission trip.

40 Days of Sent John 20:21 · Most Theologically Precise

"As the Father has sent me, I am sending you." Every member is sent. The mission field is not across the ocean — it is across the street. This title activates the missionary identity of every believer before anyone boards a plane or fills out a trip application.

2 40 Days of Your Jerusalem Acts 1:8 · Local First

Makes missions personal and local before it makes it global. Every member has a Jerusalem — a neighborhood, a workplace, a family — where the mission begins.

3 40 Days of Go Most Direct · Great Commission First Verb

Simple enough for a child to understand. Serious enough to challenge a forty-year attender who has never taken a step. The title that turns passive church members into active missionaries.

4 40 Days of Eyes to See Most Contemplative · Training Attention

Trains the congregation to see mission opportunities in ordinary moments they have been walking past for years. Built on Jesus's repeated invitation to see the harvest.

5 40 Days of The World Next Door Most Culturally Relevant · Global-Local

The world next door is the refugee family across the street, the international student at the university, the immigrant family at the next table. Makes global missions immediately local.

6 40 Days of The Great Commission Most Comprehensive · Full Assignment

The Great Commission is not one verse but a complete strategic assignment. The campaign teaches every dimension and shows how every member participates regardless of whether they travel internationally.

7 40 Days of Love Across Every Line Most Reconciliation-Oriented

Builds the theological and relational foundation for a missions culture that crosses the racial, economic, cultural, and geographic barriers that define who most Christians naturally reach — and avoid.

VIII Ministry Category Eight

Church Vision & Values Ministry —
Alignment That Becomes Culture

These titles build unified conviction around a shared direction — turning a vision statement from words on a wall into a personal conviction lived out by every member in every dimension of church life.

Ministry Launch Goal

A congregation that owns the vision personally — not just intellectually — and actively builds toward it in every ministry, every group, and every household.

40 Days of Why We Exist Most Foundational · Purpose Before Strategy

Before a congregation can pursue vision, it must understand purpose. Answers the three questions every church member is secretly asking: why are we here, what are we called to do, and why does it matter that I specifically am part of it?

2 40 Days of All In Most Commitment-Focused

Calls every member from casual agreement to full-hearted participation in what God is building through this specific church. The gap is not information but surrender.

3 40 Days of Our DNA Culture-Building · Values Transfer

Every great church has a culture more powerful than any program. This campaign defines the church's culture before it defines the church's strategy.

4 40 Days of One Vision Alignment · Post-Transition

Particularly effective for churches coming out of division, transition, or pastoral change. Creates the congregational alignment that makes the vision achievable.

5 40 Days of This Church Community-Specific · Irreplaceable

Not about church in general but about this specific congregation in this specific neighborhood at this specific moment in history. Makes every member feel they are part of something irreplaceable.

6 40 Days of Clarity Most Practically Useful

Forty days of building the clarity that turns a vision statement into a personal conviction and daily practice. For the congregation that has been given a compelling vision they have never fully understood or owned.

7 40 Days of The Next Chapter Transition-Oriented · New Season

Built for the congregation entering a new season of leadership, strategy, or growth. Honors the history while pointing toward what God is doing next — with unmistakable clarity and pastoral warmth.

IX Ministry Category Nine

Singles Ministry —
Whole, Called, and Fully Alive Now

These titles reject the waiting-room framing of singleness and build a compelling, Scripture-grounded vision for what a fully lived single life actually looks like — right now, in this season, without qualification.

Ministry Launch Goal

A thriving singles community — small groups, serving teams, mentoring partnerships, and a culture that treats single adults as fully deployed members of the body.

40 Days of Whole Most Important · Defeats the Incomplete Lie

The most important title for the singles ministry context — because the greatest lie most single people are living with is that they are incomplete. This campaign builds the theological and personal conviction that wholeness is not conditional on relationship status — full life is available right now.

2 40 Days of Now Most Activating · Against Postponement

This campaign calls single people into the fullness of their present — not a dress rehearsal, the actual performance. Against the spiritual danger of postponing full engagement with life and calling.

3 40 Days of Called and Single 1 Corinthians 7 · Gift Not Waiting Room

Built on Paul's teaching that singleness is a gift and a calling, not a waiting room. Transforms how single people understand their current season — from something to be survived to something to be fully inhabited.

4 40 Days of Fully Alive Most Aspirational · Can I Actually Thrive?

Answers the question every single adult is asking underneath every other question: can I actually thrive in this season? Yes — with theological substance and practical daily steps that prove it.

5 40 Days of Deep Roots Psalm 1 · Single Season as Investment

Makes singleness a formation investment rather than an interruption. The roots grown in this season sustain everything that comes next — whatever form that takes.

6 40 Days of The Single Life Most Direct · No Shame, No Apology

Names the experience without shame or apology. Builds a compelling, Scripture-grounded vision for what a fully lived single life actually looks like — not as consolation prize, but as a specific and honored calling.

7 40 Days of Belonging Most Community-Building for Singles

Builds the belonging community that a singles ministry exists to provide — and the groups launch on Celebration Sunday fills it. Addresses the experience of feeling profoundly alone in a room full of couples and families.

X Ministry Category Ten

Grandparents Ministry —
The Most Underdeployed Generation in the Church

These titles position grandparents not as a demographic to be served but as a generation to be deployed — the most strategically valuable formation asset in the congregation and the most powerful faith-passing force in every family.

Ministry Launch Goal

A GrandCamp or Legacy Council — organized, equipped, and intentionally deployed grandparents partnering with parents to disciple the next two generations of the congregation.

40 Days of Passing the Flame Most Theologically Powerful · Psalm 78:4 · Deuteronomy 6

The flame of faith that was passed to you was not meant to stop with you. Built on the imagery of Psalm 78:4 and Deuteronomy 6. Makes every grandparent a deliberate faith-passer rather than a hopeful bystander — the most activating title in the grandparents library.

2 40 Days of Arrows Psalm 127:4 · Grandparents as Bowmen

Grandparents are the bowmen — the ones who aim the next generation with wisdom, faith, and deliberate love. The most theologically grounded grandparenting title and the one that most powerfully reframes what a grandparent's role actually is.

3 40 Days of Grandparent on Purpose Most Intentional · Framework-Driven

The most influential grandparents are not the ones who simply show up but the ones who show up with a plan. Gives grandparents a framework for the spiritual formation of their grandchildren they have never had before.

4 40 Days of Legacy Letters Most Practically Transformational · Tangible Artifacts

Produces tangible legacy assets during the campaign that grandchildren will treasure for generations — and that Celebration Sunday showcases as living proof.

5 40 Days of The Greatest Gift Most Warmly Personal

Grandchildren are universally described as one of life's greatest gifts — and this campaign shows grandparents how to become the greatest gift in return.

6 40 Days of Second Chances Most Redemptive · Grace for Regret

For the grandparent who feels they did not do enough as a parent and sees grandparenting as the grace-filled opportunity to do it more intentionally the second time. Turns regret into renewed purpose.

7 40 Days of Wisdom to Offer Most Dignity-Affirming · Deployed Not Served

Positions grandparents as the most strategically valuable formation asset in the church — not a demographic to be served but a generation to be deployed.
