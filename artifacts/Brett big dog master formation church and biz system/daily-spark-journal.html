The Missing Layer

Before Sunday.

Everything else in SPARK happens after the sermon. This is the layer that primes the congregation before they arrive — while there is still time to invite someone, still time to read the text, still time to come expecting something.

📈 Why This Layer Matters
A congregation that arrives cold receives an announcement. A congregation that has read the passage, prayed for the service, and invited someone arrives already leaning forward. The pre-Sunday layer costs four touches and roughly doubles the formation depth of the message itself.

The Missing Layer

Every Audience.

One message, produced once, written seven ways. Right now most churches write for one adult who was present. These are the six who were not accounted for.

The Missing Layer

For Leaders.

The sermon goes to more than the congregation. Four groups need their own version — and the group leader needs theirs a day early or the whole system stalls on Tuesday.
