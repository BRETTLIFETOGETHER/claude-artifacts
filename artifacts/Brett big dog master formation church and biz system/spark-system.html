The Formation Week

The 7-Day
Sermon Devotional Arc.

Daily devotion is the greatest life transformation available to a congregation. The sermon that travels from Sunday to Saturday produces a different person by the following Sunday.

Daily Devotion Is the Greatest Life Transformation.
The Person Who Reads
The congregation member who opens Scripture every day for 40 days is not the same person at Day 40. Not because of willpower — because of exposure. The Word does the work when the person shows up for the appointment.
The Pastor Who Creates
The pastor who sends a daily formation prompt — voice memo, text, podcast — has built a pastoral relationship that exists outside of Sunday. This is how you change how church is done.
The System That Sustains
Inspiration lasts 72 hours. Formation requires architecture. The 7-day arc is the architecture. Without it, Sunday produces inspired people who change nothing. With it, Sunday produces formed people who change everything.

The Application Frameworks

Say. Mean. Do.
So What. How. Now What.

Two frameworks. One purpose: close the gap between the sermon that was heard and the life that was changed.

Framework One

Say. Mean. Do.

Three sentences. End of every sermon. The congregation leaves knowing what you said, what you meant, and what they do. The DO is not optional — it happens before the benediction.

Framework Two

So What? How What? Now What?

Three questions every listener is asking. Answer them explicitly from the platform. The sermon that does not answer all three leaves the congregation with inspiration but not formation.

This One Thing.
“If you remember nothing else from today, remember this one thing.”
Before You Go to Bed
One sentence. Tonight. What is the one thing from Sunday you want to still be thinking about on Thursday?
Feet on the Floor
Monday morning — before anything else — say this one thing out loud. Seven words or fewer. Then go.
Mark It or Write It
The one thing gets marked in your Bible or written in your journal right now — before another word is said.

Six Change Agent Principles

How Can a Pastor
Do and Be the Change?

Become a disciple maker, not just a disciple. Each principle carries one specific action you can take this week.

No Longer If or When — But Who and How.
The Old Question — If and When
If: Will the congregation grow? Will they form? Will they stay? — These assume formation is optional and the outcome uncertain.

When: When will they be ready to be discipled? When will they join a group? — These delay what can begin today.
The New Question — Who and How
Who: Who is the specific person you are walking with? Name them. Formation is relational, not programmatic.

How: What is the specific architecture — the 7-day arc, the 25 sparks, the Sunday moments — that carries formation from intention to transformation?
