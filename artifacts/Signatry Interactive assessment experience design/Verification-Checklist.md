## The prototype — eight screens, in this order

1. Who is answering (name and role)
2. The assessment — 24 statements, wording changes by role
3. **Your personal profile — straight from the assessment, no offerings in between**
4. The personal questionnaire — four quick, then ten written
5. The advisor questionnaire — optional, family sees all of it
6. The family profile — members, household picture, where the generations differ, one family experience
7. Day One — tracker by stage, open response lines, recommended next steps, tools, the Collective
8. The forty days

**Spot-check the prototype in two minutes:** answer the 24 statements quickly, and confirm you land on **your results** and not an offerings page. Then check that the results page shows **"Read These First — where God has already done something"** above **"Read These Next — where God is still asking."** No "strengths and weaknesses" anywhere.
