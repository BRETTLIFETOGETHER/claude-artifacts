What No Wealth Manager Offers

Every advisor talks about financial legacy. No one talks about this kind.

These ten titles must do something unique — speak to successful, accomplished, sophisticated families who have already heard every financial planning pitch, and make them feel that what the church is offering is something no estate attorney, financial advisor, or wealth manager can provide.

The goal isn't a single campaign. It's a family legacy cohort — high-capacity families meeting quarterly around values, calling, and generational faithfulness, with the flagship campaign as the on-ramp.

The Flagship

40 Days of Family Legacy is the foundational title — clear, aspirational, and serious enough to command the attention of a high-capacity family that is already thinking about what they will leave behind. It's the natural entry point built on Tom Conway's Family Legacy By Design curriculum.

"The family willing to ask 'what is enough and what comes after enough' is ready for the deepest legacy work."

01 Campaign One

40 Days of Family Legacy Flagship

Strongest for Family Legacy by Design

The foundational title. Clear, aspirational, and serious enough to command the attention of a high-capacity family that is already thinking about what they will leave behind. Works as the flagship campaign built on Tom Conway's Family Legacy By Design curriculum. Speaks to the family that has built something significant and now wants to ensure the right things survive them.

02 Campaign Two

40 Days of What Matters Most

Midlife Inflection · Legacy Awakening

Cuts through every accomplishment a successful family has accumulated and asks the one question they have been postponing: at the end, what will have actually mattered? Speaks directly to the midlife inflection point that drives high-capacity families toward legacy work for the first time.

03 Campaign Three

40 Days of Lasting Wealth

High Net Worth · Values Transfer

Reframes wealth as something that includes — but extends far beyond — financial assets. Values. Faith. Character. The things that compound across generations. Every wealth manager talks about financial legacy. No wealth manager talks about this kind.

04 Campaign Four

40 Days of The Second Half

Bob Buford · Halftime Framework

Built on the Halftime language that has resonated with high-capacity leaders for three decades. Speaks directly to the person who has won the first half of life by every external measure and is now asking what the second half is actually for.

05 Campaign Five

40 Days of Generational Faithfulness

Psalm 78 · Deuteronomy 6

The most theologically grounded title — rooted in Psalm 78:4, Deuteronomy 6, and the biblical theology of passing faith from one generation to the next. Appeals to families who want to do more than transfer wealth — they want to transfer conviction, character, and covenant.

06 Campaign Six

40 Days of The Wealthy Soul

Net Worth vs. Soul Worth

The most provocative title — built on the tension between net worth and soul worth, between financial abundance and spiritual poverty. Speaks directly to the high-capacity family that has everything and privately wonders why it does not feel like enough. Opens the conversation only the church can have.

07 Campaign Seven

40 Days of What We Leave Behind

Parents · Grandparents · Founders

Emotionally resonant for every parent of adult children and every grandparent of young grandchildren. Honest, universal, and impossible to ignore. Creates immediate personal connection before the first page is turned.

08 Campaign Eight

40 Days of Family on Purpose

Intentional · Strategic · Founders

Built on the language of intentionality — the concept that a family's legacy is not the residue of accumulated days but the fruit of deliberate choices. Appeals to business owners and founders who apply strategic thinking to every domain of their professional life and want to apply it to their family for the first time.

09 Campaign Nine

40 Days of The Blessed Life

Dual Meaning · Spiritually Convicting

Draws on the dual meaning of blessed — financially prosperous and spiritually grounded — and invites high-capacity families to examine which version of the blessed life they are actually building. The most spiritually convicting title in the list.

10 Campaign Ten

40 Days of Enough

Most Countercultural · Deepest Work

The most countercultural title for an ultra-high-net-worth audience — and therefore the most powerful. The family that has accumulated extraordinary wealth and is willing to ask "what is enough and what comes after enough" is ready for the deepest legacy work. This campaign changes the conversation from accumulation to distribution, from achievement to faithfulness, from net worth to kingdom worth.

"Have you prepared your family as carefully as you have prepared your estate?"
