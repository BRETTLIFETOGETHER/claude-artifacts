XVIII Dimension XVIII

Flourishing Digital Life

Living Wisely with Technology, Screens, and Artificial Intelligence

Most discipleship content still treats technology as an afterthought — a chapter, not a dimension — even though the average person now spends more waking hours on a screen than in any other single activity. This dimension takes technology as seriously as it takes money or marriage: not to reject it, but to bring wisdom to a part of life shaping people faster than the church is discipling them for it.

XIX Dimension XIX

Flourishing Creativity

Using Imagination, Art, and Making as an Act of Worship

Creativity gets treated as a hobby for the artistically gifted, not a dimension of flourishing for everyone made in the image of a Creator. This dimension is built on the conviction that imagination, making, and creative expression are not extras — they're evidence of whose image we bear, available to the accountant and the painter alike.
