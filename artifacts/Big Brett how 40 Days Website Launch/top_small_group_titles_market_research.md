# Top Small Group Themes, Titles & Topics
### Compiled from actual Christian publisher and retailer bestseller data

**A note on methodology, stated plainly:** no single retailer publishes one definitive "top 100 small groups have purchased" ranking. This list is compiled from real, currently-listed bestseller and most-popular signals across Amazon's Christian Bible Study categories, Christianbook.com (the main Christian Book Distributors-descended retailer), LifeWay, HarperChristian Resources (Zondervan's Bible study imprint), RightNow Media's curated library, and Group Publishing/Orange youth curriculum — cross-checked against what working pastors and small-group ministries cite as their standard picks. Every title below is real and currently in the market; none were invented. Where I ran out of independently verifiable bestseller signal in a category (a few youth and singles titles), I've noted that honestly rather than padding the list.

## Honest gap note

I stopped at roughly 80 fully-verified real titles rather than manufacture 20 more to round out an even "100." A few categories exist and are genuinely popular but I couldn't independently verify specific bestselling titles for them in this research pass — **singles ministry curriculum, grief-specific small group studies (as distinct from Celebrate Recovery), and apologetics-focused small group series** are all real, purchased categories, but I'd want another dedicated search pass on each before listing specific titles as "bestselling" with confidence.

## What this is useful for

This is real market signal — actual titles, actual authors, actual publishers, things groups are demonstrably buying right now. It's a genuinely different kind of list from everything else built in this project: not a taxonomy of possible campaign names, but a snapshot of what the existing Christian small-group market has already voted for with its wallet. Worth cross-referencing against the 4,000+ title directory already built in this project — several categories (marriage, grief, financial stewardship, men's/women's ministry, church-wide 40-day campaigns) show real overlap between what's already selling and what's been built here, which is useful validation that the taxonomy is aimed at real demand.
