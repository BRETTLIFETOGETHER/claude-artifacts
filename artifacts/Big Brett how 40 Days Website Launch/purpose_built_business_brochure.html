Why This Ministry Doesn't Exist Yet

Your most influential members are the least specifically served.

Christian business leaders — founders, executives, entrepreneurs, professionals — are simultaneously the most influential people in your community and the people for whom the church has historically had the least targeted ministry. They've heard every financial planning pitch, sat through every leadership book, and still show up on Sunday without a room built for the specific weight they carry Monday through Friday.

These nine campaigns change that. Each one is designed to launch and sustain a business leaders community that meets monthly, holds each other accountable, and becomes the most generosity-generating, influence-multiplying ministry in the church.

The Strategic Progression

Purpose at Work opens the door. Business as Mission gives theological foundation. Faithful with Much creates stewardship conviction. Generosity That Scales bridges directly to donor-advised-fund and charitable-giving conversations. Built to Last connects to Family Legacy and exit planning. This is the campaign architecture for the intersection of church, marketplace, and high-capacity families.

"The campaign is not separate from the ministry launch. The campaign IS the ministry launch."
