# A Different Kind of Advisor

### Six Sessions, Forty Days, Three Editions

Master architecture. One spine, three overlays. Session and day counts are identical across all editions so kits, brochures, and leader materials line up.

---

## The neutral master

Six things a person entrusted with something must release, in rising order of cost.

| # | Master principle | The release | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You are holding what is not yours | Ownership | The illusion of having earned it |
| 2 | What you know was given to you | Ideas | Credit |
| 3 | The people around you are not line items | People | Control |
| 4 | Your interest is not your client's interest | Incentive | Money |
| 5 | The window closes before the money arrives | Timing | Convenience |
| 6 | The result is not your department | Outcome | Security |

Every edition carries these six in this order. Only vocabulary, examples, and application change.

**Day distribution across all editions:** 7 / 7 / 7 / 7 / 6 / 6

# Edition One · Advisor (Christian)

*What Two Clients Taught a Lawyer About the Practice He Thought He Owned*

### Sessions

**1. Someone Else's Property** · Luke 16:10-12
Terry's office, 1976. The desk, the arrangement, and the client who did not accept it. What it means that the entire job description is handling what belongs to another.

**2. You Can't Copyright God's Principles** · Philippians 1:15-18
Three hundred and fifty violations and a refusal to sue. Applied to the practice: teaching away your process, referring business you could have kept, and what happens to a firm that stops guarding its methodology.

Meeting the need instead of the number. Applied: compensation versus development, building successors who could leave, and why nineteen-year tenure is never about the number.

**4. The Conversation That Costs You** · Matthew 6:24
The compensation conflict named out loud. Paid on assets your client should give away. The three ways advisors avoid it, and the sentence that changes the room. *Full draft exists.*

Eighteen months, one window, and the assignment of income doctrine. The most expensive piece of ignorance in Christian philanthropy is a calendar problem.

**6. Shut It Down and Go** · Psalm 127:1-2
Thirteen years, a desk drawer, and nobody calling back. Releasing the outcome of a practice, and what a book of business is actually for.

# Edition Two · Secular and Values-Based (RIA custom)

**A Different Kind of Advisor**
*Six Disciplines That Separate Counsel From Sales*

### The technical answer to serving both audiences

Do not fork the manuscript. Print one interior with a **switchable formation block**: each day carries a story, a principle, an application, and questions in universal language, plus one bracketed scripture-and-reflection block set as a discrete element. Firms with a faith-based strain include it. Firms serving broadly omit it. Same typesetting, same page count, same day numbering, one production run.

That single decision is what makes this licensable to a large RIA. A book that has to be defended to a compliance officer will not get distributed. A book with an optional block will.

### Sessions

**1. Whose It Is** · The fiduciary reality underneath the legal standard. Custody as a moral fact rather than a regulatory one.

**2. Open Source** · Giving away methodology. Why firms that publish their process outgrow firms that protect it, and what a referral you did not have to make actually buys.

**3. Beyond the Comp Plan** · People as capability rather than cost. Development, succession, and the retention math nobody runs.

**4. Disclosed Interest** · The conflict named out loud before the recommendation. The most trust-generating sentence available to a professional, and the reason it stops working the moment it becomes a technique.

**5. The Window** · Pre-liquidity planning as the highest-value and least-performed act in the profession.

**6. What You Are Measuring** · Releasing the metric. What a book of business is for, and the difference between a practice and a firm.

### Conversion rules

Vocabulary: stewardship becomes custody or responsibility. Calling becomes vocation. Congregation becomes clientele. Ministry becomes mission or impact. Formation becomes development.

Outcomes language: church editions speak formation, secular editions speak retention, referral quality, succession readiness, and client tenure. Same spine, different scoreboard.

What does not change: the six principles, the session order, the forty-day count, and the Terry narrative. His story is not religious content. It is professional history, and it belongs in every edition.

# Edition Three · Family

**What You Hold**
*Six Conversations Every Family Should Have Before the Money Arrives*

The family analog of the advisor's compensation conflict is sharper than the original. A parent's incentive is to keep control, because control is how you keep the family close. The whole book turns on that.

### Sessions

**1. Whose Is It** · The founder's assumption that he built it, and what happens to a family organized around that belief. Ownership before inheritance.

**2. Tell Them How** · Releasing knowledge. Most families transfer assets and withhold the wisdom that produced them, then wonder at the result. What it costs to teach your children how you actually did it, including the parts you are not proud of.

**3. The People Around Us** · How a family treats those who serve it. The assistant, the foreman, the caregiver, the in-law. The clearest signal children receive about what money is for.

**4. The Conversation That Costs You** · Telling your children the truth about the money. It costs you leverage, and leverage is what most patriarchs are actually protecting when they say the children are not ready.

**5. Before the Sale** · The eighteen months, from the family's side. Who is told, what is decided, and what the family will be known for, settled while it is still theoretical.

**6. Advise, Don't Control** · The release that names the book. The instrument Terry built is called donor-*advised* for a legal reason, and the same rule that makes a gift real makes an heir free.

### Format notes

Forty daily readings on the same numbering, written for both spouses and usable by adult children. Every session ends in a family exercise rather than an individual one, and the sixth ends with a family statement rather than a covenant, since a family cannot sign a pledge the way a congregation can.

Session six is the hinge and the reason this edition exists. Every other family legacy resource teaches families to give. None of them teaches a founder that the discipline required to release money is the identical discipline required to hand his children real authority.

## Build notes

The master must stay neutral. Sessions and days convert; the spine does not.

Alignment is checked at three points: session count, day count, and section order within each day. If any edition drifts, the kits stop lining up and the whole production advantage disappears.

Chapter four of the advisor edition is drafted in full and can serve as the sample chapter for all three.
