The Deep Well · A Working Brochure

Six things a person entrusted
with something has to let go of.

Families with something to pass down hire for wills, trusts, tax structure, and family meetings. The actual obstacle is that one person cannot let go, everyone in the family knows it, and nobody can say it out loud. Start by finding out where your hand is closed.

The Case

The plan is not the problem. The grip is.

What is already served

Technical transfer is well covered. Documents, structures, and vehicles are mature and competent. A family can buy excellent versions of all of it.

What nothing touches

The grip belongs to the person paying for the advice. No professional whose fee depends on that person is structurally able to name it, and most do not try.

What this is

An instrument that names it without accusation, a story that carries it without exposure, and a process that ends in one decision actually changing hands.

A daughter cannot say to her father, you did not tell us because while we did not know, you were the only one who could decide. She can say, at a table, with the book between them, that Silas should have told them. And her father can agree with her, out loud, about Silas.Why the story is an allegory
