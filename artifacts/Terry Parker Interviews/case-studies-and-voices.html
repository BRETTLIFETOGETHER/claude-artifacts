The Production Decision

Do not fork the manuscript. Switch the block.

Every daily reading carries a story, a principle, an application, and questions in universal language, plus one bracketed formation block set as a discrete typographic element. Firms include it or omit it. Same typesetting, same page count, same day numbering, one press run. A book that has to be defended to a compliance officer will not get distributed. A book with an optional block will.

Faith Driven

Full formation block. NIV scripture, a closing prayer, and the invitation question. Congregations, Christian RIAs, ministry families, denominational partners.

Faith Friendly

Reduced block. The wisdom source named without argument, no prayer, no closing invitation. Firms with a faith heritage and a mixed client base. The most commonly requested and the hardest to write well.

Values Driven

Block omitted entirely. Fiduciary and ethical language throughout. Large RIAs, wirehouse charitable desks, community foundations, corporate and family office use.

The Story · Identical in All Three

A man spent an evening at his kitchen table counting the ways another man had stolen from him. He found three hundred and fifty. Fifteen thousand copies already printed. He did the arithmetic twice, because the first number seemed impolite, and the second time it came out the same.

The man he was defending listened to all of it, thanked him, and did nothing. A month later, pressed for a reason, he said only this: now there is going to be two books instead of one.

Most professionals do not have a copyright case. They have something quieter. A colleague who repeats your idea and receives the nod. A competitor whose deck reads suspiciously like yours. A junior who learned it all from you and is now doing well without mentioning you.

The Application · Identical in All Three

Name yours. One instance where someone took what was yours and did not credit you, and where you are still, privately, keeping the tally. Write the person's name on paper.

Then write beside it the honest answer to one question: did the thing you care about advance, or not? If it advanced, tear the page up. That is the whole exercise, and it is harder than it reads.

Composite

The Manufacturer

Founder, 68 · Second-generation business · 14 months from a sale

He inherited a shop with nine employees and built it to two hundred. Three adult children, one inside the business. He has told nobody outside the room that the offer exists.

Open Case
FindingTruth diverged by twelve points. He scored 18; his children averaged 6. People and Ownership were aligned and strong. AssembledShelf three. Four sessions, certified facilitator, advisor not in the room for sessions two and three at his own recommendation. SequenceThree Names, then the Failure Ledger, then the Sealed Envelope, then the Handover. OutcomeHe told them about the offer in session two, eleven months earlier than he had planned to. Session four moved signing authority on capital expenditure under a threshold to his daughter, with a date.

What actually turned it

Not the envelope exercise. The Failure Ledger. He read a 1998 decision that nearly cost him the company, in operational detail, and his son said afterward that he had never once heard his father describe being wrong. The room changed before anything difficult was attempted.

What did not happen. The charitable conversation. The window is open and nobody has raised it, because the advisor was not in the room and the facilitator does not do that. This is the seam in the model and it is not solved.
Composite

The Farm

Fourth generation · 2,400 acres · Grain, equipment, and no cash

Two brothers farm it. A sister lives out of state and owns a third. Nobody has discussed the will since their mother's stroke.

Open Case
FindingAligned on People and Ownership. Divided on Release by nine. The sister's scores diverged most from everyone's, and she had said almost nothing in eight years. AssembledShelf two first, deliberately, before anything about the land. One overnight. SequenceThe Origin Trip to the original quarter section, the Missing Witness with an 89-year-old aunt, then the Empty Chair. OutcomeThe aunt named two people who had been missing from the founding story for sixty years, one of whom was the sister's grandmother. The land conversation happened four months later and did not require a facilitator.

The asset nobody had considered

Grain. A gift of unsold crop before sale is one of the most efficient charitable transfers available to a farm family and one of the least used. It surfaced because the assessment asked what they held, not what they earned.

Honest caution. Agricultural gifting has timing and structuring requirements that are unforgiving. Nothing here substitutes for the family's own tax counsel and the receiving organization's gift planning team.
Composite

The Sudden One

Founder, 34 · Software · Liquidity 90 days after the assessment

No prior wealth. Parents still working. Married five years, no children, and eleven friends who now behave differently around him.

Open Case
FindingScored 96, one of the highest individual totals recorded in the pilot, and Release at 6. High conviction, no structure, no practice. AssembledShelf one, then shelf four directly. Shelf three skipped entirely, correctly. SequenceThe Enough Line, then the Where instrument, then Before the Sale in a single compressed week. OutcomeA pre-agreement gift of founder stock. He set an enough number at 34 that his advisor described as the most consequential sentence of his financial life.

Why this case matters to the model

Everything in this library assumes an older founder and a family with history. This one had neither. The six releases held anyway, which is the first evidence that the architecture is not age-dependent.

Unresolved. There is no next-generation track for a family with no next generation yet, and no product for the friendships that change after a liquidity event. Both are gaps.
Composite

The Widow

72 · Eight months after · Learned the full balance sheet at the reading

Married 49 years. Never attended a meeting with the attorney or the advisor. Four children, two of whom have opinions about what their father would have wanted.

Open Case
FindingHer Truth score was the lowest single dimension in the pilot. She had been treated well for fifty years and trusted with almost nothing, and had never had language for the difference. AssembledShelf one only, for nine months. No decisions, no handovers, no structure work of any kind. SequenceFirst Memory, Recipe Night, the Annual Letter written by her rather than about her. OutcomeShe ran the second year's family meeting herself. The estate conversation happened in year two and she chaired it.

The design lesson

The pressure in this case was entirely toward decisions, from the children, the attorney, and the advisor, all with good intentions. The correct move was nine months of shelf one, and the model has to be able to say no to urgency that is not the family's own.

Note. Grief is not a shelf. Bereavement in the first year requires professional support the library does not provide and should never imply it does.
Composite

The Cousins

Third generation · 19 shareholders · Founder dead 22 years

Nobody in the room built anything. Four of the nineteen have never met. Two branches have not spoken since 2011.

Open Case
FindingNo divergence anywhere, and low totals across the board. Alignment produced by disengagement rather than by health, which the instrument cannot distinguish and a facilitator can. AssembledCousin consortium format. Two days. Story before governance, against the wishes of the two family members who called. SequenceThe Reverse Interview with the three oldest, the Auction, then a structured vote on whether to remain a shared enterprise. OutcomeThey voted to stay together, eleven to eight, and the eight were not punished. Two branches sold out over the following two years, amicably, at a valuation nobody contested.

The instrument's blind spot

A uniformly low, uniformly aligned grid looks calm and is not. This is the one pattern where the assessment can mislead, and it is why a facilitator reads it rather than software.

Different problem entirely. Third generation onward is not about release. It is about whether a shared thing is worth keeping, and the tool is a vote nobody is punished for.

The Blended House

Remarried 12 years · Two sets of children · Unequal contribution, unequal expectation

He brought the business. She brought two children and a decade of building it with him. Nobody has said the quiet part in twelve years.

Open Case
FindingOwnership diverged by eleven, which is unusual and diagnostic. His children and her children were describing two different companies. AssembledShelf two, then a founder-and-spouse session before anything involving the children. SequenceThree Names run twice, once per branch, then compared. Then the Empty Chair, then the One-Page Constitution. OutcomeHer name appeared on none of his children's lists and on both of hers. He noticed before anyone said anything, and the constitution was written by her.

The tool that did the work

Three Names. It requires no accusation, produces the evidence in four minutes of silence, and cannot be argued with because nobody made a claim.

Not addressed. Prenuptial and postnuptial realities sit underneath every blended case and the library has nothing on them. That is a gap and it needs counsel, not curriculum.

The Regional RIA

$2.4B AUM · 11 advisors · Faith heritage, mixed client base

Founded by two elders of the same church. Two thirds of current clients have no faith affiliation and the firm has quietly stopped mentioning its origins.

Open Case
ProblemThe founding partners believed the firm's convictions were an asset and could not find a way to use them that survived a compliance review.SolutionFaith Friendly register across all client-facing material, with the Faith Driven edition available on request. Two hundred copies of each.ResultThe date-column exercise run firm-wide found that in 31 of 44 liquidity events over five years, the charitable conversation followed the letter of intent.

What changed operationally

One field added to the CRM: date of charitable conversation, compared against date of LOI. Reviewed quarterly. That is the entire intervention and it is the one most likely to be adopted.

Secular · 900 funds · Values Driven register only

Serves an entire county across every tradition and none. Cannot distribute anything with religious content and has no equivalent resource.

It is the proof that the material travels outside the faith market. If the six releases work in a secular county foundation, the addressable market is every sponsoring organization in the country rather than the Christian slice of it.

The Gift Planner

National charitable sponsor · Non-cash asset specialist

Structures business interests, real estate, and agricultural gifts. Sees families at the moment of the transaction and almost never before it.

Open Case
ProblemTechnically excellent, arriving too late, and repeatedly watching families sign binding agreements before anyone asked the question.SolutionThe assessment handed to referring advisors as a free instrument, twelve to twenty-four months upstream.ResultReferral conversations shifted from what can you do with this asset to what is this family actually for.
The honest limit. No instrument fixes a referral that arrives after signing. The window is a calendar problem and the only solution is earlier, which is a behavior change in the referring advisor, not a product.

The Church of 1,800

Suburban · One executive pastor · No generosity staff

Six business owners in the congregation, two within three years of an exit. Nobody at the church knows which two.

Open Case
ProblemThe largest gift any member will ever make will not go to the church, and the church has no vehicle, no conversation, and nobody whose job this is.SolutionAssessment offered free to the whole congregation. No fund, no vehicle, no liability. Marketplace ministry lay leader as the operator.ResultFour of the six owners surfaced themselves. The church-branded fund became the second conversation rather than the first, carried legally by a national sponsor.
Where this fails. Without a lay operator it is shelfware. The product is not the tool. It is the person inside the church whose job this is, and most churches do not have one.
