The Family Library · Working Document

The plan is not the problem.
The grip is.

Families with something to pass down hire for wills, trusts, tax structure, and family meetings. The actual obstacle is that one person cannot let go, everyone knows it, and nobody can say it out loud. Every resource here exists to let a family have that conversation without anyone having to accuse anyone.

The Case

Why any of this needs to exist.

What the market already serves

Technical transfer is well covered. Documents, structures, and vehicles are mature, competitive, and competent. A family can buy excellent versions of all of it.

What it does not touch

The grip belongs to the person paying for the advice. No professional whose fee depends on that person is structurally able to name it, and most do not try.

What we build

An instrument that names it without accusation, a story that carries it without exposure, and a process that ends in one decision actually changing hands.

The mechanism

A daughter cannot say to her father, you did not tell us because while we did not know, you were the only one who could decide. She can say, at a table, with the book between them, that Silas should have told them. And her father can agree with her, out loud, about Silas.

That is not a literary preference. It is a safety device, and it is the only mechanism we know of that lets a family have this conversation without someone leaving the room.

The measure

A family reads it, has six conversations, and nothing changes. That is the common failure in this category and it is not usually the family's fault.

So the test is not whether they liked it. The test is whether one specific thing got decided that had been postponed. Something told that had been withheld. A date set. A piece of authority actually handed over rather than promised.

Hope From Here

What this looks like if it works.

01The Record Survives

Ten thousand families holding a founding story with the missing people restored, a failure ledger, and an hour of the founder teaching how he actually decides.

02The Window Gets Used

Advisors raising the charitable question eighteen months before a letter of intent as ordinary practice rather than heroism.

03Authority Moves Early

A generation that receives real decisions at thirty rather than documents at sixty, and knows what to do with them.

04The Founding Is Told

The origin of the modern donor-advised fund recorded in the voice of the man who built it, before that becomes impossible.

Ten Journeys · Open Any to See a Sample

Each journey is four to six sessions, assembled for the family that requested it.

01
Entry & Discovery
For a family that has never done this and is not sure it wants to.
Open Sample

Nothing asks anyone to be vulnerable. One evening, one instrument, one reading. The purpose is not insight. It is a second meeting. Carries the Personal Assessment, First Memory, the Table Map, and the Giving Dinner.

Sample · Session One Opening

Before we talk about anything difficult, everyone is going to answer one question, and it is not about money. What is the first thing you remember costing something? Not a price. A cost.

Three minutes each. Youngest goes first. Nobody responds to anyone until all of us have gone.

02
Story & Heritage
For a family whose founding story has one person in it.
Open Sample

Removes the founder's exemption without anyone challenging him. Ends with a scheduled interview of the oldest living witness, which is the deliverable families most often skip and most often regret skipping.

Sample · The Three Names Instruction

Write three people, other than yourself, without whom this family would not have what it has. Four minutes of silence, and I am going to enforce it, because the first name is easy and the third one is the exercise.

We read youngest to oldest. Whoever built this reads last.

03
Clarity & Values
For a family that agrees on values in the abstract and nowhere else.
Open Sample

Forced ranking and real trading rather than consensus, because every family values statement written by agreement in one sitting is unusable. Ends in a one-page constitution written in ninety minutes, which gets followed precisely because it is rough.

Sample · The Enough Line

Write your own number. Not the family's. What your household actually needs, annually, to live the life you believe you are called to live.

Then write what happens to everything above it. If you have never written that second sentence, that is the finding, and you are in good company.

04
Communication & Conflict
Facilitator required. Never a family's first meeting.
Open Sample

The Rope, the Sealed Envelope, the Empty Chair, and the sibling, in-law, and founder-and-spouse sessions. Designed around consent rather than exposure at every step.

Sample · Sealed Envelope Rules, Read Twice Aloud

Write one thing you know that the others do not, and the real reason you have not said it. Seal it. Nothing is opened tonight.

Whether these are ever opened is decided by all of us, unanimously. If one person says no, the answer is no, and that person is never identified. That rule does not expire.

05
Generosity
For a family that gives well and has never decided where.
Open Sample

The Where instrument, the Giving Dinner, a site visit, the grant rhythm, and successor advisor designation. Why is conviction, how much is capacity, when is timing. Where is discernment, and it is the question nobody teaches.

Sample · The Where Instrument

Three questions, in this order. What has God already put in front of us, meaning proximity and history rather than whoever asked most recently. What are we uniquely able to do, meaning our capital, our relationships, our expertise.

And what will we be known for. That third one is the only question that requires all of us to agree, which is why most families never ask it.

06
Financial Competence
For heirs who have seen outcomes and never the arithmetic.
Open Sample

The family balance sheet explained honestly, taxes in plain language, investment literacy, debt and dignity, first job and first paycheck. Pairs with third-party financial discipleship titles rather than duplicating them.

Sample · The Balance Sheet Session

We are going to look at the actual statement. Not a summary, not a pie chart. The document.

You will not be asked to have an opinion. You will be asked, at the end, to explain one line of it back to the person sitting next to you. That is the whole test, and most adults cannot do it about their own household.

07
Business & Succession
For an owner between five and twenty-four months from a decision.
Open Sample

Shadow Day, Teach One Thing, ownership versus management, non-family leadership, and Before the Sale. Contains the one item in the entire library that cannot be fixed after the fact.

Sample · Before the Sale, Opening

There is one decision in this process that has a door on it, and the door closes at the signing of a binding agreement. Everything else can be revisited. This cannot.

So we are going to make it now, while it is still theoretical and still generous, rather than in ninety days when it is real and you are a different person about it.

08
Next Generation
Paired cohorts only. Never run without a founder track.
Open Sample

Rising generation and founders meet separately and converge on the last morning. Founder deliverable: one transferred decision with a date. Rising gen deliverable: one venture that is not the family enterprise.

Sample · Dig Your Own, Founder Briefing

You will want to instruct. Four or five times today you will hear something you know to be a mistake. Here is the sentence you will use instead: here is what I know, and it is your call.

If a proposal is genuinely bad, that is not your problem to fix in this room.

09
Marriage & Partnership
Two childhoods, one checkbook.
Open Sample

Spouses as full members rather than attendees, in-law integration, blended family money, disagreement protocols, and surviving spouse readiness. The most underserved journey in the library and the one families ask for second.

Sample · Spouses as Full Members

A question for the person who married into this family. How many years have you been in this room, and how many times has anyone asked you a direct question in it?

You do not have to answer out loud. But somebody in this family should hear the number.

10
Transition & Grief
For the year everything changes, and the year after.
Open Sample

The estate conversation while everyone is well, the Auction, legacy wishes, the first year after, dividing the objects, estrangement and return. Includes the founder's second assignment, which almost nothing on the market addresses.

Sample · The Auction Rule

Everything is on the table. Before you may claim anything, you tell the story of why you want it, out loud, to everyone.

Nothing goes by seniority and nothing goes by value. We are doing this while the person who owns it is alive and can hear it, which is the entire point, and which is why it will not happen in a hallway after a funeral.

Dynamic by Design

No two families receive the same library.

The assessment names the shelf. The shelf narrows the journey. The journey assembles the tools. The advisor does not choose any of it, and should be able to say so to the family.

01Instrument

Everyone answers privately. The system scores the spread, not the average. Divergence of nine or more on any dimension outranks every low score on the grid.

02Assembly

The journey is generated from the finding: which shelf, which four to six sessions, which tools, and which are withheld because the family is not ready.

03Intensity

One evening, one day, one overnight, or six sessions across a year. Same master, four intensities, identical section order so materials line up.

04Record

Every session writes to the profile. Decisions, dates, owners, and the capture archive, under three-tier permissions the family controls.

What personalizes

  • Shelf and sequence — from the spread, not from preference
  • Values overlay — secular, values-based, or Christian, via the switchable formation block
  • Tool set — shelf three tools are locked until a facilitator is assigned
  • Experience format — evening, day, overnight, or trip
  • Language and region — generated from the master, never forked

What never varies

  • Six releases, in order — ownership, ideas, people, incentive, timing, outcome
  • Forty days — seven, seven, seven, seven, six, six
  • The consent rules — unanimity on the envelope, no exceptions
  • The ethics rule — private disclosure is never sales intelligence
  • The handover — no journey ends without one real decision and a date

Live Instrument · Ten Minutes

The Deep Well Personal Assessment

Six dimensions, twenty-four statements, one hundred twenty points. Rate each 1 to 5. 1 is not true of me, 5 is consistently true. Answer as you are, not as you intend to be. Nothing is transmitted anywhere; scoring happens in your browser.

Illustrative Composites · Not Real Clients

What it looks like when it works.

These are constructions built from patterns, written to show the shape of an outcome. No real family or client detail appears here. Real stories require named permission and are gathered separately.

Advisor

The Date Column

He ran the five-minute exercise and found that in four of his last five liquidity events, the charitable conversation happened after the letter of intent. Nobody had ever shown him the pattern. He now logs the date against the LOI on every file.

The Book on the Desk

She stopped presenting and started handing over a book with her name nowhere in it. Six of eleven clients raised the subject themselves within a month. A client who raises it is a different client than one who was pitched.

The Sentence

He told a client out loud what the recommendation would cost him. The client took the advice, and then introduced him to two families, and the referral had nothing to do with performance.

Family

The Third Name

The founder's list had three business partners on it. His wife's list had his mother, a lender who took a risk in 1987, and him. Nobody argued. The story changed anyway, and his son noticed first.

Twelve

Truth diverged by twelve points. They ran four sessions instead of the six they wanted, and the fourth ended with a signing authority moving to a daughter with a date attached. Nothing else on the agenda got done.

The Objects

They ran the Auction on a Saturday with the mother present. Two items were claimed by people nobody expected, for reasons nobody knew. The hallway conversation that everyone had been dreading never happened.

Counsel

The Family Legacy by Design Series for Attorneys

Estate and tax attorneys sit closer to the grip than anyone else in a family's professional life, and are the least equipped to name it, because the document is the deliverable and the conversation is not billable. Faculty: Tom Conway, Brett Eastman, Michael King, and a working group of practicing attorneys.

Session One

The Document Is Not the Plan

A perfect instrument executed by a family that has never spoken is a well-drafted future lawsuit.

Session Two

The Conversation Before the Draft

The questions that change the document, asked before anyone opens a template.

Session Three

Advise, Don't Control

Why the instrument is called donor-advised, and what the same distinction does inside a family.

Session Four

The Client's Children

Meeting the next generation before the reading of the will, and the ethics of doing so.

Session Five

Before the Signing

The window, the assignment of income doctrine, and the calendar problem that costs families more than any drafting error.

Session Six

When the Client Dies

The first ninety days, the objects, the silence, and the family that now has only you.
