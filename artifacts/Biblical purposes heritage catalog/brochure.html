Why This Library?

Thirty Years of Biblical Purpose, Made New

For more than three decades, the biblical purposes of worship, fellowship, discipleship, ministry, and mission have transformed churches around the world.

LifeTogether expands those same biblical themes into a comprehensive discipleship library for today's church.
