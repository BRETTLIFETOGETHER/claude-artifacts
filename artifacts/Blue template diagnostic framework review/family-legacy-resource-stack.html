The problem it solves

Families have assets, values and intentions. Almost none have a system.

Legacy is usually treated as a planning event with a signature at the end. This system treats it as a developmental journey the family takes while everyone is still in the room — moving from what we leave behind to how we form a family across generations.

For the family

Clarity about where you actually are, discernment about what matters in this season, and a tangible artifact at the end of every domain — a mission statement, a blessing letter, a governance charter — that becomes the family's institutional memory.

For the advisor

A defensible way to lead the most important conversation in the relationship without drifting into therapy, law or pastoral care. Briefings, discovery questions, scripts, diagnostic signals, and a clear boundary of expertise with a referral path.
