Obedience · Campaign 01 · The flagship

Irrational Obedience

Saying yes to God before the reasons arrive.
01

The flagship of the category and the entry point to everything under it. Irrational Obedience confronts the pattern nobody names: almost every pivotal instruction in Scripture would fail a reasonable review at the moment it is given — and the people who obeyed were not being unreasonable. They were working from a larger set of facts.

The big idea
God's clearest instructions rarely make sense at the time. Irrational is the observer's word, never the obeyer's — and the difference between them is what the whole book is about.

The core problem

We have been taught to test guidance against prudence, and prudence is a good test — until it becomes the only one. So we wait for an instruction that is both clearly from God and obviously sensible, and that combination almost never arrives in Scripture.

The transformation promise

Participants stop asking whether an ask makes sense and start asking what they can see that the spreadsheet cannot. They learn to obey on a bigger ledger — and to tell the difference between faith and recklessness.

Obedience · Campaign 02 · The follow-through

Living Obedience

Turn what you hear from God into how you live.
02

Living Obedience confronts the most common spiritual stall: hearing clearly and obeying rarely. It moves people from admiring God's Word to actually doing what it says — and it is where a household finds out whether the yes it made was a moment or a life.

The big idea
Hearing without doing isn't neutral — it's self-deception. Obedience is where faith stops being an idea and becomes a life.

The core problem

It is possible to hear God clearly and still not obey Him. We nod along on Sunday, underline the verse, feel the conviction — and then live Monday exactly as we would have anyway. The gap is rarely a hearing problem; it is a doing problem.

The transformation promise

Participants move from admiring God's Word to actually obeying it, building the daily habit of saying yes until obedience stops being an occasional decision and becomes a settled way of life.
