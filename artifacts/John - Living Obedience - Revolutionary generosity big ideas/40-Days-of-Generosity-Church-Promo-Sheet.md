# 40 DAYS OF GENEROSITY
### A churchwide campaign · Church adoption sheet

---

## What it is

Six weeks. Three tracks. One book. A whole-church journey into what the Bible actually says about ownership, contentment, fear, sight, family, and joy — built so a congregation of any size and any income can travel it together.

**Nobody is asked for anything.** No pledge cards, no commitment weekend, no capital appeal built into week six. That constraint is the point. It is what makes people talk honestly, and it is the reason this belongs in a season when the church needs nothing.

---

## The three tracks

**The weekend.** Six messages, with outlines, illustrations, and Scripture supplied. Run this alone and the campaign still works — which is how most churches should start.

**The groups.** Six sessions, ninety minutes each. Participant guide and leader script for every week, written so a first-time host can lead it without training.

**The daily reader.** Forty readings, running the whole campaign. A story, a short teaching, an action step, and three reflection questions each day. Someone who never joins a group still moves with the church.

## Why now

The largest transfer of wealth in history is moving between generations, and almost all of it is being handled by attorneys rather than by anyone asking what it is for. Meanwhile most congregations only hear about money when the church needs some — which trains people to brace at the word *generosity* and to hear every mention of it as an appeal.

This campaign exists to break that pattern in your congregation. It disciples people out of fear rather than into the offering.

---

## A word about timing

Churches often ask whether to run this at the end of a fiscal year. Our counsel is no.

Run it in a season when the church needs nothing, and the congregation will believe you. Run it beside a budget appeal and they will read the whole six weeks as a setup — reasonably, because it usually is one. Giving frequently rises after this campaign. That is a side effect, and the moment it becomes the goal the campaign stops working.

If your church must run it near a funding season, say so from the platform in week one and keep every ask entirely outside the groups.

## What a pastor has to decide

**How many tracks.** One, two, or three. Most start with the weekend and add groups the second time.

**When.** Any season with nothing attached. January, post-Easter, and early fall all work well.

**Who tells the stories.** Six testimonies, three minutes each, no dollar amounts. Vary them deliberately — if all six come from the wealthiest households in the church, the campaign quietly teaches that generosity belongs to them.
