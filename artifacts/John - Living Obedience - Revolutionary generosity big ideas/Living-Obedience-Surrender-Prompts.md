## Before you generate — the cliché problem

The arms-outstretched figure is the single most photographed pose in Christian publishing. Your own Surrender cover did it well in 2002; ten thousand stock photos have done it badly since. Four things separate a great one from a stock one, and every prompt below builds them in:

**Weather does the work.** Calm sky is stock. Breaking storm, blowing rain, tearing cloud — that is drama.
**The figure is small.** Scale is what makes surrender read as surrender. A large figure is a portrait; a small one against something vast is a posture.
**Always from behind.** No faces. It keeps the reader in the frame rather than watching someone else, and it removes a permissions problem.
**Found light, never lit.** One sun, one break in the cloud. The instant it looks studio-lit, it looks bought.

## How to choose

**Generate four of each.** The gap between the second-best and the best generation is usually the entire cover.

**Judge the bottom of the frame first.** If the lower third is busy, discard it however good the top is — that is where LIVING OBEDIENCE has to sit, and no compositing rescues a crowded floor.

**Then judge scale.** If the figure feels big, regenerate with "far smaller in the frame, tiny against the landscape." Small reads as surrender; large reads as achievement, which is the opposite of the book.

**My ranking for this book:** 02 Prostrate, then 01 Headland, then 05 Kneeling Field. Prostrate is the truest picture of the thing and almost nobody uses it. Headland is the safe, proven, Surrender-lineage choice. Summit is the most commercial and the most seen.

**Send the frames back and I will composite** the family type — Signatry eyebrow, title, gold rule, subtitle, author block — plus the family grain and vignette, and deliver finished covers plus the strip.

## If a generation misses

Never fix it in the composite. Regenerate, and add the failed constraint stated positively:

- Figure too large → *"the figure is very small in the frame, dwarfed by the landscape"*
- Bottom too busy → *"the lower third of the image is nearly empty, deep shadow with one soft pool of warm light"*
- Too calm → *"violent breaking storm cloud, strong wind, dramatic contrast between shadow and one shaft of light"*
- Face visible → *"seen entirely from behind, the face is not visible"*
- Looks like stock → *"documentary, unposed, found natural light, imperfect and real"*
