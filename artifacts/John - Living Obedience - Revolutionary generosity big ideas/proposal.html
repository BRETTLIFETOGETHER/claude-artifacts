Prepared for Dale Armstrong & the Board of The Signatry  ·  August 2026

The board has approved a book. This proposal is that book — and an honest account of what it could become if The Signatry decides it wants more than a book.

Everything on page one is ready to commission today. Pages two and three are there so the board can see the whole field before it chooses how much of it to play.

Why this title
Living Obedience is not a generosity book. Generosity is what flows out of it — which is exactly why it will reach people a generosity book never reaches.

Obedience is the capstone idea and the counterintuitive one. Living matters as much as obedience: it is a participle, a pathway, a daily practice rather than a single decision. For families of significant capacity, that distinction is the difference between a message they admire and one they can actually enter.

A realistic range
Commissioned in full, Tier Two runs roughly $430,000 to $600,000 across 24–30 months. Commissioned in sequence — one volume and the campaign in year two, the film series in year three — it is a manageable annual line rather than a single decision.

The progression

Living Obedience is the yes. Lifestyle Obedience is the day after. Living Obedience Together is the household. The Living Pledge is the yes put in writing and witnessed. Living Legacy is what outlives you. Living Leadership carries it into the enterprise.

Why a series rather than a sequel

One book reaches a reader once. A progression gives The Signatry a reason to be in a family's life for five years, and gives an advisor something to bring back a second and third time. The economics of the second volume are entirely different from the first.

Tier Three · The Platform, and why now

What only The Signatry can publish

The differentiator
Give · Grow · Grant. Every organization in this field can teach giving and granting. The Signatry is the only one that can write honestly about what happens to the money in between.

Twenty types of alternative investment in five years. General partner positions in operating businesses. Donor capital curated and grown rather than parked. That is the chapter no other publisher in this category can commission — and it is what turns Living Obedience from a good book anyone could have written into the book only this house could produce.

It also argues for doing it now rather than later. What this project really captures is an operating system, in the words of the people who built it, while they are still building it.

Tier Three

The platform — proposed with no fee attached

Licensing to churches and advisory firms. The tool library. The enterprise and cohort model. An assessment that produces annual published data.

LifeTogether does not propose to charge The Signatry to build this. We propose to build and operate it at our own cost and risk, co-branded, on a revenue participation. If it works, both organizations earn. If it doesn't, the board has paid nothing for it. That structure is deliberate — the board should not be asked to fund a platform on faith, and we should not ask to be paid for something we believe will pay for itself.
