How It Works

Four steps. Your time required: far less than you'd think.

i

Discover

A conversation about what exists — manuscripts, recordings, notes — and a simple agreement that keeps you as the owner of every word.

ii

Organize

We collect, transcribe, and tag your material against the themes and needs your congregation actually faces.

iii

Verify

A human editor checks every citation and quote before anything moves forward. Nothing goes to your church unchecked.

iv

Produce

Your first finished resource — a devotional, a small group series, or the start of a book — designed and, if you choose, printed.
