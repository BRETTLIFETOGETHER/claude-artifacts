Every Family Leaves One

"A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children's children."Proverbs 13:22 · KJV

Here is a sentence worth sitting with before the coffee is finished: your family is already leaving a legacy. Not will leave. Is leaving. Right now, in the way this morning started, in what got talked about at dinner last night and what got avoided, in what your children or grandchildren watch you reach for when the day gets hard — something is being handed down.

Notice what Proverbs does not say. It does not say a rich man leaves an inheritance for his children's children. It says a good one. Scripture quietly moves inheritance out of the category of net worth and into the category of intention.

Tonight, At Your Table

Go around the table once, everyone answers: "What is one thing — good or bad — that our family would pass on if nothing ever changed?" No fixing, no defending. Just listen.

Pray

Father, You have been thinking about my children's children longer than I have. Open my eyes today to what our family is already handing down — the good I never noticed and the drift I never meant…
