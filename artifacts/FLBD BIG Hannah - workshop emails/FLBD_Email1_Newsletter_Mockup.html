70% of family wealth is gone by the second generation.

And the #1 reason isn't financial. It's relational.

[ Hero photo — e.g., Tom in conversation with a client ]

The number every advisor should sit with

By the third generation, it's 97%. The reflex is to call it a planning failure — the wrong structure, the wrong documents. It almost never is. The research points somewhere else entirely: the number-one cause is a breakdown in communication and trust.

Which means the thing most likely to undo even the best plan isn't technical. It's relational — and it shows up in a moment most advisors have already felt, when a conversation could go deeper but it's not clear how to take it there without overstepping. Most advisors were never trained for that part.

The workshop walks through all five — with a structure you can run with a real client the week you get home.
