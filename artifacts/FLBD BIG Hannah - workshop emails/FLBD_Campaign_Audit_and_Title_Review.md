## PART B — Title & clarity review

Audience reminder: older (55–65+), faith-leaning, established advisors serving wealthy multi-generational families. They value clarity and gravitas over clever/consumer phrasing. (Russ previously flagged an unclear title — so we're pressure-testing.)

Legend: ✅ keep · ⚠️ review · 🔴 rework

| # | Current title | Call | Notes & alternatives |
|---|---------------|------|----------------------|
| 1 | 70% of it disappears. Most advisors miss why. | 🔴 | "Miss why" reads as condemning (already flagged). **Alts:** "70% of family wealth disappears. Here's where you come in." · "The conversation your clients didn't need — until now." · "Why most family plans quietly fall apart." |
| 2 | This one's in your backyard — SoCal, Aug 12–13 | ⚠️ | "Backyard"/"SoCal" casual. **Alts:** "We're coming to Southern California — Aug 12–13." · "Two days in Southern California, close to home." |
| 3 | The 5 conversations families avoid | ✅ | Clear, concrete. (Optional: "The 5 conversations most families never have.") |
| 4 | A sample of what you'll actually practice | ⚠️ | Vague/awkward. **Alts:** "Try this question in your next client meeting." · "One question that changes the conversation." |
| 5 | How do you actually start this with a client? | ✅* | Proven performer (25.2% opens). *Keep, but the preview must carry the context ("this" is vague alone). |
| 6 | Advisors near you are already doing this | ⚠️ | Another vague "this." **Alts:** "Advisors in your region are already leading these conversations." · "How peers near you handle the hard conversations." |
| 7 | "There was no manual. Now there is." | ✅ | Strong, quote-driven, fits the brand's gravitas. |
| 8 | Discount ends [DATE] — SoCal seats | ⚠️ | Functional; just "SoCal" → "Southern California seats." |
| 9 | Will this actually grow your practice? | ⚠️ | Slightly salesy/skeptical; faith-leaning advisors may prefer service framing. **Alts:** "What this does for your practice — honestly." · "Is this worth two days?" · "Will this change how you serve families?" |
| 10 | Half the room is full | ✅ | Clear, good urgency. |
| 11 | What you'll do Monday, not "someday" | ⚠️ | Clever but consumer-y for this audience. **Alts:** "A process you'll use the week you get home." · "Practical from day one." |
| 12 | Your SoCal plan: venue, travel, what to bring | ⚠️ | Just "SoCal" → "Your Southern California plan: venue, travel, what to bring." |
| 13 | Last few local seats | ✅ | Clear urgency. |
| 14 | Final call: Aug 12–13 | ✅ | Clear. |
| 15 | Doors close [DATE] | ✅ | Clear, urgent (pairs with "last email about August"). |
| 16 | We start tomorrow | ⚠️ | Thin alone in an inbox. **Alts:** "The workshop starts tomorrow." · "Tomorrow: two days in Southern California." |

**Decision needed:** confirm keeps, and pick alternatives (or supply your own) for the ⚠️/🔴 rows.
