## 2. Strategic principles (the upgrades over the May campaign)

1. **Escalate, don't repeat.** Every email delivers one *new* useful thing. No recycled paragraphs.
2. **Teach in every send.** Give a tool/insight even to people who never register (a sample question, one of the 5 Areas, a clip with a specific takeaway).
3. **Make value concrete.** Name the 2-day schedule, the 5 Areas of Legacy, the Goals Conversation, the Family Review, the workbook you keep.
4. **Be transparent about investment + what's included**, and create a real deadline (a discount cutoff) to drive action.
5. **Prove scarcity.** Use a real cohort cap and seats-remaining language — not vague "limited seats" every time.
6. **Commit on faith positioning.** Faith-forward for warm/West Coast; values-based for broad.
7. **Subject + preview as a pair.** Preview extends the subject, never repeats it.
8. **One primary CTA per email.** Soft CTA (watch/read/download) early → hard CTA (register) later.
9. **Add 3–4 short articles** as fuel + evergreen credibility (ideas in §4).
10. **Segment.** West Coast = "in your backyard." Broad = "worth the trip + here's the value."
