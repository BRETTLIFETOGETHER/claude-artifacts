The number every advisor should sit with

70% of family wealth is gone by the second generation.

By the third, it's 97%.

The reflex is to assume it's a planning failure — the wrong structure, the wrong documents. It almost never is. The research points somewhere else entirely: the number-one cause is a breakdown in communication and trust.

Which means the thing most likely to undo even the best plan isn't technical. It's relational. It shows up in a moment most advisors have already felt — when a client conversation could go deeper, but it's not clear how to take it there without overstepping.

Most advisors were never trained for that part. Here's why it happens, and what actually changes it.
