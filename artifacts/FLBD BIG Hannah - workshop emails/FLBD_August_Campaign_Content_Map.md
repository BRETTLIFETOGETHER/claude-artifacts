## Send discipline (protects deliverability)

- **Saturday ~6am** = anchor (proven best slot); **Wednesday** = secondary
- **Batch large sends:** 6 batches, 15 min apart for full-list; 3 batches/10 min for segments
- **Never** two urgency emails back-to-back; **never** three "last call" emails (the spring mistake)
- **Segment where possible:** spring openers/clickers get the firmer asks first
- **Link-check every send** — a broken link cost ~762 clicks in spring
