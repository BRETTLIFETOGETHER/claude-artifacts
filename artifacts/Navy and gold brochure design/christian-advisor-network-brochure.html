Getting Started

How to Partner with a Local Church

1

Begin with Service, Not Sales

Ask what financial pressures your people face and what ministry the church would build if resources were available.

2

Learn the Pastor's Vision

Understand the church's mission, discipleship strategy, sermon calendar, and existing financial ministries before offering a program.

3

Offer One Practical Resource

Start with something useful — a sermon research packet, a 7-day devotional, or a family legacy worksheet — not a major campaign.

4

Establish Boundaries

Agree in writing on roles, content, promotion, data privacy, referrals, compliance, and use of the church name.

5

Pilot a Small Experience

Good first pilots include a 7-day financial wisdom devotional, a Marriage and Money seminar, or a Family Legacy workshop.

6

Measure Ministry Outcomes

Track family conversations started, giving commitments, and requests for pastoral care — not only leads and appointments.

7

Build an Annual Ministry Plan

A twelve-month rhythm: financial wisdom, marriage and family, purpose and generosity, legacy and year-end giving.
