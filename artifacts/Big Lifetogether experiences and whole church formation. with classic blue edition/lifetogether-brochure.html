I. The Question

Every church has a mission

Few have a comprehensive formation strategy.

Most churches are rich in biblical teaching — gifted pastors, faithful leaders, inspiring sermons, thriving ministries. Yet weekend messages, small groups, men's and women's ministry, students, children, marriage, stewardship, serving, and leadership often run as effective individual programs that were never asked to move in the same direction.

The result is good ministry — but fragmented discipleship. Whole-Church Formation™ replaces the question "what curriculum should we teach next?" with a larger one: how are we forming the people God has entrusted to our church?

30-Day Experience

VI. A Proven Architecture

This isn't a new format

The Book and the Curriculum.

The same daily-book-plus-weekly-guide architecture is the one behind 40 Days of Purpose — the campaign built on The Purpose Driven Life that ran in more than 30,000 churches and reached over 50 million people. What made it work wasn't only the message. It was the format.

A daily book shaped a person's morning. A weekly guide shaped a group's Tuesday night. Both ran off the same content, in the same season, reinforcing each other. That's the operating system LifeTogether is built on — and it's proven at a scale very few formats can claim.

The day count can change. Seven, twenty-one, thirty, forty — matched to a church's calendar, season, or the size of the moment. What doesn't change is the format underneath every length: daily reading, weekly conversation, weeks of community. The campaign experience can be shorter or longer. The architecture is constant.

Daily Spiritual Growth Journey

Call it what it is: a real book. Roughly 200 pages, full color, one reading for every day of the experience. It's a devotional. It's a journal. It's something a person underlines, dog-ears, and keeps on a nightstand long after the campaign ends — Scripture, teaching, reflection, prayer, and space to write.

Weekly Conversation Guide

The small-group curriculum a host actually opens on a Tuesday night — video or no video, six-plus sessions, built to run alongside the book or stand on its own for groups, classes, and ministry teams.

Either product can be purchased and used independently — two distinct products, two distinct revenue opportunities. The greatest transformation happens when they run together.

VII. The Smart Curriculum

Not just a discussion guide — an operating system for the group

What made the original curriculum "smart."

Rick Warren described the 40 Days of Purpose small-group curriculum as a smart curriculum — one with its own intelligent DNA. It didn't just prompt conversation. It gave every person in the group a next step and a role to grow into.

Attend
Show up, engage the material, take a next step personally.
Serve
Discover a place to contribute inside the group or the church.
Give
Practice generosity as a spiritual discipline, not just a request.
Discover
Understand their own design, gifts, and mission in life.
Host & Lead
Open a home, multiply the group, develop the next leader.

That's the standard we're building to — not a lighter version of a small group, but the same intelligent operating system, in a format a church can run at 7, 21, 30, or 40 days.
