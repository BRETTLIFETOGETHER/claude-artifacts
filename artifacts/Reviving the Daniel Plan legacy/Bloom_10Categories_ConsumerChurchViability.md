# BLOOM/VITALITY: 10 CORE CATEGORIES + 10 FLAGSHIP SERIES
## The Cascade Model: Body → Community → Meaning → Resilience → Spirit

**Core Positioning:**
"Christians have been told: 'Fix your mental health. Fix your marriage. Fix your finances. Fix your meaning.' We say: Start where the body is. Rest. Eat well. Move. Be with people. Watch what happens. Energy returns. Community forms. Belonging restores meaning. Meaning makes mental resilience possible. Resilience opens the capacity for spiritual depth and virtue. It's not linear. It's cascading. That's flourishing."

**Research Foundation:**
Byron Johnson (Baylor/Harvard): Religious community participation is the *strongest predictor* of flourishing across all domains—stronger than wealth, education, or health alone. Integration of physical health + spiritual formation + relational thriving = flourishing.
