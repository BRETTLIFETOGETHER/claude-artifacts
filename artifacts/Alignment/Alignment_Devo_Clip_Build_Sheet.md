**Session 4 needs a fifth clip.** Three strong passages exist in the main Session 4 recording and appear in none of the four cuts. In order of what I would take:

1. **The ninety-two-year-old and the twenty-five thousand people.** Three generations in the room, a billion-and-a-half-dollar company, eighty-five hundred employees, and the grandfather saying the planning is not about the fourteen people at the table. This is the strongest single story in the entire module and it is currently on the cutting room floor.
2. The chief executive with twenty-five million of company-owned life insurance and roughly ten million in avoidable estate tax.
3. The man with sixty million of coverage who no longer needs it because the estate is going to charity.

Take the first. It sits naturally at Day 19, between the coverage-drift material and the closing placement teaching.
