## Scriptures that are recommendations needing Tom's approval

Twenty-five. Fourteen of them are already inside Family Legacy by Design material, which means they are house scripture rather than imported. Eleven are outside it and carry more risk.

**Drawn from the Family Book, Coaching Book, or Training Manual, so already in the family of material:**

Proverbs 27:23 (Day 1), Ecclesiastes 11:2 (Day 6), Proverbs 21:5 (Day 9), Matthew 7:12 (Day 10), 1 Chronicles 29:11-12 (Day 13), Proverbs 6:6-8 (Day 14), Proverbs 21:20 (Day 15), 1 Timothy 5:8 (Day 16), Matthew 6:32 (Day 18), Philippians 4:19 (Day 20), Proverbs 22:7 (Day 22), 2 Corinthians 9:7 (Day 24), Romans 13:7 (Day 25), Proverbs 13:11 (Day 27).

**Not drawn from existing material and needing a closer look:**

Proverbs 15:22 (Day 3), Proverbs 20:21 (Day 4), Psalm 133:1 (Day 5), Proverbs 24:3 (Day 7), Proverbs 13:22 (Day 8), Habakkuk 2:2 (Day 11), Proverbs 27:1 (Day 12), Proverbs 14:15 (Day 17), Genesis 41:34-36 (Day 19), Luke 12:2 (Day 21), Luke 14:28 (Day 23).

Two of those need a second look for a specific reason. **Luke 12:2** is about the eventual disclosure of hidden things, spoken to disciples about hypocrisy, and is used here for the tax return as the most revealing document. The reading says out loud that Jesus is not talking about tax returns, but it is the longest reach in the set. **Proverbs 20:21** is rendered here as an inheritance claimed too soon; translations vary considerably on that verse and the wording should be checked against whichever version becomes the standard for the series.

**Translation standard.** Still unresolved across the whole project. Proverbs 21:5 is cited TLB, following the Family Book. Everything else reads NIV. This needs to be settled once for the curriculum and the devotional together.

## Attribution confidence

The six VTT files have no speaker labels. Every phrase used in this book was placed with Tom on the basis of context, and one was corrected during the build when the context showed it belonged to Brett. Before print, the thirty titles and thirty subtitles should be checked against the video by someone who can hear which voice is speaking. That is a two-hour job and it protects the central claim of the product, which is that these are Tom's words.
