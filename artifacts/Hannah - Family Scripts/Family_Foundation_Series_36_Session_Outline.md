# FAMILY FOUNDATION SERIES

## Six-Session Outline, All Six Books

Thirty-six sessions. Derived from the filmed coaching series, the Family Book,
the Training Manual, and the Workbook Legend. Session titles and subtitles are
proposed for lock. Each session carries its source, so nothing here is invented.

**Three things run through every session in every book**, per the pre-build audit:

- **A next-generation address.** Something written to a G2 or G3 reader, not only
  about them.
- **A family action.** One thing, specific, doable before the next session.
- **Scale neutrality.** Every principle works at $100,000 and at $100 million.

Sessions that require net new writing, or that carry an unresolved permission or
sourcing item, are flagged inline.

# BOOK 1 — FAMILY LEGACY BY DESIGN

*Leading Your Family from Legacy by Default to Legacy by Design*

## Session 1 — What Is a Legacy, Really?
### Understanding What You Are Already Passing On

The reality check: I will die, I will take nothing with me, I will probably die
at a time I did not anticipate, someone else will get my things, and I can only
decide before I die who gets them after. Legacy as something received and
something transmitted. Legacy is not primarily about money. The three kinds of
legacy: the one you received, the one you will pass on, and the one you are
living out today. Why the third one is the one that matters most.

*Source: Family Book ch. 1; Workbook Legend S1; Coaching Overview script S1*

**Family action.** Name one thing you received from the generation before you
that had nothing to do with money.

## Session 2 — The Family God Designed
### Why Your Family Was Built to Last Longer Than You

The modern view against the biblical view. Raise them up and kick them out,
against multiple generations loving and serving one another. Independence against
interdependence. God's covenant promise in Genesis 17. Deuteronomy 6 and Psalm
78. This was never about one generation.

*Source: Family Book ch. 2; Workbook Legend S1; filmed Meetings S3*

**Family action.** Ask each adult in your family what they believe the purpose of
your family is. Do not correct the answers.

> **New writing.** Replaces the coaching Session 2 on moving from advice to
> guidance, which has no family equivalent.

## Session 3 — The Legacy You Are Living Right Now
### What Your Family Sees When You Are Not Explaining Yourself

More is caught than taught. People do not replicate what you say, they replicate
what you live. Your children are watching, your grandchildren are watching, the
people you work with are watching. Modeling as the primary transfer mechanism.
The gap between what a family says matters and where the time and the money
actually go.

*Source: Family Book ch. 3; Coaching Overview script S1; filmed Clarity S2*

**Family action.** Ask someone who lives with you what they would say your family
values, based only on what they can see.

## Session 4 — Wealth and Wisdom
### Why One Without the Other Does Not Work

The twin W's. Every family passes on both, intentionally or not. Wealth
transferred without wisdom creates more problems than it solves. Biblical
stewardship: God owns it all and we manage it. Stewardship means management, not
giving. The inheritance question that runs through the whole series: a blessing
and not a curse.

*Source: Family Book ch. 4; Workbook Legend S1; filmed Clarity S4; filmed Next
Gen S4*

**Family action.** Write down what you hope an inheritance would actually do in
the life of each person who receives it.

## Session 5 — Why Families Drift
### Legacy by Default and How It Happens

Nobody plans to drift. The causes of generational breakdown: communication and
trust, unprepared heirs, and a lack of clarity about where the family is going.
The common points of failure and the common points of success, side by side.
Design against default.

*Source: Workbook Legend S1; filmed Next Gen S1; filmed Clarity S1*

**Family action.** Which of the common points of failure is most true of your
family right now. Just name it.

> **Blocked.** The statistics attribution decision has to be settled before this
> session is written. See the audit, Part 8.

## Session 6 — The Path Ahead
### Clarity, Alignment, and the Conversations That Carry Them

The three key words and why the order matters. The two goals: family harmony, and
the wise transfer of both wealth and wisdom. A preview of the five books that
follow and what each one asks of a family. What it looks like to begin.

*Source: Workbook Legend S1; filmed Clarity S1; filmed Alignment S3*

**Family action.** Choose which of the next five books your family starts with,
and put a date on the calendar.

# BOOK 2 — WHAT WILL YOU DO WITH WHAT YOU'VE BEEN GIVEN?

*Discovering Your Family's Values, Purpose, and Direction*

## Session 1 — The Five Areas of Your Legacy
### Bringing Focus to Something That Feels Too Big

Legacy is a broad word and most families never narrow it. The five areas:
personal, family, financial, business, and charitable. Why five and not one. The
disconnect that shows up when a successful family is asked what matters most and
gives five different answers. Clarity as the first of the three key words.

*Source: filmed Clarity S1; Family Book ch. 5; Workbook Legend S4*

**Family action.** Rate each of the five areas from one to ten. Which is clearest,
which is most undefined.

## Session 2 — Your Personal Legacy
### What Do You Want to Be Remembered For

Slowing down long enough to answer the questions nobody asks. What has shaped
your life. What has God entrusted to you and what are you meant to do with it.
Calling and gifting. The eulogy question and the obituary question. The gap
between what you say matters and how you are actually living.

*Source: filmed Clarity S2; Family Book ch. 6*

**Family action.** Write the two or three sentences you would want said about
your life. Show them to one person.

**Next-gen address.** The same questions, asked at the beginning of an adult life
rather than near the end of one.

## Session 3 — Your Family Legacy
### Who Your Family Is and How They Are Really Doing

Knowing the family. Who is actually in it, including the people who married in
and the values they brought with them. How each person is doing spiritually,
relationally, vocationally, financially. Shared understanding against individual
assumptions. What do you want this family to be known for.

*Source: filmed Clarity S3; Family Book ch. 5*

**Family action.** Walk the family health questions. Not to evaluate anyone, to
understand.

> **Permission item.** Source contains the six questions Tom asked his
> sons-in-law before they proposed.

## Session 4 — Your Financial Legacy
### A Blessing and Not a Curse

Not how much, but what for. What do you want this to accomplish in the life of
each person who receives it. Every child is different: love them equally and
treat them uniquely. Readiness, timing, and structure. Giving while you are
living so you can see where it goes.

*Source: filmed Clarity S4; Family Book ch. 7*

**Family action.** For each person who will receive something, write what you
hope it does in their life.

> **Scale rule applies hardest here.** Filmed source runs to $45 million.

## Session 5 — Your Work and Business Legacy
### What You Have Built and What Happens to It

For families who own a business and for families who lead in their work. The
business affects every other area of life, including the marriage. Continuity
against succession. Who is ready, who wants it, and who does not. The golden
goose. Why a last name is not a qualification.

*Source: filmed Clarity S5; Family Book ch. 8*

**Family action.** If you were unavailable for thirty days, who decides. Write
the name down.

> **Adapted.** Broadened from ownership to work and leadership so it lands for
> families without a business. Source supports it: Tom addresses business leaders
> as well as owners on camera.

## Session 6 — Your Charitable Legacy
### What Will You Do for the Kingdom, Now and at the End

Most families already give and few could describe what they are trying to
accomplish. Moving from responsive to intentional. What problem do you want to
help solve. Charity as another child. Giving as a family activity rather than a
private decision. Joy against obligation.

*Source: filmed Clarity S6; Family Book ch. 9*

**Family action.** Name the one or two things you actually care about most, and
give more to fewer places.

# BOOK 3 — GETTING ON THE SAME PAGE AS A FAMILY

*Aligning Your Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most*

Six sessions, one per letter of FAMILY. Lighter cut throughout: the unit of work
is the conversation, not the document review. See the audit, Part 5.

## Session 1 — What Do We Own and What Do We Owe
### F is for Financial Statement

Introduces the acrostic. Most families do not know what they have, what it is
worth, or who owns it. A financial statement is what you own minus what you owe.
The husband and wife conversation that comes before the family conversation.
Goals and gaps: where you are against where you said you wanted to be.

*Source: filmed Alignment S1; Family Book ch. 10; Training Manual S6*

**Family action.** Both spouses list what you own and what you owe, separately.
Then compare.

## Session 2 — Is Everything Pulling the Same Direction
### A is for Assets Under Management

Families build over time, with different decisions made at different moments.
Each piece can make sense on its own and still not work together. Who owns what.
How things will actually move from one generation to the next, which is often not
what anyone assumed. Integration rather than performance.

*Source: filmed Alignment S2*

**Family action.** For each major asset, write down who owns it and where it goes.

## Session 3 — Do Our Documents Say What We Would Say
### M is for Mandatory Documents

If it is not written down it does not happen, no matter what you meant. The state
has a document ready for you and you will not like it. Documents go out of date
because families change. The three myths of estate planning. Who you have named,
and whether they know you named them.

*Source: filmed Alignment S3; Family Book ch. 10*

**Family action.** Find your documents. Read who is named. Call them.

## Session 4 — What Are We Actually Protecting
### I is for Insurance Coverage

Not a product conversation, a protection conversation. What are you protecting
and does the coverage still match the life you are living now. Who owns it, who
is insured, who benefits. The perspective shift from the people in the room to
everyone who depends on what you have built.

*Source: filmed Alignment S4*

**Family action.** Name what you are protecting. Then check whether the coverage
still matches it.

## Session 5 — What Is Attached to What We Have Built
### L is for Liabilities and Taxes

Families look at what they have built and rarely at what is attached to it. Debt
shapes freedom. Taxes shape what is actually available. The tax return as the
most revealing document in the house. Planning ahead rather than reacting under
pressure.

*Source: filmed Alignment S5*

**Family action.** Look at last year's return together. Not to judge, to
understand.

## Session 6 — Where Is Our Money Actually Going
### Y is for Yearly Cash Flow

The five uses of money: lifestyle, giving, taxes, debt, savings. There is no such
thing as an independent financial decision. Values against valuables. Living
within your means at any scale. Where your treasure is, there your heart will be
also.

*Source: filmed Alignment S6; Training Manual S6*

**Family action.** Allocate the five uses for the coming year. Revisit in six
months.

# BOOK 4 — CONVERSATIONS THAT MATTER

*Why Families Don't Talk, and How to Start*

> **Thinnest source in the series.** Four of these six sessions are assembled
> from the other five titles rather than derived from a Communication source.
> Boundaries with Books 5 and 6 have to be drawn before any copy is written. See
> the audit, Part 6.

## Session 1 — Why Families Don't Talk About What Matters
### The Conversations That Never Happen

Families that love each other, see each other constantly, and still leave the
important things unsaid for years. Not avoidance, uncertainty. Anticipating how
it will go and delaying instead. The conversation does not disappear, it gets
more complicated. What happens when people are kept in the dark.

*Source: Coaching Communication script S2; filmed Meetings S1*

**Family action.** Name one conversation your family has been putting off. Only
name it.

> **New writing.** Replaces the coaching Session 1 on the advisor as conversation
> guide, which has no family equivalent.

## Session 2 — Understanding, Not Agreement
### The Goal Is Not to Win

Agreement is transactional. Understanding is relational and leads to planning and
problem solving. Not just what did they say, but why did they say it. The way to
understanding is empathetic listening. Trust as the foundation underneath: when
it is missing, everything above it stays guarded.

*Source: Workbook Legend S5; Coaching Overview script S4; filmed Next Gen S3*

**Family action.** In your next disagreement, ask one question before you respond.

## Session 3 — Learning to Listen
### What Happens When Someone Finally Feels Heard

Listening to understand, not to judge and not to reply. Open-ended questions, and
the follow-up question that matters more than the first one. Giving space and not
filling the silence. Making sure both spouses actually speak. Drawing out the
person who has not said anything.

*Source: filmed Clarity S1 and S2; filmed Meetings S3*

**Family action.** Ask one person a question and say nothing until they are
completely finished.

## Session 4 — Saying It Well
### Direct, Truthful, and Respectful

The three keys to biblical communication. Direct without being harsh. Speaking
truth in love. Watching for triangulation. Vain imaginations: filling a silence
with a story nobody has confirmed. Humility, and what it does to a family when a
parent asks to be forgiven.

*Source: Workbook Legend S5 and S11; filmed Next Gen S3*

**Family action.** Find one assumption you have been carrying and check it with
the person it is about.

## Session 5 — The Hard Conversations
### Money, Expectations, and Conflict

The topics everyone is thinking about and nobody says out loud. Talking about
wealth and what it represents to each person. Conflict is not always bad and
sometimes brings the truth out. Avoidance against escalation. Knowing the moment
to bring someone in.

*Source: Coaching Communication script S4; filmed Meetings S2; filmed Clarity S4*

**Family action.** Have the conversation you named in Session 1. Or set the date.

## Session 6 — Keeping It Going
### One Conversation Creates Clarity, a Pattern Creates a Family

A family is not changed by one conversation. Trust gets built, falls down, and
gets rebuilt. Rhythms of connection that are not meetings. Praying for one
another as ongoing communication. What this looks like over years rather than
weeks.

*Source: Coaching Communication script S6; filmed Meetings S6; filmed Clarity S2*

**Family action.** Ask each person for one thing you can pray about this year.
Write it down. Report back next year.

# BOOK 5 — FAMILY MEETINGS THAT BRING YOU TOGETHER

*Creating a Space for Honest Conversations, Alignment, and Growth*

## Session 1 — Why Have a Family Meeting at All
### There Is Going to Be a Family Meeting

There is going to be a family meeting, the only question is whether you will be
there for it. Not complicated and not formal. Creating space that does not happen
on its own. Why families delay: they are anticipating how it will go. Purpose
before structure. If something is worth doing, it is worth doing poorly.

*Source: filmed Meetings S1; Meetings script S1; Family Book ch. 12*

**Family action.** Pick a date. That is the entire assignment.

## Session 2 — Different Ways to Gather
### Not Every Meeting Should Look the Same

Four kinds of meeting: learning, alignment, decision making, and long term
planning. Four categories of topic: personal, business, estate, philanthropic.
Matching the format to the purpose instead of trying to cover everything at once.
Including spouses and in-laws, and exactly why that matters.

*Source: filmed Meetings S2; Training Manual S10*

**Family action.** Decide what kind of meeting your first one is. Only one kind.

## Session 3 — Who Leads and How
### When Dad Runs the Meeting, Everyone Already Knows the Answer

The patriarch who does not know he intimidates his children, and the wife who
confirms it. Why the person with the most authority should not always hold the
chair. Rotating facilitation around the family. Ground rules that create safety.
Two votes out of ten. Splitting the room when it helps.

*Source: filmed Meetings S2, S3, and S5*

**Family action.** Decide who leads the first one, and let it be someone other
than the person who usually decides.

> **Most affected session in the series.** In the coaching edition the
> facilitator is the advisor. Here the family has to answer it themselves. This
> is where the audit's central design problem gets solved or does not.

## Session 4 — What to Talk About
### Choosing One or Two Things That Actually Matter

Conversations expand and then drift, and the drift is never dramatic, it is
subtle. Acknowledging what someone said without letting it take over. Deciding
what belongs in this meeting and what waits for the next one. Family fights are
rarely about the money. They are about the china, the jewelry, and the guns.

*Source: filmed Meetings S2 and S4*

**Family action.** Write the agenda. Two items maximum.

## Session 5 — Staying Anchored in What Matters Most
### Your Family's Values, Mission, and Vision

A separate and longer gathering, half a day at least, where everyone speaks into
it. Writing the vision down and making it plain. Keeping it visible so it shapes
decisions instead of sitting in a drawer. Coming back to it: is this still true,
and how are we actually doing against it.

*Source: filmed Meetings S5; filmed Clarity S3*

**Family action.** Schedule the values conversation. Half a day. Everyone in the
room.

## Session 6 — Building a Rhythm That Lasts
### Staying Connected Between the Gatherings

A meeting is an event. Rhythm is what turns it into something that continues.
What works depends entirely on the family: annual, semi-annual, a standing call,
a prayer time over video, a trip everyone will show up for. Geography and full
calendars. It does not have to be a burden. It does have to happen at least once.

*Source: filmed Meetings S6; Family Book ch. 12*

**Family action.** Choose one rhythm and start it this month.

# BOOK 6 — PREPARING YOUR NEXT GENERATION

*Helping Your Children Grow in Wisdom, Purpose, and Responsibility*

## Session 1 — From Heirs to Stewards
### The Difference Between Receiving and Being Responsible

An heir receives. A steward is responsible. Scripture does not call us heirs, it
calls us stewards. Communication builds trust, and trust is what makes
preparation possible. Adult children as an oxymoron: are you treating them as
adults or as children. Growing adults. Why the next generation may well do more
than you did.

*Source: filmed Next Gen S1; Family Book ch. 11*

**Family action.** Tell one adult child something about your family they do not
currently know.

**Next-gen address.** What it means to receive something you did not build.

## Session 2 — Passing On Faith and Values
### What Does Not Transfer Automatically

Money transfers through documents. Faith and values transfer through
intentionality, conversation, and example. Modeling as the single most important
thing you do. Teaching along the way, when you sit down and when you walk. The
culture is discipling your children whether you are or not. Service as a
formative practice.

*Source: filmed Next Gen S2; Family Book ch. 11*

**Family action.** Tell one story about a decision that shaped your faith. Out
loud, to your family.

## Session 3 — Building Trust Across Generations
### Why Assumptions Fill Every Silence

Sixty percent of generational breakdown traces to communication and trust. Vain
imaginations and catastrophe thinking. The parent who believes the child is
disengaging and the child who believes they are not trusted, and neither one
says it. Trust gets built, falls down, and gets rebuilt. Humility, and asking to
be forgiven.

*Source: filmed Next Gen S3*

**Family action.** Ask each person whether there is anyone in the family they
have something unresolved with.

## Session 4 — Teaching Them to Handle What They Will Receive
### Stewardship Means Management, Not Giving

The three jars: give, save, spend. The five things you can do with money. There
is no such thing as an independent financial decision. Starting when the amounts
are small. Matching what they save. Co-ownership rather than provision. Giving
real responsibility before the responsibility arrives.

*Source: filmed Next Gen S4; Training Manual S6*

**Family action.** Give one real financial responsibility to someone in the next
generation.

> **Permission item.** Carries the Tom family material: his father matching every
> saved dollar, the Renault, and the college food budget that went to Croatia.

## Session 5 — Helping Them Find Their Calling
### Giving Them Freedom to Be Different From You

Every child is different. Finding what they are gifted and passionate about
rather than fitting them into the box you built. Observation over direction. The
courage to let them go a different way than you would choose. Sometimes God makes
what you should not do clearer than what you should. Praying about it with them,
not just about them.

*Source: filmed Next Gen S5; Family Book ch. 11*

**Family action.** Ask one person what they would do if nothing at all were
expected of them.

**Next-gen address.** How to say what you actually want without feeling like you
are disappointing the people who raised you.

## Session 6 — Growing the Whole Person
### Six Areas, Not One

Spiritual, family, financial, physical, vocational, social. Preparing someone
financially and nowhere else creates an imbalance that shows up later. Rating
yourself and then coming back to it. Who is developing your children: you, their
friends, or their feeds. The question is not what they will manage. It is who
they will become.

*Source: filmed Next Gen S6; Training Manual S9*

**Family action.** Everyone rates themselves across the six areas. Compare in six
months.

> **Carries the Assessment instrument.** Resolve the zero-to-ten against
> one-to-five conflict before writing.

**Books 2, 3, and 6 map almost cleanly.** They sit on the four filmed titles and
the session architecture already works for a family audience once the language
and the unit of work change.

**Books 1 and 4 need net new sessions**, both in the same place and for the same
reason: the coaching editions open by teaching an advisor how to be an advisor,
and there is nothing to translate.

**Book 5 Session 3 is the pivot point** for the whole series. It is where the
family has to answer the question the advisor answered in every coaching title.
