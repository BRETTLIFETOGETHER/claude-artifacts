# COACHING FOUNDATION SERIES — SESSION OPENS

## Book 1 (Family Legacy Coaching by Design) and Book 4 (Communication)

Built to the open pattern extracted from the six filmed Clarity transcripts.

**Placement:** each open replaces the current cold open and runs before the existing
`BRETT AND TOM CENTER` block. The existing Brett teaching paragraph becomes the second
beat, or gets absorbed. Tom's first scripted line follows the handoff question unchanged.

**Camera:** BRETT CENTER for the full open. TURN on the handoff question to Tom.

---

# THE PATTERN (reusable for all remaining titles)

Six moves, in this order, 150 to 250 words.

**1. Series welcome.** "Welcome back to Family Legacy Coaching by Design."
Brett says *welcome back* even on a title kickoff, then adds that the series itself
is brand new. Full name and short name ("Family Legacy Coaching") both appear on film.
Alternate them so it does not sound canned.

**2. Name the pair.** "My name is Brett, and this is Tom." First session of a title
may carry the full Tom credential. Every other session keeps it to a clause.

**3. Gratitude to the viewer.** Honored, grateful, glad you are back. Vary the wording.

**4. Locate the session.** Name the series, name this session's topic, recap the prior
session. Final session of a title recaps the whole arc.

**5. Address both audiences.** Advisors serving families AND the patriarch or matriarch
in their own family. Never advisor-only. This is the move most easily dropped.

**6. Hand to Tom by name with an open question.** Warm and expansive, not technical.
