## NOTES

**Short names.** Books 2 through 5 take the Family prefix naturally. Book 6 does not, since "Family Next Generation" reads awkwardly. Book 1 is the overview, so "Family Legacy Overview" distinguishes it from the series name itself.

**Book descriptions.** Books 1, 3, 4, and 5 use the one-line descriptions already in your scripts. Books 2 and 6 have no description in their scripts; those two lines are written.

**Two book titles differ from your scripts.** Book 1's script says *Building a Multi-Generational Legacy That Lasts.* Book 4's script title line reads *Alignment in Action Leading Conversations That Matter,* with Book 3's title welded on. Your title sheet is used above for both.

**Two session titles come from contents pages rather than headers.** Book 4 Session 5 and Book 5 Session 3. In both cases the session header inside the script says something different.
