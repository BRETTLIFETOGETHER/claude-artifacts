## BUILD NOTES

**All six sessions complete.** Book 2, Clarity, family edition.

**Word counts.** Roughly 1,700 to 1,900 per session. The coaching scripts ran
1,100 to 1,800. These sit at the top of that band because of the three family
additions: safety language, the next-generation address, and the family action.

**Story cues.** Fourteen across the six sessions. Each marks a place where Tom
told a real story on camera in the filmed Clarity series and where the family
edition needs one at family scale. Sessions 4 and 6 carry the most, because those
are the two sessions where his filmed material was densest and also where the
dollar figures were largest.

**Scenario device.** Two per session, introduced by Brett, with "let me give you
another version of this" as the handoff. Carried from the coaching scripts and
confirmed in the filmed sessions.

**Scale rule applied.** Every scenario is constructed rather than drawn from a
real family, which keeps them clear of permissions. Where Tom's filmed story is
the point, it is left as a cue rather than written out with figures. The one
place a number survives is Session 4's close, where Tom's own line about a
hundred thousand or five hundred thousand being a huge amount to a normal family
is the argument for scale neutrality, so it stays.

**Scripture.** NASB. Session 1 Proverbs 20:5, Session 2 Ephesians 2:10, Session 3
Psalm 133:1, Session 4 Proverbs 13:22, Session 5 Colossians 3:23, Session 6
Matthew 25:21 with John 15 paraphrased in Tom's close.

**Series naming.** Across all twenty-four filmed sessions, the marketing titles
are never spoken. Not once. Brett names the series by its plain topic: "family
clarity," "family alignment," "family meetings," "preparing the next generation."
The opens here follow that. Session 1 uses "a brand new series on family
clarity," close to verbatim from filmed Clarity Session 1, and Session 2 uses "a
series on family clarity," verbatim from filmed Clarity Session 2.

If you want the family edition to speak the curriculum title on camera so the
video and the book connect for a buyer, that is a deliberate departure from the
coaching pattern and should be set once for all six books rather than per
session.

**Title convention.** One set of titles, used on both the script and the
curriculum. Length and style match the coaching scripts. Four of the six
subtitles come from on-camera language rather than from me.

**Applies to the other five books.** The thirty-six-session outline carries
longer subtitles on several titles, written before this convention was set. Those
need the same pass: trim to the coaching band, and pull from filmed language
wherever it exists. Book 6 will be easiest since every Next Generation session
was named on camera.
