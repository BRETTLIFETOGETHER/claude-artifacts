Book titles follow the locked title sheet. Coaching session titles for Books 1,
4, and 5 are exact from the scripts. Coaching session titles for Books 2, 3, and

said on camera and written to match the house style of the three scripted books.

- ✅ exact from an existing script
- ⬜ proposed, no formal title exists
- ⚠️ defect found during comparison, listed in full at the end
- ★ recommended change to the locked title sheet, listed in full at the end

# ⚠️ DEFECTS FOUND

**1. Book 1 coaching subtitle does not match the title sheet.** The script says
*Building a Multi-Generational Legacy That Lasts.* The sheet says *Guiding
Families to Move from Legacy by Default to Legacy by Design.*

**2. Book 1 coaching contents page** reads "Helping Clients Clarifying Values and
Priorities." The session header itself is correct with "Clarify." Contents page
only.

**3. Book 4 coaching title line reads "Alignment in Action Leading Conversations
That Matter."** Two book titles concatenated. "Alignment in Action" belongs to
Book 3 and has to come off.

**4. Book 4 coaching Session 5 exists in two versions.** Contents page says
"Facilitating Family Conversations," session header says "Facilitating Family
Dialogue."

**5. Book 5 coaching Session 3 exists in three versions.** Contents page says
"Leading Meaningful Conversations," session header says "Facilitating Meaningful
Conversations," and on camera it is "Facilitating Meaningful Conversation."

**6. Eighteen coaching sessions have no formal title.** All of Books 2, 3, and 6.
They were filmed without a titled script, so the only naming that exists is
descriptive language spoken on camera. Everything marked ⬜ above is proposed and
needs approval before it can go to layout, packaging, or the certification
pathway.

# ★ RECOMMENDED CHANGES TO THE TITLE SHEET

**★1 — Alignment subtitles, both editions.** Currently promise values, direction,
and shared action. The six filmed sessions are financial statements, assets under
management, mandatory documents, insurance, liabilities and taxes, and cash flow.
Recommend surfacing the acrostic:

- Family: *Aligning Your Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most*
- Coaching: *Helping Families Align Their Documents, Decisions, and Resources with What Matters Most*

**★2 — Family Communication subtitle** uses an em-dash, against the series
editorial rule. Recommend *Why Families Don't Talk, and How to Start.*

**★3 — Family Book 6 title.** "Raising the Next Generation" contradicts the
content. The title teaches that adult children is an oxymoron and pushes parents
to stop treating grown children as children. Brett coins "growing adults" on
camera in Session 1. Recommend **Preparing Your Next Generation**, which also
mirrors the you/they pivot the Clarity pair already does well.
