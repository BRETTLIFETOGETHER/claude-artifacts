# LIFETOGETHER TAXONOMY · 100 · THE MASTER CATEGORIES
### Categories 51–100 — Doubling the Original 50

*Brett Eastman · Lifetogether · brett@lifetogether.com*

The original 50 Master Curriculum Categories covered the formation, relational, health, leadership, worship, and cultural-engagement core of church life. These 50 new categories (51–100) extend the taxonomy into church planting and multiplication, family complexity, specific recovery pathways, ministry to overlooked populations, justice and compassion, creative and technical arts, church operations, leader development, the global and historical church, and emerging frontiers — doubling the platform to **100 master categories** and **1,000 representative series titles** at 10 per category, scaling to 4,000+ at full depth.
