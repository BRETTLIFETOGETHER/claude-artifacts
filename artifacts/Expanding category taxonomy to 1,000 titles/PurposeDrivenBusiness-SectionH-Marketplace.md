*Building an organization that develops leaders faster than it loses them*
**Corporate parallel to:** Category 54, Church Multiplication & Reproducing Leaders

### PRIORITY 3 — Multi-Location & Franchise Operations
*Remote offices, franchise units, regional managers, culture transfer, and leading one company in many locations*
**Corporate parallel to:** Category 52, Multisite & Campus Ministry

The multi-location model solves a real capacity problem, but creates a new one: how does one company's culture and standards transfer faithfully across locations leadership can't be in every week? This category addresses the leadership and systems questions that determine whether a multi-location company stays genuinely one company or slowly fragments into disconnected outposts. **Moderate-high priority** — essential for companies actively expanding physical footprint, less relevant to single-location businesses.

*Leading a plateaued, declining, or struggling company back to health, focus, and momentum*
**Corporate parallel to:** Category 53, Church Revitalization & Turnaround
