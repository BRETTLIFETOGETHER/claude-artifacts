# FAMILY SERIES CONTENTS
## Review Against the Source Library
Six titles, thirty-six sessions. What is sourced, what collides, what is missing.
## WHAT IS STRONG
**The parallel structure holds.** Six family titles against six advisor titles, matched theme for theme. An advisor certified in the advisor series can hand a family the corresponding family title without explaining anything, and that is the whole business model expressed as a table of contents.

**Family Clarity is the cleanest title in the set.** Sessions 2 through 6 map one to one onto Tom's Five Areas of Legacy: Personal, Family, Financial, Business, Kingdom or Charitable. Session 1 supplies the why. Nothing in that title needs sourcing work because the framework already exists in the Workbook Legend and the Coaching Book, and the naming matches.

**Family Alignment is the FAMILY Review, and using it as a spine is a good call.** The acronym is canonical and the sessions follow it. It also gives that title the most concrete, least abstract content in the family series, which matters because it is the one a skeptical business owner will pick up first.

**Next Generation follows the Six Areas of Life.** Also canonical.

**Success to significance is sourced.** It appears in the Coaching Book, in Tom's own line about moving from success to significance and from portfolios to people. No attribution problem.

## COLLISIONS
### 1. Two titles now share a name with a book
*Family Legacy by Design* is the title of the existing family-facing companion book. It is now also the title of the family foundation guide.

This is the second instance of the same problem. On the advisor side, *Family Legacy Coaching by Design* is the Coaching Book and was also proposed as the guide title, which is why the guide was locked as *Legacy Coaching by Design*.

**Recommended fix, parallel to the advisor side:** the guide takes a distinct title and the book keeps *Family Legacy by Design*. Options that preserve the series voice: **Legacy by Design: The Family Guide**, or **A Family Legacy by Design**, or move the existing subtitle up. The pairing to aim for is guide and book that are obviously related and not identically named.

### 2. Alignment means two different things in the two series
In the advisor series, Alignment is relational. The framework is **Understanding, Not Agreement**, and the session is about bringing generations to shared understanding.

In the family series, Family Alignment is technical. It is the FAMILY Review: statements, assets, documents, insurance, taxes, cash flow.

Both are defensible. The Goals and Gaps Analysis is genuinely about aligning a plan to stated values, so plan alignment is a legitimate reading of the word. But an advisor who has completed the advisor Alignment title and then hands a family the Family Alignment title will find the two have almost nothing in common.

**Two ways out.** Rename title 3 to something that signals the technical content, such as **Family Plan Alignment** or **Aligning the Plan**. Or keep the name and place the relational framework inside it, most naturally as Session 1 in place of The Purpose of Planning.

### 3. Family Meetings appears twice
Family Communication Session 5 is *Family Meetings Conversations*, and title 5 is *Family Meetings* in full. A whole session previewing a whole title is defensible as a bridge, but as written it reads as duplication rather than as a doorway.

**Recommended fix:** rename the session to signal that it is the on-ramp, along the lines of *Preparing for the First Family Meeting*, and let it end by naming the deeper title.

### 4. Stage One is split across two titles
In the source, Stage 1 has three steps in order: the Goals Conversation, the review of the existing plan using the FAMILY Review, and then the Goals and Gaps Analysis as the deliverable.

In this structure the FAMILY Review is title 3 and the Goals and Gaps Conversation is title 4, Session 2. The deliverable has been separated from the input that produces it, and a family will meet the gaps analysis before they have done the review it depends on.

**Recommended fix:** move Goals and Gaps into Family Alignment, where the FAMILY Review already lives, most naturally as the closing session. Family Communication then gains a slot, which the next item needs.
## GAPS
### 1. Understanding, Not Agreement has no home anywhere in the family series
This is the sharpest framework in the entire system. Agreement is transactional, understanding is relational, and the way to understanding is empathetic listening. It is the ground rule that lets a family talk without bracing for a fight, and it is the single most transferable idea Tom teaches.

Scanning all thirty-six family sessions, there is no obvious place it lands.

**Recommended fix:** Family Communication Session 1, currently *Why Families Struggle to Communicate*. That session is diagnostic and needs a resolution, and this framework is the resolution.

### 2. The Reality Check has no home
The five realities of life, read out loud, ending on the only decision available. It is the instrument Tom uses to open with a family and it is the family-facing equivalent of the commercial case: it establishes stakes once, early, without sentiment.

**Recommended fix:** Family Legacy by Design, Session 1. It belongs at the very front of the family series and nowhere else.

### 3. The Three Keys to Communication and the triangulation warning
Be Direct, Be Truthful, Be Respectful, with the warning about two people discussing a third. Nothing in the Family Communication contents names them.

**Recommended fix:** Family Communication Session 1 alongside Understanding Not Agreement, or Family Meetings Session 3, which is about creating an environment where every voice can be heard.

### 4. Two of the Six Areas of Life are unnamed
Next Generation Sessions 2 through 5 name Spiritual, Family, Financial, and Vocational. Physical and Social are folded into Session 6, *Whole-Life Formation*.

That works and it is not wrong. The alternative is naming all six, which would require either seven sessions or combining Family with Social.

**Recommended default: keep six sessions and name the fold explicitly in the Session 6 subtitle**, so a reader who has seen the six areas elsewhere does not think two went missing.

### 5. The FAMILY acronym is six letters across five sessions
Insurance and Liabilities and Taxes are combined into Session 5. Sessions 2 through 6 therefore carry F, A, M, I and L together, and Y.

**Recommended default: leave it.** Splitting them would require moving Session 1 into front matter, and Session 1 is doing real work as the on-ramp. Just be aware that a family looking for the six letters will find five sessions.

### 6. There is still no product addressed to the next generation
Title 6's subtitle reads *Preparing Future Stewards to Carry Your Values, Wealth, and Legacy Forward.* The word is your. That title is written to the first generation, about the next generation.

Which means across twelve titles and seventy-two sessions, nothing in this system speaks to a thirty-two-year-old directly.

The recurring finding across every session of the foundation title is that the failure originates with the people who receive. That audience is now the only one in the ecosystem without a book. Flagging it again because this contents page makes the omission concrete rather than theoretical.
## WHAT THIS CONTENTS PAGE DECIDES BY IMPLICATION
Two of the four open decisions from the conversion architecture are answered here, and one of them may be answered without anyone intending to.

**Facilitator, answered yes.** Family Legacy by Design Session 2 is *The Legacy Process*, which is the three-stage Multi-Generational Legacy Coaching Process. Presenting a coaching process to a family only makes sense if a coach is running it. This contents page assumes a coach-led engagement, and the self-guided family is not currently accounted for.

**What replaces advisor Session 2, answered.** *The Legacy Process* is a better answer than the one I proposed. It tells a family what journey they are on before asking them to start it.

**Which family, still open and now more consequential.** Family Alignment's content is unambiguously first generation. A twenty-eight-year-old is not reviewing the family's insurance coverage. Family Clarity Session 2, Personal Clarity, is unambiguously individual. Next Generation is written to the parents. Three titles, three implied readers, and no stated decision.

**Video, still open and now multiplied by six.** Thirty-six family sessions and no family-facing footage.
## COPY AND EDITORIAL FIXES
**Three em-dashes**, which the series rules prohibit:
- Title 1, Session 1: *Understanding What You Will Leave Behind and Why It Matters*
- Title 2, Session 1: *Why Families Drift Without It and How to Find Direction Again*
- Title 4, Session 2: *Understanding Where You Are and Where You Want to Be*

**Title 1, Session 5 subtitle** reads *Creating Meaningful Conversations that brings a family together.* Agreement error, and the title case drops mid-phrase. Suggested: *Creating the Conversations That Bring a Family Together.*

**Title 1, Session 6** uses an ampersand in *Family Harmony & Wealth Transfer* where the rest of the series uses and. Also carries stray formatting around the ampersand.

**Title 6** drops the Family prefix that titles 2 through 5 all carry. Either **Family Next Generation**, or drop the prefix from the others and let each title stand on its noun.

**Title 4, Session 2** has a trailing space after *Goals and Gaps Conversation*.

## THE ONE QUESTION I WOULD PUT TO TOM
The advisor series and the family series now cover the same six themes in seventy-two sessions total, and a family working with a certified coach will encounter the same frameworks from two directions in the same year.

That is either the strength of the system or its redundancy, depending on one decision: whether the family titles are meant to be worked **with** the advisor titles, in parallel, or **instead of** them, with the advisor keeping his own guide private.

If parallel, the family titles should reference the advisor's process openly and the two should be sold together. If instead, the family titles have to stand alone completely and the overlap has to be invisible.

Nothing else in the build can be settled cleanly until that is.
