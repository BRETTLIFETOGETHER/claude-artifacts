# HOW TO USE THIS
Five readings a week, Monday through Friday, for six weeks. One week for each session of the curriculum.

Weekends are open on purpose. If you miss a day, take it Saturday and you are still on pace. Nobody needs to fall behind over a busy Tuesday.

Each reading takes a few minutes. There is a short passage, four questions, a written exercise, one question to take to a client, and a private section at the end that nobody else sees.

Write the answers rather than think them. That is the whole instruction and it is the difference between a book you read and a book that changes how you work. A thought you have while reading is gone by the afternoon. A sentence you wrote is still there in six weeks when you need it.

The section called One Question to Carry is the one that produces most of the results. It asks you to take a single question into an actual client conversation that week. Thirty days produces thirty of those, and by the end you will have asked things you have never asked in a decade of meetings.

The Bucket is private. It turns the day on you rather than on the people you serve, and nothing in it is meant to be shared with anyone.

The prayer at the end of each day is a direction rather than a script. Pray it in your own words.
