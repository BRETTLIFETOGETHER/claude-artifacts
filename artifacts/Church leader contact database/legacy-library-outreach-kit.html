The Outreach Kit

Three emails, merge-ready, each built for a different door: the senior pastor's own inbox, the office inbox that a gatekeeper reads, and the webinar invitation that warms the whole list at once. Every claim in these emails is true for every record they will touch — the provenance rule below is what makes that possible on a twenty-year-old list.

How to run this

The provenance rule. Email One has two openings. Opening A claims the church ran a campaign with us — use it only for records where that is verified in the master file (a purchase record, a campaign registration). Opening B claims only what is true of every record: the name has been in the master file since the 40 Days era. When in doubt, B. A pastor who catches one false familiarity claim deletes everything after it.

The address rule. A personal address (jsmith@, pastorbob@) gets Email One. An office inbox (info@, pastorsoffice@, mail@, webmaster@) gets Email Two — it is written to be read by the person who screens, and it gives them a reason to walk it down the hall instead of deleting it.

The sequence. Week one: Email One to personal addresses, Email Two to office inboxes. Week three: Email Three (the webinar) to everyone who has not replied, and to the wider list. Brett's own dictated personal note, when it is ready, runs ahead of all of this to the handful of relationships that deserve a human opener — the merge never touches those names.

The landing page. Where an email references a one-page overview, that is the Legacy Library offer page — host it or export it to PDF before the first send, and put the live link or attachment in place of [OFFER LINK].
