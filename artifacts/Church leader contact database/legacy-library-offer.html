The Evidence

This market already exists. We indexed it.

In August 2026 we ran field research across more than one hundred American churches, from 2,400 in weekend attendance to nearly 12,000. What we found is the case for this work:

A pastor's archive is already a retail product.

John MacArthur's complete sermon archive — 3,127 messages across five decades — is sold today as a $399 digital library, alongside the free archive his ministry maintains. The library outlived the pulpit and now runs as a publishing operation.

The largest churches already run this model.

Church of the Resurrection packages entire sermon series — video, curriculum, children's materials — and shares them with other churches. North Coast Church has tied a produced study guide to every weekend message since 1985 and reports eighty percent of adults in groups.

Almost nobody else does.

Of the churches we indexed, nearly all publish the audio, a minority publish an outline, and only a sliver put a produced guide in the congregation's hands. The sermon is preached once and shelved. That gap is exactly what this engagement closes.

The Purpose Driven precedent.

Rick Warren did not write The Purpose Driven Life alone. Behind it stood a team, a process, and a system that translated twenty years of pastoral wisdom into a form fifty million people could hold. Most senior pastors have material that rich. What they lack is not the content. It is the system.
