## How This Document Is Built

Seven layers, merged into one master directory:

1. **Part One — The 50 Converted Categories.** Every category from the original 50 Master Curriculum Categories, re-languaged for business and marketplace use, expanded to its full 10-campaign depth.
2. **Part Two — 25 New Corporate-Native Categories.** The top themes, courses, and masterclasses actually requested inside HR, L&D, and culture departments today.
3. **Part Three — 10 More You're Missing.** A first gap-check: compliance, safety, recruiting, and the categories most training platforms forget.
4. **Part Four — 25 More You Might Be Missing.** A second gap-check: industry-general and ownership-level categories — family business governance, startup scaling, small business owner peer community, professional services culture, and more.
5. **Part Five — 10 Decision-Closing Categories.** The categories a business leader expects to find before they commit — handbook communication, meeting culture, delegation, continuity planning, and more.
6. **Part Six — 20 Industry Verticals.** Recovered from a separate prior project (Marketplace Intelligence Industry Edition) — physicians, architects, bankers, private equity, family offices, engineers, and 14 more, each addressing that profession's specific calling and pressure.
7. **Part Seven — 25 Double-Depth Categories.** Built at 25 campaigns each instead of 10 — the broad-appeal categories most likely to get an owner to say yes: AI literacy, pricing strategy, fundraising, multigenerational management, exit readiness, and 20 more.

Two existing flagship frameworks are folded in throughout rather than duplicated:
- **Purpose Based Business** (Category 05) — the five biblical purposes (Worship, Fellowship, Discipleship, Ministry, Mission) relanguaged as a five-week company framework. The eight founding themes for this category were confirmed from prior work; a larger 500–1,500 series expansion was referenced this session but could not be located and hasn't yet been built.
- **The Flourishing Workplace** (Category 20) — the actual six-week Flourishing framework (with God, together, from within, through contribution, for others, across generations) and its 10 built series, recovered from prior work and restored here.

**Totals:** 165 categories · 2,025 individual courses/campaigns · 2 unifying weekly frameworks · 1 platform.
